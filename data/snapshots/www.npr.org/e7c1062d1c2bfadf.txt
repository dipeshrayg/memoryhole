Today, Peggy King, a singer and actress who got her start singing in big bands in the 1950s and had a string of hits, a contract with MGM and was a popular fixture on many TV variety shows. She died late last month at the age of 96. Here she is in 1951, with Ralph Flanagan and his orchestra.

BIANCULLI: Peggy King signed a long-term contract with Columbia Records and recorded two bestselling albums, "Wish Upon A Star" and "Girl Meets Boy." In 1952, MGM signed her to a movie contract. She was often told at MGM she looked and sounded like Judy Garland, something King disputed. She made a singing cameo in the film "The Bad And The Beautiful," and she became a regular on the TV variety shows, starring Mel Torme and George Gobel. She made guest appearances on other TV programs like "Dragnet." In 1955, on that show, she played a nightclub singer who may have encountered a suspect.
