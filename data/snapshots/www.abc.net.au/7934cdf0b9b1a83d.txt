Qantas ground crews vote in favour of industrial action over pay and safety conditions
In short:
Some regional Qantas ground crews have voted in favour of taking industrial action to demand better pay.
The Transport Workers Union says 97 per cent of 350 workers who voted backed industrial action, including work stoppages ranging from one to 24 hours.
What's next?
The union is required to give three days' notice before a strike.
