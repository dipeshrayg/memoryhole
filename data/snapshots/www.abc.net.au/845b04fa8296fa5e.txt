Fri, 11 Sept, 7:50 pm, Final score, Rabbitohs 10, Knights 20
James Tedesco wins Clive Churchill Medal in Roosters' win over Knights in NRL grand final
In short:
James Tedesco was awarded the Clive Churchill Medal as the player of the match in the grand final.
Tedesco was immense in both attack and defence during the 19-18 win over Newcastle.
It was Tedesco's third premiership but his first Clive Churchill Medal.
