Bogie triple murder trial views photos of rifle cartridges, alleged crime scene
In short:
Photos of rifle cartridge packets, a bloodstained Land Cruiser driver's seat and shell casings around the bodies of two people have been presented to a triple murder trial in north Queensland.
Darryl Valroy Young, 63, is charged with three counts of murder and one count of attempted murder and has pleaded not guilty to all charges.
The trial has also heard that Mr Young's stepmother, whom he spent hours with after the alleged shooting, has declined to give a statement to police.
