French burglar used artificial intelligence to locate Canberra target in luxury goods heist
In short:
One of the four French nationals who stole a haul of luxury goods from a Canberra home last year used artificial intelligence to locate their target.
The four men have pleaded guilty to charges of aggravated burglary and joint commission theft, but their lawyers have disputed the total value of the stolen items.
What's next?
The ACT Supreme Court will set a date for the mens' sentencing next week.
