Russia, Ukraine pause fighting in Oleshky after truce announced by Melania Trump
In short:
A regional truce between Russia and Ukraine is in effect in order to establish a humanitarian corridor for the city of Oleshky.
Melania Trump announced the truce last week, saying civilian officials would provide aid to residents and help them leave if they wished.
What's next?
US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine are set to resume next month.
