Kangaroos avoid Rugby League World Cup upset, beating Fiji 36-22 at Lang Park
The Kangaroos have locked in their semifinal spot at the Rugby League World Cup, beating Fiji 36-22 after receiving an almighty scare in Brisbane.
Tom Trbojevic's second-half hat-trick helped Australia over the line after the Bati twice took the lead either side of half-time at Lang Park.
Fijian five-eighth Kurt Donoghoe was rightly named player of the match after laying on three tries and dictating terms for much of the game.
"I think we need more from everyone," Kangaroos coach Kevin Walters said after the game.
"We know we can play better but let's give Fiji a lot of credit. At times it seemed like we were getting on top and they pulled themselves back into the game."
Despite coming into the game as 50-1 outsiders, making three early errors and conceding the first try of the night, Fiji hit back through Semi Valemei and Taane Milne to lead 10-6 after half an hour.
For the second straight game, Kevin Walters's side looked vulnerable in defence and struggled with their last-tackle options, but still led at half-time after some James Tedesco brilliance led to a try for Sam Walker on debut.
But again the Fijians hit back, with Valemei's second retaking the lead for the underdogs just after the break and Solomone Saukuru appearing to extend soon after, only for the bunker to rule a line-ball obstruction.
The Kangaroos immediately went up the other end and Tom Trbojevic dove in for his first of three straight tries to snuff out the Fijian challenge.
Valemei completed his hat-trick late on before Jacob Preston added Australia's sixth shortly before full-time to lock in their semifinal berth, playing a double-header with the similarly undefeated Jillaroos at the Sydney Football Stadium next Sunday.
Fiji will have a chance to push for a surprise final-four spot if it can pull off an upset win over New Zealand next week, which suddenly does not seem so far-fetched.
