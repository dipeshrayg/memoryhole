Mexico's military captures drug kingpin 'El Chapo Isidro' on FBI's most wanted list
In short:
Mexico's military says it has captured the leader of a drug trafficking group among the 10 most wanted fugitives sought by US law enforcement.
Fausto Isidro Meza - whose alias is "Chapo Isidro" - and two other leaders of his gang were captured during a special forces raid in Sinaloa state.
The kingpin faces criminal charges in both Mexico and the United States.
