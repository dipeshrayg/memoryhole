Woman found dead in Carlton apartment identified as 21-year-old Jayda Allen Bonner
In short:
A woman found dead in a Melbourne apartment block overnight has been identified by family as 21-year-old Jayda Allen Bonner.
A homicide investigation is underway; no charges have been laid over the incident, and one person arrested has since been released.
Ms Allen Bonner's aunt told the ABC that the family had been concerned for the young woman's safety and had tried to get help from authorities.
