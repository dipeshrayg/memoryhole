Ex-Tasmanian senior firefighter avoids prison for assaulting massage client
In short:
Valentine Ansett was sentenced for indecent assault and assault with indecent intent in Hobart on Wednesday.
The court heard one of the offences occurred during a so-called "chakra balancing" aspect of a professional massage he was providing to the woman.
What's next?
He has avoided prison time, and his lawyer said he has already appealed the conviction.
