High Court finds secret recording can be used as evidence in DV case
In short:
DV survivors may now be able to use secret recordings of admissions of abuse to seek safety after a major High Court decision.
While most recordings of private conversations are illegal, the High Court found the recording was allowed because it was necessary to protect the victim's "bodily autonomy and integrity".
What's next?
It remains to be seen how far courts will allow secret recordings to be used to as evidence against less obvious forms of abuse.
