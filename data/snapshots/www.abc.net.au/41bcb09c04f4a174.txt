Sun 2 Aug 2026 at 6:18pm
Track cycling: Welcome back to the velodrome
Pretty loaded schedule for the track cyclists on this final day, with plenty of medals to dish out as well.
We're kicking off with the women's Keirin first round heats. Australia's Alessia McCaig has qualified fastest out of her heat, but Liliya Tatarinoff finished third in hers' so is currently stuck as a reserve.
Kristina Clonan finished second in hers to qualify for the quarterfinals, but faces a little bit of a wait to find out if she was to blame for what was a very nasty crash with three laps remaining. Scotland's Lauren Bell took the worst of it, and looks to have done some serious damage to her collarbone. Rhian Edmunds of Wales also limps off in some serious pain.
But Clonan is cleared of any wrongdoing and will compete in the Kierin quarterfinals.

Netball: Diamonds ahead by end of first quarter
The Aussies have clearly learned from last night's devastating loss at The Hydro, they're looking a lot more calm and collected on the court tonight.
But that's not to say our (incredibly fit and quick) ladies have slowed down, we're still reading England's plays and zooming across the court to intercept them - we've nabbed five intercepts so far (the Roses haven't got any yet)!
Australia is pulling ahead as the first quarter comes to a close, we're looking at a score of 19-11.
If Australia wins tonight's game, they'll claim bronze for the first time in Commonwealth Games history - adding to their previous gold and silver medals to round out the podium for the Diamonds.
