Fri, 11 Sept, 7:50 pm, Final score, Rabbitohs 10, Knights 20
Matty Johns praises Newcastle Knights fans in emotional tribute after NRL preliminary final
In short:
Former Knights star Matty Johns has praised the Knights and their fans after Newcastle reached the NRL grand final for the first time in 25 years.
Johns became emotional as he hailed the supporters for sticking by the team.
What's next?
The Knights will face the Roosters in the NRL grand final on Sunday, October 4 from 7:30pm AEDT.
