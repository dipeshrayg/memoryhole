Fri, 18 Sept, 7:50 pm, Final score, Tigers 0, Raiders 6
Broncos defeat Knights 40-16 to set up NRLW preliminary final against Titans
In short:
The Broncos produced a dominant display to defeat the Knights 40-16 in their elimination final.
The defending premiers scored seven tries to three, with Romy Teitzel crossing for a double.
What's next?
The Broncos face the Titans in a preliminary final next weekend.
