Mon, 14 Sept, 11:00 pm, Final score, ZIM 235, AUS 8/294
Brisbane Heat thump Hobart Hurricanes by 10 wickets in WBBL
In short:
Defending WBBL champions, the Hobart Hurricanes have slumped to 0-6 after a 10-wicket loss to the Brisbane Heat.
The Hurricanes were dismissed for just 88 inside 18 overs, which the Heat chased down in just 5.4 overs.
Heat opener Grace Harris top-scored with 52 off just 22 balls.
