Man fined $31,000 over attempt to mail 65 lizards from WA to NSW
In short:
A New South Wales man has been fined $31,000 for attempting to smuggle lizards out of Western Australia.
Nicholas Thomas Havet, 28, travelled to Western Australia in 2023, where he captured the wild reptiles and mailed them to New South Wales.
What's next?
Havet was found to have exposed the reptiles to "cruel and inhumane treatment", with four animals killed and a further 51 having to be euthanised.
