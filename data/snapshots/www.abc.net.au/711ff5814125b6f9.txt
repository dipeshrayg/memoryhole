Australian found alive after Nepal-Tibet floods
In short:
One of 38 Australians reported missing in the Nepal-Tibet flood disaster has been found alive in Tibet.
The person is receiving support from Australian consular officers and has spoken with family members but China says the person was not in the "epicentre of the disaster".
What's next?
More than 4,400 people remain missing across the affected areas, with search and rescue efforts continuing along the Nepal-China border.
