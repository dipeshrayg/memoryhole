Earthquake rocks central Japan, shattering shop windows and injuring at least 22
In short:
Central Japan was shaken by a strong earthquake on Sunday that shattered store windows and caused people to flee buildings, leaving at least 22 people injured, according to local media.
A wide area near Aichi Prefecture was affected, including the tourist city of Kyoto, and the nearby prefecture of Yamanashi, where Mount Fuji is located.
What's next?
Japan's Meteorological Agency warned people to brace for possible strong quakes in the coming week, especially the next two to three days.
