Woman cleared of Cassius Turvey's murder avoids jail over suspended sentence breach
In short:
Aleesha Gilmore, found not guilty of murdering Perth school boy Cassius Turvey, has avoided an immediate prison term for breaching the terms of her suspended sentence.
Gilmore was convicted of offences she committed in the days leading up to the assault on Cassius Turvey.
She breached the terms of that sentence by driving without a licence, but the Supreme Court said she did not have to immediately go to jail to give her a chance at rehabilitation.
