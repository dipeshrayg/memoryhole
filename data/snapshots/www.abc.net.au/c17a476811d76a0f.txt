Japan's Kyushu island hit by two strong earthquakes within an hour
In short:
Japan's Kyushu island has been struck by two strong earthquakes within an hour.
An initial magnitude-7.1 quake hit the Kumamoto prefecture at 4:35pm local time, before another magnitude-6.1 tremor struck 45 minutes later.
What's next?
The initial tremor sparked tsunami alerts for at least two prefectures, with waves of up to one metre expected.
