Eels NRL star Josh Addo-Carr allegedly returns positive test for cocaine
In short:
Parramatta Eels player Josh Addo-Carr is being investigated by police after allegedly returning a positive test for cocaine.
An investigation is ongoing, as police await the results of a forensic analysis.
The Eels say they have notified the NRL integrity unit.
