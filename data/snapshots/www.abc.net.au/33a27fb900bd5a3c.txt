Police release image of e-bike rider they say can help with 'sexual touching' probe at Narrabeen Lagoon
In short:
Police have increased surveillance around Narrabeen Lagoon after multiple reports of alleged sexual offences against women.
The offences took place between October 1 and October 12, mostly between 4pm and 6pm.
What's next?
An image has been released of an e-bike rider police say can help with their investigation, and are urging the man to come forward.
