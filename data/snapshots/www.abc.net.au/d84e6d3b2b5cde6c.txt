2026 AFL draft: Brisbane Lions interrupt graduation ceremony with consecutive picks
Brisbane has disrupted a high school graduation ceremony by drafting classmates Kodah Edwards and Archie Van Dyk with consecutive picks in the second round of the AFL draft.
The Lions were left with three consecutive second-round selections after failing to match a bid from Sydney for their academy prospect Caylen Murray in the first round, and chose Edwards with the 27th pick and Van Dyk with the 28th.
Incredibly, Edwards and Van Dyk weren't the only players to be selected from the same high school ceremony, with Port Adelaide grabbing Angus White out of the same ceremony with the 47th pick later in the night.
However, the celebration of the entire draft came at the 33rd pick when the Western Bulldogs matched Adelaide's bid for academy prospect Khaled El Souki.
El Souki's draft party took place in a warehouse with approximately 100 people in attendance with fireworks exploding and Lebanese drummers banging away in the background upon his selection.
There was also some father-son romance at the back end of the draft as Cooper Hodge (the son of Luke Hodge) landed at Hawthorn, while Aidan McCartney (the son of Jason McCartney) landed at North Melbourne and Harper Banfield (the son of Drew Banfield) was selected by West Coast with the final pick.
Former No.1 pick Jamarra Ugle-Hagan was re-drafted by Gold Coast alongside fellow veteran Elliott Himmelberg with one of the final picks of the night.
