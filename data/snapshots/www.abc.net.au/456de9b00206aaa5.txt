Swedish opposition leader Magdalena Andersson says she cannot form goverment
In short:
Swedish Social Democrat leader Magdalena Andersson says she cannot form a government after her centre-left bloc won a narrow majority in last month's general election.
One of the bloc's three parties announced over the weekend it would back a parliament speaker candidate from the rival Moderate Party, which Ms Andersson says undercut her attempt to form government.
What's next?
The speaker of parliament is expected to task another leader, likely the outgoing Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson, with forming a new government.
