Myanmar dismisses UN court ruling finding country responsible of Rohingya genocide as 'unfair'
In short:
Myanmar has called the International Court of Justice ruling on genocide "unfair".
Yesterday the United Nations' highest court found Myanmar's security forces conducted state-sponsored genocide against the country's Rohingya Muslim minority.
What's next?
Thousands of Rohingya refugees living in camps in Bangladesh have welcomed the verdict, but it remains unclear when they will be able to return home.
