Pauline Hanson reveals Gina Rinehart paid for Italy flights and Dolce & Gabbana show
In short:
One Nation leader Pauline Hanson has updated her parliamentary register of interests, declaring Gina Rinehart paid for her London to Sicily flights.
The senator's trip came under scrutiny after she was spotted poolside with Mrs Rinehart in Italy and attended a Dolce and Gabbana fashion show, raising questions about who footed the bill.
What's next?
Ms Hanson has not declared the Australia to London flights as part of the gift registry update.
