Iran offers deal to US to reopen Strait of Hormuz that Donald Trump has reportedly rejected
In short:
Iran Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi says his nation has offered a deal to the US that would reopen the Strait of Hormuz within a week.
But US President Donald Trump has already rejected the idea, according to the Wall Street Journal, renaming the waterway "Trump Strait" on his Truth Social feed.
What's next?
Iran wants the memorandum of understanding signed in June to form the basis of any future peace agreements with the US and Israel.
