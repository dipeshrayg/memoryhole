Australia names team for second Test against Bangladesh
In short:
Australia Test captain Pat Cummins has confirmed Matt Renshaw will open the batting in the second Test against Bangladesh.
Renshaw's inclusion at the expense of Jake Weatherald is the only change to the Australian side who were stunningly defeated in the first Test.
What's next?
ABC Sport will have a live blog of every ball between Australia and Bangladesh, with the second Test beginning on Saturday.
