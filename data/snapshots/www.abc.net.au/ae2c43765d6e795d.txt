South Australian Police have attended the Tollgate Motel in Glen Osmond on Saturday morning to investigate a reported infant death.
ABC News understands police and ambulances were called to the Tollgate Motel, adjacent to the South Eastern Freeway, five kilometres south-east of the Adelaide CBD, from 9am.
Some witnesses estimated around 20 officers were present.
"There were at least four or five police cars here," a nearby resident told the ABC.
"It's a bit concerning that we've got police cars down the end of our quiet cul-de-sac."
Police left the scene by mid-afternoon, after establishing a cordon.
The cause of the child's death is currently undetermined.
Police have confirmed that an investigation is ongoing with the assistance of crime scene investigators and Eastern District Detectives, and a report will be prepared for the coroner.
