A woman has been refused bail after being charged with domestic violence-related murder following the discovery of a man's body in a creek at a property on the NSW north coast.
Police were called just after midday on Thursday to a house on Teven Road, in Teven, west of Ballina, after reports of a concern for welfare.
Officers searched the property and found the body of an 81-year-old man in a creek.
Police have since identified the man as Sam Singh and provided an image supplied by his family, who have requested privacy.
A crime scene was established, and an on-site investigation into the circumstances of the man's death is underway.
NSW Police alleged the man and woman were known to one another.
Elizabeth Simone Fell, 51, was arrested at the scene and taken to the Lismore police station.
She was later transported to Lismore Base Hospital for assessment and then released into police custody on Friday morning, where she was taken back to the station and charged with murder.
Bail refused
Wearing a hospital gown, Ms Fell faced a bail court via audio visual link on Saturday morning.
Her defence lawyer told the court she had number of health conditions, including bipolar, ADHD and autism.
Ms Fell asked the court when she would be able to see a doctor, saying: "I need to be in hospital."
The judge said a request had been made and Corrective Services would facilitate a medical consultation for her.
Bail was not applied for and it was formally refused.
The case was adjourned to Lismore Local Court in late October.
