FIFA unveils presidential candidates as Diallo, Tashima challenge Infantino
In short:
FIFA has confirmed that Gianni Infantino, Philippe Diallo and Kozo Tashima are running for FIFA president.
Mr Infantino has faced repeated calls for his resignation since his attempt to sell stakes in the World Cup failed.
What's next?
The election will be the first time Mr Infantino has faced a challenger after being re-elected without opposition in 2023.
