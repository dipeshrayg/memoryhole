Lock downs at WA public schools surge last year as more violent threats made against students, staff
In short:
Violent threats have triggered 1,107 incidents of lock down at Western Australian schools last year, according to the Department of Education.
The lockdowns applied to entire schools, blocks or a class room, the figures show.
The education minister said the increase, which the opposition called "staggering", was due to stricter reporting protocols introduced in 2020.
