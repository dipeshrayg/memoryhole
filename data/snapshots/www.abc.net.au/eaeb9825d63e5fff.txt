Detectives are investigating the circumstances around the death of a woman and a child in Melbourne's east on Thursday evening.
Victoria Police said they were called to Prospect Street in Box Hill just before 6:20pm, with reports a woman had been found unresponsive outside an apartment block.
An unresponsive child was discovered nearby a short time later.
"The woman and primary school-aged child could not be revived and sadly died at the scene," a police spokesperson said.
Police said they were not looking for anyone else in relation to the incident.
Residents told the ABC they were prevented from seeing much of what happened last night because of a heavy police presence.
Many received texts from police asking them to remain indoors.
One man who works opposite the building who identified himself as Michael told the ABC his staff were too upset today to come to work.
"I still call them to come because we need to support each other," he said.
"I call them, if you need help or anything to see doctor or anything, tell me."
Michael said the tragedy had deeply affected the building's residents.
"I can feel whole building mourning and crying," he said.
People at the scene have told the ABC the woman did not live in the building.
Anyone who witnessed the incident or has information has been urged to phone Crime Stoppers on 1800 333 000.
