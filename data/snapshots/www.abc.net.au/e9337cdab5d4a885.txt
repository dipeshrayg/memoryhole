Mahli Beardman's six-wicket haul on debut a cause for celebration across regional WA
In short:
Fast bowler Mahli Beardman's 6/24 against England was the best ODI debut performance of all time by an Australian bowler.
Beardman, 21, played his initial and representative junior cricket in Dampier in WA's Pilbara.
Cricket figures hope his performance encourages the next generation of country cricketers.
