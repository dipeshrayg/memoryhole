Adelaide's tight-knit lacrosse community turns out to support girl with leukaemia
In short:
Five-year-old Margot Whinnen was diagnosed with acute myeloid leukaemia, a form of blood cancer, after reporting stomach pains last Friday.
Her family, who play for the local Sturt Lacrosse Club, have received support from the sport's local and global community.
What's next
Margot is undergoing chemotherapy in the Women's and Children's Hospital.
