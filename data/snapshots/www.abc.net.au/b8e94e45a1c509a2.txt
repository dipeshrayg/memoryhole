After weathering the first 20 minutes, things turned ugly in a hurry for France against England.

How the women's progression works

Hi Mike,
No, the women's side of the Cup is more traditional.
While the men have one main pool and a combined Pool C and B, their are only two groups of four women's teams with the top two in each group progressing to the semifinals.
Interestingly, heading into tonight's game England is actually leading the Jillaroos in Pool A courtesy of a 70-6 win over Wales, while Australia beat Samoa by a paltry 40-0.
