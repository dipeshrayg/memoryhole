Man charged with alleged domestic violence murder of Port Macquarie woman Megan Tangye
In short:
NSW Police have charged a 47-year-old man with the murder of his former partner, who they allege he killed in December 2025.
Police say they arrested the man, who they say was in a relationship with Megan Tangye at the time of her death, after they saw the final post-mortem examination results.
What's next?
The man's matters are due for mention in court tomorrow.
