Death toll sits at 392
Just in the last hour, Nepal Police say the death toll on their side of the border stands at 389.
Adding that to the three deaths that have been reported in Tibet, and the current total death toll from the flood now sits at 392.
A further 466 people are reported as injured, Nepal Police say.
There has been no update on the 35 Australians currently missing after the floods which unfolded on Wednesday.
Key Event
Focus turns to second possible flood
The big concern going into today is the possibility of a second flood along the same river.
Two barrier lakes have been formed as a result of Wednesday's disaster, one in Nepal and one in China.
The worry, according to officials in both countries, is that either or both are expected to continue filling with water and then overflow, leading to a second debris flow down the same river, and through the same disaster-hit communities.
Nepal's disaster authority issued a notice on Thursday warning that a lake formed by debris blocking a river at the flood site is rising and at risk of bursting.
Meanwhile, China is warning that 3 million cubic metres of water are expected to flow into an already dammed lake on its side of the border within the next three days.
