Former Darwin councillor Paul Arnold sentenced to home detention over 'serious domestic violence' offences
In short:
Award-winning photographer and former Darwin councillor Paul Arnold has been sentenced to a two-year intensive community corrections order over a string of domestic violence offences.
He was found guilty on more than a dozen charges related to multiple incidents involving his former girlfriend.
What's next?
Arnold will spend nine months in home detention and has been ordered to complete a men's behavioural change program.
