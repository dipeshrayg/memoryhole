Yemen's Houthi rebels seize more key islands in southern Red Sea to tighten grip on shipping route
In short:
Officials say Yemen's Iranian-backed Houthi rebels have captured more key islands, strengthening control of a major shipping route.
The capture of the islands of Greater and Lesser Hanish comes after the group seized a port city, a third key island and attacked a Saudi Air base.
What's next?
The advances tighten the group's hold on maritime oil shipments, with a meeting set to negotiate the Strait of Hormuz between the Gulf states and Iran called off by Saudi Arabia.
