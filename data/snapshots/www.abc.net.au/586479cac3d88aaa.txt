Christopher Mark Jordan to be tried in April 2027 for murder of Shyanne-Lee Tatnell
In short:
The trial of Christopher Mark Jordan, 39, who is accused of murdering 14-year-old Shyanne-Lee Tatnell, has been set for April 2027.
The Burnie teenager's remains were found about three months after she went missing.
The trial is expected to run for five weeks.
