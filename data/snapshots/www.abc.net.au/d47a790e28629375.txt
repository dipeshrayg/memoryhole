In short:
Robert Irwin has been named the 2027 Australian of the Year for Queensland.
Gout Gout, Carmel Crouch and Noel Brown have also been recognised as Queenslanders of the year.
Wildlife conservationist Robert Irwin has been declared the Australian of the Year for Queensland at tonight's awards.
The 22-year-old was named the 2027 Australian of the Year for Queensland for his environmental advocacy and work at Australia Zoo.
Robert Irwin is the son of Australian conservationists Steve and Terri Irwin, and the grandson of Australia Zoo founders Bob and Lyn Irwin.
The Young Australian of the Year for Queensland was Ipswich sprinting champion Gout Gout.
Gout Gout broke the world under-20 record for the 200-metre dash in April, with a time of 19.67 seconds.
He ran the 200m in 20.04 seconds at the 2024 Australian All School Athletics Championship, becoming the fastest 16-year-old in history.
The now 18-year-old plans to compete in the 2028 Olympics, followed by the 2032 Brisbane Olympic Games.
The Senior Australian of the Year for Queenslander award was taken out by disability advocate Carmel Crouch.
The 80-year-old was a founding board member of STEPS Group Australia, which helps people with disabilities gain skills and land jobs.
Under her leadership, the group pioneered STEPS Pathways College, which teaches students with disability and autism.
Ms Crouch has since broadened her reach to migrants, refugees and First Nations communities through a series of social enterprises.
The Local Hero for Queensland award was taken out by Noel Brown, who is the specialist First Nations greeter at Cairns Hospital.
The 65-year-old provides warm smiles, conversation and cultural connection to Aboriginal patients going through the Cairns medical system.
Inspiring stories
National Australia Day Council CEO Carrie Leeson congratulated the Queensland award recipients.
"The achievements of our Queensland award recipients are nothing short of inspirational,"
Ms Leeson said.
"Robert is continuing his family's legacy and sharing their passion for conservation with a new generation, while Gout is a teenage sprint sensation breaking world records.
"Carmel is helping people gain independence and dignity, while Noel provides understanding and support when it's needed most."
