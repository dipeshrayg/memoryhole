Bulldogs coach Cameron Ciraldo urges NRL to improve rule-change communication
In short:
Canterbury Bulldogs coach Cameron Ciraldo wants the NRL to communicate rule changes for next season well in advance.
Ciraldo said he only learned his team will be subjected to a trial of new rule changes this weekend via an email.
What's next?
Ciraldo's Bulldogs will wrap up their 2026 season with a clash against the Broncos on Thursday night at Stadium Australia.
