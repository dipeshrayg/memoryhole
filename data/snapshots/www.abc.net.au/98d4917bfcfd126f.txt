More than 400 jobs affected as Mater moves to external pathology providers
In short:
More than 400 positions are expected to be affected at Mater Pathology in Brisbane and Springfield as the hospitals transition to an external pathology provider.
A spokesperson for the healthcare provider blamed rising costs for the decision.
What's next?
More than 100 positions will be lost in the next two months, with most of the remaining positions ending in 2027.
