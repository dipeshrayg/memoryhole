Gina Rinehart puts ABC on notice over Race around the World episode action
In short:
Gina Rinehart and her company, Hancock Prospecting, have demanded the removal of an episode of Race Around the World.
Contestant Kate McGuinness, who portrayed the fictional scenario involving a violent attack on Mrs Rinehart, has apologised to anyone she offended.
What's next?
An ABC spokesperson said the ABC Ombudsman was independently reviewing audience complaints in relation to the episode and it would respond to legal correspondence in due time.
