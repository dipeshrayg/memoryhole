Thieves steal three paintings from Renoir Museum worth 9 million euros
In short:
Thieves have stolen three paintings from the Renoir Museum in France, worth 9 million euros ($14.5 million).
Two thieves broke into the museum in the early hours of the morning, stealing four artworks, dropping one as they fled.
What's next?
The thieves have not been caught and are being "actively sought", according to local mayor.
