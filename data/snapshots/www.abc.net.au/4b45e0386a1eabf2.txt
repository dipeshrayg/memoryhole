New Brisbane Heat captain Georgia Redmayne looks for WBBL improvement after 2025 winless season
In short:
New Brisbane Heat skipper Georgia Redmayne says her side is still a winning team despite coming off a winless WBBL season last year.
Redmayne captained the Queensland Fire to a win in the 2025/2026 WNCL before taking over from Heat all-rounder Jess Jonassen.
What's next?
The Heat start the WBBL season at home to Sydney Sixers on Friday night.
