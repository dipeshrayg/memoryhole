Fri, 11 Sept, 7:50 pm, Final score, Rabbitohs 10, Knights 20
Jillaroos qualify for Rugby League World Cup final after scare against Fiji
In short:
The Jillaroos qualified for a fifth consecutive World Cup final, overcoming an early scare to defeat Fiji 30-14.
Fiji raced out to an 8-0 first-half lead to threaten one of the sport's all-time upsets.
What's next?
Australia will face New Zealand in next Sunday's decider at Lang Park.
