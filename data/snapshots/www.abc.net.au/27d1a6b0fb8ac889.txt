Harry Styles, Sam Fender, Billie Eilish and Cher to headline Glastonbury in 2027
In short:
The first details have been released about the artists performing at the UK's Glastonbury Festival in 2027.
Harry Styles, Sam Fender and Billie Eilish are the headline acts, with Cher taking the Sunday afternoon legend's slot.
What's next?
More details about the line-up are expected in the New Year.
