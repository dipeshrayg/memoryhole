Collingwood confirms strategic review of AFLW program amid upheaval at club
In short:
Collingwood has announced a review of its struggling AFLW program, saying it remains committed to ensuring the club is ready for success.
The Magpies have won just one game this season, and coach Sam Wright and captain Ruby Schleicher will leave after the final match of the year.
What's next?
Collingwood will finish its season against Essendon on Sunday.
