Police charge 38yo Perth man with murder after Keysbrook shooting death
In short:
Police have charged a 38-year-old man with murder following a major security operation that was triggered by the killing of a 44-year-old man on a rural property south of Perth.
Firearms were seized from the scene, and police say the two men were known to each other.
What's next?
The accused is due to appear in court on Monday.
