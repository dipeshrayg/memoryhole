North Melbourne coach Alastair Clarkson says he's not worried about contract as Kangaroos set to miss finals
In short:
North Melbourne coach Alastair Clarkson says he won't be pushing for a contract extension in the off-season after another frustrating year for the Kangaroos.
Clarkson has one year left on his five-year contract, and he urged the club to mimic others like Brisbane and Adelaide by "sticking to the path".
What's next?
The Kangaroos, who are 13th on the ladder, face a challenging run home starting with Western Bulldogs on Thursday night.
