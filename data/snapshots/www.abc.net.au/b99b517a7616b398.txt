Sat, 12 Sept, 1:05 pm, Final score, Hawks 41, Bulldogs 30
Hawks' 11-point AFLW win over Bulldogs soured by leg injury for Emily Bates
In short:
Emily Bates was taken off the field on a stretcher after her right leg twisted underneath her in a tackle during the Hawks' win over the Bulldogs.
The Hawks also lost Laura Stone to concussion after an early head clash.
The Suns defeated the Magpies by 30 points in Carrara.
