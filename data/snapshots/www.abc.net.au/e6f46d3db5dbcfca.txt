In short:
Five people have been shot, one fatally, in Washington DC.
Authorities believe a dispute over an apartment may have sparked the shooting.
The suspected gunman died by suicide after barricading himself inside an apartment, police said.
One person has been killed and four others injured after a man opened fire inside a gym in a central Washington DC neighbourhood.
Police said the suspected gunman shot himself after he barricaded himself inside an apartment building above the gym. Police later confirmed he had died.
Negotiators had been communicating with the man for more than an hour before forcing their way inside the seventh-floor apartment.
The area is about 1.5 kilometres north of the White House.
Multiple local media outlets identified the shooter as Matt Lambert, a prominent local emergency-room doctor who had taken legal action against his home-owners' association over mould and water damage in his apartment.
Dr Lambert's online profile also describes him as a published author and a "dedicated philanthropist" who had volunteered in Kenya, Belize and Haiti.
Panic spread through the area after the man opened fire in the Vida gym on 15th St Northwest, in the Logan Circle neighbourhood, about 8am Thursday, local time.
Images on social media showed law enforcement officers on rooftops and balconies, guns drawn, as apartment complexes and businesses were locked down.
One of the victims was in a critical condition, police said. The three other surviving victims had non-life-threatening injuries.
Local area councillor Brooke Pinto said she believed the victim was targeted by the suspect over the apartment building dispute.
"The suspect, in fleeing the scene, also shot others," Councillor Pinto told local television.
Washington DC's acting police chief, Jeffery Carroll, earlier confirmed the suspect had been involved in a "conflict" with the home-owners' association of the building.
Gym-goers ran into a nearby bagel shop to shelter after the man started shooting, witnesses told reporters at the scene.
One woman said she was on a treadmill when she heard a gunshot and saw a victim on the ground.
"It was, like, chaos. It was horrific," she said.
"We exited and then had to tell the police what was happening, and that's when more shots were happening, and that's when we all ran into Call Your Mother [bagel shop] around the corner," she said.
Streets and buildings in the area were placed into lockdown and several schools were on alert as authorities surrounded the apartment complex and negotiated with the suspect.
Staff in some downtown government buildings were also ordered to shelter in place. Some were moved away from windows for safety.
Dozens of police vehicles were at the scene, which was cordoned off during the morning peak.
Members of the National Guard, who have been present in Washington DC as part of a crime crackdown ordered by US President Donald Trump, were also at the scene.
