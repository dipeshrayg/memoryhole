Phoebe Litchfield fires as Australia cruise to seven-wicket victory over Bangladesh
In short:
Australia defeated Bangladesh by seven wickets to take a 1-0 lead in the ODI series in Brisbane.
Opening batter Phoebe Litchfield struck 77 from 57 deliveries as the hosts chased down their target of 167 in just 25.4 overs.
What's next?
The series will continue on Sunday and then finish on Wednesday, with both the second and third ODIs also to be played at Allan Border Field.
