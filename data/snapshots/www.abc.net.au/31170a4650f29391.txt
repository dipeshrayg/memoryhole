Senna Agius to race in motorcycle racing's MotoGP next year with KTM Tech3
In short:
Australian rider Senna Agius has signed to race in MotoGP next season with KTM Tech3.
Agius is currently fourth in the Moto2 standings, with two wins this season in the United States and Spain.
What's next?
Aguis will be the only Australian on the MotoGP grid in 2027, with Jack Miller moving to World Superbike.
