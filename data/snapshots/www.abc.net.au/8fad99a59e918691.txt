Crowd watch
We were left with about 47,000 empty seats across three games in Sydney in the first week of finals, so it will be interesting to see what sort of crowd we get tonight.
The Sharks fans proved last week with their 17,429-strong crowd that they don't love travelling to the Sydney Football Stadium, and the Roosters aren't renowned for having the most passionate fanbase in Australian sport.
It's never a good look when the NRL can't fill a 42,000-seat stadium for a final, especially when the AFL is packing out far bigger arenas on the same weekend.
