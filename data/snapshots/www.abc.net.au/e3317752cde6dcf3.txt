Burnouts and drag races in Alice Springs as hundreds descend for 2026 Red CentreNATS
In short:
Beloved Alice Springs car festival the Red CentreNATS has drawn about 700 registered entrants for this year's event.
The event's program featured street cruising, drag races, burnout competitions and a street parade.
According to estimates, the festival - now in its 12th year - boosts the Alice Springs local economy by about $5 million each year.
