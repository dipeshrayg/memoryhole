At least four people have died in a fiery head-on crash between two vehicles on the Stuart Highway, south-east of Darwin, this afternoon.
In a statement, a Northern Territory Police Force spokesperson said the crash had occurred about 2 kilometres south of the turn-off to Marrakai Road, with police receiving multiple reports about 12:50pm.
"Police, Fire and Rescue Service crews and St John Ambulance attended the scene, where both vehicles were fully engulfed in flames," the spokesperson said.
"Sadly, there were no surviving occupants."
Police are continuing to investigate the circumstances surrounding the crash.
NT Police Detective Senior Sergeant Michael Schumacher said "due to the extent of the damage, [investigators] are still working to formally identify the deceased and determine whether there were any additional occupants in either vehicle".
"Due to the severity of the crash, which appears to have been a high-speed head-on collision, both vehicles caught fire, making this an extremely complex investigation," he said.
"At this stage, we have confirmed at least four people have died.
"One vehicle, believed to be a goods van, contains one deceased person.
"The second vehicle, a dual-cab utility, contains three confirmed deceased."
A section of the Stuart Highway has been closed in both directions.
Earlier this afternoon, traffic diversions were causing heavy delays to traffic and heavy duty vehicles were banked up on the side of the road.
"Detours for light vehicles will be implemented by the Department of Logistics and Infrastructure as soon as possible," the police statement said.
"Heavy vehicles are unable to pass through the area at this time, and motorists are urged to avoid the area and expect significant delays."
