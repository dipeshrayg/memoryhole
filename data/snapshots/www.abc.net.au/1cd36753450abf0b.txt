Fears shared on Australia reaching bleak milestone in the spread of H5 bird flu
In short:
Citizen scientists who have found dead birds during South Australia's harmful algal bloom say finding sick and dying birds since H5 bird flu arrived in Australia has been even more distressing.
Authorities are investigating a mass bird mortality event after 80 dead and sick birds were found on an island near Cape Jaffa on Friday.
What's next?
Results on the findings are expected in the coming days.
