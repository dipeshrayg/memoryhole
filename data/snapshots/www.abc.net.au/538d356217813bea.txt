Steeple chase/swimming
I think this is something we need to investigate a bit more.
At the 1900 Olympic Games in Paris, an obstacle race was a part of the swimming progamme.
Perhaps Welsh runner Cari Hughes was trying to channel that spirit when she took a dip in the water jump during the 3k steeple chase yesterday night.
Peter Kemp, who won a bronze medal in that race for Britain, was from just down the road in Galashiels, after all...
Aussie Frederick Lane won the obstacle event, if you're interested, and the non-obstacle 200m as well - although given both events were held in the Seine, I don't think it was exactly a flat water swim...
Kemp had to make do with a gold in the men's water polo instead.
Hughes, incidentally, still managed to run a national record of 9:40.71 despite her mid-race dip, to which she deserves an awful lot of credit.
