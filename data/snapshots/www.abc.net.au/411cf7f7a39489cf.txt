Australian Federal Police detective jailed after admitting to lying under oath about evidence
In short:
Trent Madders gave sworn evidence in 2020 that he later admitted was false, pleading guilty to perjury in the ACT Supreme Court after being charged in 2024.
Madders, a senior Australian Federal Police detective, was today sentenced to jail for the crime, which the judge described as a "tragic misjudgement".
What's next?
Madders, who has been suspended from duty since 2024, was sentenced to a year in prison, to be suspended after four months.
