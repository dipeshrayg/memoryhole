Police investigate punch during country football match in WA
In short:
A man in his 20s is recovering after being punched in the head at a country football game in WA.
The player was knocked unconscious during the weekend game between Bridgetown and Kojonup in WA's Lower South West Football League.
What's next?
Police are investigating the incident and the offending player has been referred to the Country Football WA tribunal process.
