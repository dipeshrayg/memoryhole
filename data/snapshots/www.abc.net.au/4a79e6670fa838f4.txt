In short:
Stacey Wakka scored three tries as New Zealand defeated Fetu Samoa in the women's Rugby League World Cup semifinal.
The Kiwi Ferns have made it to the decider in all seven women's World Cups since the inaugural tournament in 2000.
What's next?
New Zealand will face either Australia or Fiji, who play the second semifinal in Sydney on Sunday.
New Zealand cross-code superstar Stacey Waaka has led her country to another women's Rugby League World Cup final, scoring three tries in a dominant win over Fetu Samoa.
The Kiwi Ferns had made the final in all six previous editions of the women's world championship, but faced a stern early test from Fetu Samoa in their maiden semifinal.
But three tries to Waaka, and poor execution from the Samoans, ensured New Zealand would make it to a seventh consecutive decider.
Waaka, who won two Olympic gold medals in rugby 7s and two women's Rugby Union World Cups with New Zealand, continued her brilliant tournament where she has already scored six tries.
"A really close first half, but the team who was going to win was the one who played right to the end, then I think we done that," Waaka told the 7 Network post-match
"I love playing games with these young guns. It's fun learning off them too, because they've been in the game a little bit longer, but I'm just enjoying the whole experience.
"I can't wait for this next week and bring on whoever is in the final with us."
New Zealand will face either Australia or Fiji, who play the second semifinal in Sydney on Sunday.
In their maiden semifinal, Fetu Samoa suffered a horrible start with hooker Mary-Jane Taito throwing a forward pass in the first set of the match.
The Kiwi Ferns punished them immediately, with winger Mackenzie Wiki crossing in the right corner to claim an early lead after two minutes.
Fetus Samoa got themselves back into the contest with firm defence, as handling errors cruelled both sides. The Kiwi Ferns had nine errors and Samoa had seven, highlighting the tension of a semifinal.
Samoa's best chance of the first half came midway through the 35 minutes when they had four consecutive sets at the Kiwi Ferns' goal-line. But the first-time semifinalists failed to score, and would not come close again before half-time.
As the wind picked up and dark clouds closed in on Hunter Stadium, New Zealand scored a second try when Stacey Waaka charged onto a kick that was allowed to bounce by the Samoans to ensure her nation had an 8-0 lead.
As Fetu Samoa's errors continued, the Kiwi Ferns were able to punish them and take away any confidence the Pacific Island nation had.
Another kick to the in-goal was caught by a flying Waaka in the 44th minute for her second try, extending the lead to 14 points.
The Kiwis continued to press and iced the match with a three-try burst in six minutes to Mele Hufanga (58th minute), Kaiyah Atai (60th minute) and Waaka (64th minute).
Kiwi Ferns captain Raecene McGregor scored three minutes from time to cap off a well-earned win.
