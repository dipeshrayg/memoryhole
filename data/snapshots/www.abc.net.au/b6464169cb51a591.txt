Australian cricket star Beth Mooney set for her 100th ODI, with no eye on retirement
In short:
World Cup revenge is driving Beth Mooney as the Australian women's cricket star eyes long-term goals even as she gets set for a career milestone.
Mooney will face Bangladesh in her 100th career ODI in Brisbane on Wednesday, but she is considering pushing on to the 2029 Women's Cricket World Cup and beyond.
Mooney says she is still disappointed after Australia's World Cup semi-final loss to India last year.
