Trial of man accused of murdering friend with machete underway in Victoria
In short:
Christopher Reyes Gonzaga, 45, is accused of murdering Gregory Tan, 45, in an empty home in western Melbourne in 2024.
As his Victorian Supreme Court trial began on Monday, a jury was shown security camera footage in which yells could be heard ringing out in Deer Park in the early hours of April 15.
What's next?
Mr Gonzaga's barrister will make an opening address to the jury on Tuesday morning.
