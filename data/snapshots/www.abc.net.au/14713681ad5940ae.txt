Anthropic researcher quits saying AI developers believe 'it could kill us all'
In short:
An Anthropic researcher is quitting the AI company saying those building the technology "earnestly believe that it could kill us all by the end of the decade".
Jacob Coxon is one of a growing number of AI experts to call on the industry to slow down.
What's next?
Hundreds of employees from leading tech firms in July urged the US government to support an international effort to develop tools for managing the pace of advanced AI development.
