Victorian Health Complaints Commissioner warns against engaging alleged fake Melbourne doctor
In short:
The Victorian Health Complaints Commissioner has issued a public warning about a Melbourne woman who is alleged to have charged $20,000 for unapproved treatments while pretending to be a doctor.
Assma Samadi was charged by police in July with trafficking steroids, possessing drugs and common assault.
What's next?
Ms Samadi will next face court on October 19.
