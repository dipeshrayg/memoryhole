Watch and Act issued as out-of-control bushfire burns along Gippsland coast
In short:
The CFA has told residents of Golden Beach, Paradise Beach, Seacombe and Dutson Downs in Victoria's Gippsland region to monitor conditions after an out-of-control bushfire broke out on Sunday afternoon.
The fire is burning at the south end of Longford-Loch Sport Road, in a south-easterly direction towards Paradise Beach.
What's next?
CFA crews are continuing to fight the blaze.
