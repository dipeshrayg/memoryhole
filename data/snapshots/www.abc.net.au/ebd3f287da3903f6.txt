Watch and Act issued as out-of-control bushfire burns in Victoria's Central Gippsland
In short:
The CFA has told residents of Golden Beach, Paradise Beach, Seacombe and Dutson Downs in Victoria's Gippsland region to monitor conditions after an out-of-control bushfire broke out on Sunday afternoon.
The fire is burning in a south-easterly direction along the Longford-Loch Sport Road at Seacombe.
What's next?
The CFA will provide further updates as crews fight the blaze.
