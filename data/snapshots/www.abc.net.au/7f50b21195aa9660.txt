VCAA investigating after website hacked during student exam period
In short:
The Victorian Curriculum and Assessment Authority (VCAA) says there is no indication exam papers have been accessed after its website was hacked.
Students in Victoria are still sitting exams, with a message on the compromised version of the VCAA website claiming that exam papers had been downloaded and distributed.
What's next?
The VCAA says it is conducting a forensic investigation to determine the extent of the attack.
