Spectacular try helps England to Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup glory
In short:
England claimed a third Wheelchair Rugby League World Cup title with victory over France in Wollongong.
Jack Brown's spectacular try on the stroke of half-time proved pivotal for the English, who powered to a 50-38 win.
Australia claimed third place with a 70-24 rout of Ireland.
