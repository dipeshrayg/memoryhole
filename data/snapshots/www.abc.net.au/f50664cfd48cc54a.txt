Charlie Hills bailed over alleged Grant Whelan assault, home arson in Coles Bay
In short:
Charlie Hills, 29, is accused of assaulting a 76-year-old man, alongside two other men, in the early hours of July 11 at a Coles Bay property, on Tasmania's east coast.
Mr Hills has been granted bail.
Jason Byrnes, 33, and Hayden Morrison, 31, are also accused of attempted aggravated burglary, aggravated burglary, aggravated armed robbery, aggravated assault, arson and motor vehicle stealing.
