'Enfants Rouges' covered food market in Paris destroyed by 'violent' fire
In short:
A "violent" fire destroyed the Enfants Rouges market in Paris early on Monday, with flames engulfing the 17th-century building.
It had existed since 1615 and was listed as a historical monument in 1982.
What's next?
As the cause of the fire is investigated, authorities say temporary locations will be found for the impacted businesses.
