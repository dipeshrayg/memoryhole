Police allegedly attacked with rocks and metal bars in 'appalling display' of unrest in Wadeye
In short:
Northern Territory Police say they were forced to use pepper spray on a group of armed men during a violent clash in the remote community of Wadeye yesterday.
Police allege officers were pelted with rocks, window louvres and metal bars, with one person allegedly firing a compound bow and striking a police car with an arrow.
What's next?
Police say investigations are ongoing and any identified offenders will be prosecuted.
