Mitchell Moses calf injury ends Rugby League World Cup for Kangaroos halfback as PNG begins tournament with wins
In short:
Mitchell Moses will miss the remainder of the Rugby League World Cup after being ruled out of the Kangaroos' title defence with a lower leg injury.
The Parramatta playmaker suffered the injury during Australia's tournament opener on Thursday night.
Meanwhile, Papua New Guinea's men's and women's sides began their tournaments with victories, and England eased to a 70-6 win over Wales in the women's competition.
