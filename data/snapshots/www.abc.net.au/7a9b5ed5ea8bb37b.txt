Mitchell Moses' calf injury ends Rugby League World Cup for Kangaroos halfback as Tonga defeats England
In short:
Mitchell Moses will miss the remainder of the Rugby League World Cup after being ruled out of the Kangaroos' title defence with a lower leg injury.
Meanwhile, England's hopes of reaching the semi-finals are already on life support after suffering a 30-12 loss to Tonga in Perth.
Papua New Guinea's men's and women's sides began their tournaments with victories, while England breezed past Wales in the women's competition.
