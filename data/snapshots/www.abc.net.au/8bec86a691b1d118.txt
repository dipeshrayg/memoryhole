'Cockroach' movement leaders jailed for five days after New Delhi protest
In short:
Two leaders of the youth-led Cockroach Janta Party have been detained in jail for five days.
They were detained after they scaled barricades to enter a historic site popular for protests and chanted slogans demanding the election chief's resignation.
What's next?
The detentions risk triggering fresh protests by youth supporters at a time when opposition groups and student bodies are holding almost daily demonstrations.
