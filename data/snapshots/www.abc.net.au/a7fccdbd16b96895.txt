Prominent Darwin doctor charged with child abuse material offences makes second bid for bail
In short:
A prominent Darwin doctor charged with possessing thousands of child sexual abuse images and videos will remain behind bars for at least another week while he is assessed for bail.
The man was denied bail in the Darwin Local Court earlier this week but has appealed that decision to the Northern Territory Supreme Court.
What's next?
The doctor will remain behind bars until at least August 10 when a final decision will be made on whether he should be released.
