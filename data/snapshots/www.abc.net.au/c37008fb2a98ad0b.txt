Fri, 30 Oct, 7:15 pm, Final score, Cats 88, Bulldogs 20
North Melbourne seals AFLW minor premiership, St Kilda suffers 11th loss
In short:
North Melbourne has defeated Richmond to secure the AFLW minor premiership.
The Kangaroos are the first club in the AFLW to win the minor premiership in three consecutive seasons.
St Kilda ended its season with a loss to Port Adelaide, the club's 11th defeat this season.
