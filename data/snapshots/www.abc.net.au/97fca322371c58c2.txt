NT Police Commissioner acknowledges officers' 'compassion and resilience' after Kumanjayi Little Baby
In short:
Increasing mental health support for Northern Territory police officers has been flagged as a key part of improving attrition rates during this year's NT Police Association conference in Darwin.
During his speech, Commissioner Martin Dole acknowledged the challenges faced by police this year, including the tragic death of five-year-old Kumanjayi Little Baby in Alice Springs.
What's next?
The police union has called on the NT government to bolster support for remote police including by offering better housing and incentives.
