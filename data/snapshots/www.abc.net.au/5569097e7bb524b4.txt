Fri, 30 Oct, 7:15 pm, Final score, Cats 88, Bulldogs 20
Darcy Vescio stars in milestone game as Blues and Swans reach AFLW preliminary finals
In short:
Darcy Vescio starred in her 100th game as Carlton defeated Hawthorn in their AFLW preliminary final.
Sydney upset Geelong by three points at Kardinia Park, marred by a serious head injury to Cats ruck Piper Dunlop.
What's next?
Geelong will face Melbourne and Sydney take on North Melbourne in the preliminary finals.
