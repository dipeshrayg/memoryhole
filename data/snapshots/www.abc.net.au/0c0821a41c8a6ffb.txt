Five people die from suspected overdoses in Canberra in a fortnight, prompting warning from health authorities
In short:
ACT health authorities have issued a warning after five people died within a fortnight from suspected drug overdoses and another two people survived an overdose in the past week.
It is suspected that opioids were involved in each case, a drug that carries a high risk of addiction.
What's next?
The public has been reminded to carry naloxone if they plan to take drugs, as it can rapidly reverse the effects of an overdose.
