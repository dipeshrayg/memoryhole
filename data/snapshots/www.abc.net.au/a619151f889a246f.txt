Four people connected to pork industry charged with large-scale financial crimes
In short:
Four people connected to the pork industry have been charged over an alleged large-scale financial crime operation.
Police allege about $10 million was funnelled out of businesses and more than $30 million in cash was transported between New South Wales and Queensland between 2017 and 2026.
What's next?
Two of the alleged offenders are due to appear in the Brisbane Magistrates Court on September 14.
