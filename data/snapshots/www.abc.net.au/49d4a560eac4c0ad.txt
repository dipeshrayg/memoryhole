What's on in Glasgow on day three?
It's day three of competition, with more swimming - Australian world record holder Cam McEvoy will go for gold in the 50m freestyle along with Jamie Jack and Flynn Southam. Lani Pallister will start her campaign for the 1,500m freestyle.
Teenage gun Sienna Toohey is up for a possible first medal in the women's 100m breaststroke, while the men's 4x200m freestyle relay team could get close to the top step on the dais in tomorrow morning's final.
The Diamonds had an easy win against Tonga last night, but tonight they're up against England's Roses from about 7:30pm.
In artistic gymnastics, Georgia Godwin will be hoping to defend her 2022 individual all-around gold medal.
Here's the basic rundown.
- 3x3 basketball/wheelchair basketball (8:30pm-6:15am)
- Artistic gymnastics (9pm-6am)
- Bowls (5:30pm-7am)
- Boxing (8pm-6:45am)
- Netball (6pm-7:30am)

Can the Aussies can secure another win in netball tonight?
Tonight will be the second time the Diamonds take the court for the 2026 Commonwealth Games, after defeating the Tongan Tala side on day two.
The Aussies will play England on day three, and we're expecting that match to be much closer than yesterday's end score of 99-38 (although I reckon the green and gold are strong enough to bag another win here!).
The English netballers defeated Northern Ireland last night, 83-34, and will be looking for another win - I'm sure the Roses haven't forgotten the 2022 Commonwealth Games semi-final, when the Diamonds claimed victory over England by 9 points.
Wales and Scotland are working their way through the second quarter of their match now, then the Aussies will return to The Hydro for their game.
If we win tonight, it'll be the seventh Commonwealth Games victory Australia holds over England - the Roses have only defeated us one on previous occasion.
