SA Health employee reprimanded for accessing Tony Modra's medical records
In short:
One SA Health employee has been reprimanded for accessing the private medical files of AFL legend Tony Modra.
Modra was seriously injured in a truck crash near Victor Harbor in June.
What's next?
SA Health says an investigation into two more workers is ongoing, while six other employees were no longer being investigated.
