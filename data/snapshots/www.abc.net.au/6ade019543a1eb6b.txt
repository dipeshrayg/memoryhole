Top End shaken as major earthquake hits Timor-Leste
In short:
Top End residents have been rattled after a 6.8-magnitude quake struck north-east of Lospalos in Timor-Leste on Saturday.
Darwin is regularly shaken by quakes occurring near Indonesia and Timor-Leste but this was stronger than usual.
Darwin felt a lighter shaking just days ago from a 6.6-magnitude quake in the Banda Sea.
