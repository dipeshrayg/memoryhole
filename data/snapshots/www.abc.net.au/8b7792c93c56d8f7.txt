Kangaroos survive Blues scare to keep AFLW record alive with 10-point win
In short:
North Melbourne is still unbeaten after a gritty 10-point victory over Carlton at Arden Street.
The Kangaroos have won a record 32 consecutive matches.
Melbourne, Richmond, Fremantle and Sydney also posted wins on Sunday.
