Pedestrian dies after crash at Salisbury North in Adelaide's northern suburbs
In short:
A pedestrian has died at the scene of a crash at Salisbury North in Adelaide's northern suburbs on Sunday morning, while a second pedestrian was taken to hospital with serious injuries.
Emergency services were called to the intersection of Waterloo Corner Road and Whites Road just after 10am when a vehicle collided with a traffic light pole and two pedestrians.
What's next?
The 19-year-old driver, who was uninjured, has been charged with causing death by dangerous driving and will appear in the Elizabeth Magistrates Court on December 14.
