Sat, 15 Aug, 11:00 pm, Final score, AUS 198 & 284, BAN 426 & 1/57
Georgia Voll smashes quickfire ton as Australia wraps up Bangladesh series
In short:
Australia defeated Bangladesh by nine wickets to take an unassailable 2-0 lead in their ODI series at Allan Border field.
Aussie opening batter Georgia Voll struck an unbeaten 131 off 97 balls after Sobhana Mostary had become the first woman from Bangladesh to score an ODI century against Australia.
What's next?
The three-match series concludes on Wednesday at the same venue.
