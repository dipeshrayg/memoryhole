Radio giant ARN unveils Kyle and Jackie O replacement trio
In short:
Bondi Vet television personality Chris Brown, occasional KIIS fill-in host Amy Gerard and former Nova star Jack Charles have been named as the Sydney trio replacing Kyle Sandilands and Jackie "O" Henderson.
The announcement comes after radio giant ARN cancelled the Kyle and Jackie O show after an on-air spat in February.
What's next?
Another team will be announced to replace the outgoing hosts' broadcast in Melbourne.
