GWS captain Toby Greene speaks about future, says he's made no decision to leave the Giants
In short:
GWS skipper Toby Greene denies he has made up his mind to go home to Victoria.
Greene told a podcast he was planning to meet Geelong and Essendon but he would have told GWS if he was leaving.
Greene has played 282 games for the Giants since the club's first AFL game in 2012.
