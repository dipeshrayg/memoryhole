Dolphins hang on to beat the Titans
The Dolphins led 12-0 inside the opening 10 minutes and 14-12 at the break, but despite a late surge could not beat the finals-bound Dolphins, who claimed a tense 20-24 victory at Robina.
Jayden Campbell and AJ Brimson gave the Titans a brilliant start, before Hamiso Tabuai-Fidow and Herbie Farnworth fought back before the break.
The Dolphins looked to kick away early in the second term with tries to Ray Stone and Tabuai-Fidow.
But the Titans fought back, Campbell scoring a second in game 100 to make it 20-24.
The hosts came desperately close to levelling things late on, but a last-minute sin binning to Moeaki Fotuaika ended the Titans' hopes, despite the home crowd believing with some justification that the sin binning was not only harsh, but should have been been shown to at least one Dolphin, with a very late shot and a trip going unpunished.
