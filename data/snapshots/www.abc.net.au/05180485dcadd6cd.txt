Taiwan shuts down ahead of Typhoon Champi as nearly 1,000 flights cancelled
In short:
Taiwan shut down schools and grounded hundreds of flights as Typhoon Champi hit the island today.
One person has been killed and more than 2,600 people have been evacuated.
What's next?
The storm's forecast track shows it will pass just off north-eastern Taiwan, turn north toward the Chinese coast and eventually move into southern Japan.
