Matt Canavan on brink of lower house move with Michelle Landry set to announce retirement
In short:
Nationals MP Michelle Landry is expected to announce on Friday that she will retire from federal politics at the next election.
Ms Landry's announcement paves the way for Nationals leader Matt Canavan to move to the lower house.
What's next?
Senator Canavan is expected to nominate for preselection to run as the Nationals candidate in Capricornia.
