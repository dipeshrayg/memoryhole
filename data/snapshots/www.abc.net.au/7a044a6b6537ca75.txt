Authorities respond as three detainees scale Banksia Hill Detention Centre roof
In short:
Three detainees scaled the roof of the Banksia Hill youth detention centre in Perth, damaging windows and an air-conditioning unit, before voluntarily heading down.
A state minister condemned the detainees' behaviour and said the incident would be investigated.
What's next?
Youth advocates and the opposition said the incident, which occurred a year after a similar event, highlighted the need for change in the youth justice system.
