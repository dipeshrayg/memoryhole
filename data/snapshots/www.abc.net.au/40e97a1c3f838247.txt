Sheffield Shield: Topsy turvy day at Gabba between Queensland and Victoria
Victoria started with a century opening stand, collapsed from 1-132 to 5-159, before Fergus O'Neill's half century allowed them to reach 9-282 at the close of day one against Queensland at the Gabba.
Sam Harper (63), Marcus Harris (56) and Campbell Kellaway (64) all scored half centuries at the top of the order before Peter Handscomb (4), Oliver Peake (0) and Liam Blackford (4) contributed just eight runs between them.
O'Neill steadied the ship to score 52, ably supported by Will Sutherland (22 not out), after Queensland's fightback.

Sheffield Shield: Tasmania dominate NSW in Hobart
Four wickets to veteran bowler Jackson Bird (4-39) has put Tasmania in a dominant position against New South Wales after two days at Bellerive Oval.
Tasmania were bowled out for 427, with Bradley Hope falling just short of a century to be dismissed on 99, one of Ben Dwarshuis's four wickets (4-85).
Then, Bird went to work, reducing the Blues to 8-174, with high profile batters Sam Konstas (5) and Kurtis Patterson (14) failing to score well.
Sheffield Shield: WA beat South Australia by 105 runs
The defending Sheffield Shield champions South Australia have fallen to a second-straight defeat, as WA completed a 105-run win at Adelaide Oval.

However, Ryan Rickelton thinks that Australia will find it very tough, especially their right handers.
"It was a lot easier for me as a left-hander because there wasn't much turn from the straight areas of the pitch," Rickelton said.
"When the off-spinner went wider and bowled into the rough, trying to lure me into playing outside off stump, there was a much greater chance of an edge or a dismissal.

What is the highest score chasing to win at Newlands?
Just so we know what Australia is up against here today, these are the highest successful chases at Newlands in Tests.
- 6-334 Australia vs South Africa 2002
- 2-236 South Africa vs Australia 2011
- 3-212 South Africa vs India 2022
- 5-211 South Africa vs India 2007
- 3-186 South Africa vs West Indies 2008
- 6-182 South Africa vs Pakistan 2013
So Australia, if they are to win today, will have to record the second highest fourth innings run chase in the ground's Test history by knocking off 288.
Of course, that 2002 Test was a cracker, an unbeaten 100 from Ricky Ponting getting the Aussies over the line for a four-wicket win as they successfully chased 331.
Australia still needed 202 runs to win heading into the final day, with Pointing anchoring the victory after a 101-run opening partnership between Matthew Hayden and Justin Langer.
There have been higher fourth innings scores than that at Newlands that have not resulted in a win:
- 5-354 West Indies vs South Africa 2004 (draw)
- 335 South Africa vs New Zealand 1962 (loss)
- 306 Sri Lanka vs South Africa 1998 (loss)
- 304 England vs South Africa 2005 (loss)
- 9-296 England vs South Africa 2010 (draw)
So it is more than possible to score big on this ground on a wearing pitch.
If Australia scores 288 it will be the seventh highest fourth innings score in Newlands' history as a Test venue.
