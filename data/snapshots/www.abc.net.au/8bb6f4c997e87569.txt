Funeral for Corporal Brendan Hannam killed in NT military training exercise held today
In short:
A funeral has been held for an Australian Army soldier killed during a military training exercise south of Darwin a fortnight ago.
Corporal Brendan Hannam was today remembered for his loyalty, dedication and commitment to his family.
What's next?
An investigation into the crash that killed Corporal Hannam is underway and the use of the Supacat HMT military vehicle has also been paused.
