Matt Renshaw has scored his first ODI century, while Nathan Ellis took five wickets to guide Australia to a 59-run win over hosts Zimbabwe in the first clash of their three-match series.
Australia chose to bat first, and the surprise decision from captain Mitchell Marsh was vindicated as the tourists scored 8-294 from their 50 overs, setting Zimbabwe an imposing target at Harare Sports Club.
In reply, Zimbabwe was dismissed for 235 in 45.1 overs, showing good fight but always behind the required run rate.
Renshaw, who last scored an international ton in a Test against Pakistan in 2017, made 109.
He featured in some key partnerships, while Cooper Connolly made 51.
Renshaw put on 72 runs with Alex Carey for the fifth wicket and 75 with Ollie Peake for the sixth wicket, leaving Zimbabwe with a required run rate of 5.90.
The 20-year-old left-arm quick Newman Nyamhuri finished with 5-62, only the second Zimbabwe bowler to take five wickets in an ODI against Australia.
Zimbabwe lost four wickets quickly before Craig Ervine and Sikandar steadied the innings with a 98-run partnership that offered the hosts hope of victory on a surface that traditionally gets better for batting as the day wears on.
Ellis had taken two wickets in his first spell and when he was reintroduced, took three balls to clean bowl Ervine for 59 and put the brakes on Zimbabwe.
Sikandar was dispensed by Spencer Johnson for 57 to leave Zimbabwe still significantly adrift.
Ellis finished with 5-31 in 9.1 overs, also cleaning up the tail.
The second ODI will be played on Friday in Harare, after which Australia moves to South Africa.
