Australians in Riyadh told to shelter in place as Saudis and Houthis trade attacks
In short:
The Australian embassy in Riyadh has issued a new shelter in place order as Saudi Arabia and Yemen's Houthis continue to exchange deadly attacks.
King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh closed over the weekend following an attack on the site which killed 14 people and injured more than 320 others.
What's next?
Riyadh's airport closure was extended on Monday, and Saudi Arabia's General Authority of Civil Aviation did not say when it would be reopened.
