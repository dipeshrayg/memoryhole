Commonwealth Games: Lachlan Kennedy and Torrie Lewis break Australian 100m national records, Eleanor Patterson wins gold in high jump
Eleanor Patterson won her second Commonwealth Games gold medal in the women's high jump on day five in Glasgow, leading home an Australian 1-2 on another stunning day for Australia.
Patterson was one of nine Australian gold medal performances, taking the team to 35 gold medals for the Games so far.
Twelve years after first triumphing in Glasgow as a teenager, Patterson equalled the Games record of 1.96m in a rain-interrupted final.
She managed the height with her third and last attempt before unsuccessfully attempting a new Games mark of 2m.
Patterson edged out Australian teammate and world champion, Nicola Olyslagers (1.93m).
Olyslagers failed with her three attempts at 1.96m and settled for silver.
On a big night at the main stadium, Lachie Kennedy and Torrie Lewis both missed out on gold, but broke 100m national records while capturing medals.
Kennedy ended Australia's agonising wait for a men's Commonwealth Games 100m medal by taking silver.
Australia's fastest man ran a personal best 9.85 seconds, beating Patrick Johnson's mark of 9.93 set in May 2003. It took a Games record to beat Kennedy, with Cameroon's Emmanuel Eseme stopping the clock at 9.83.
Nigeria's Kayinsola Ajayi (9.90) was third, with Australian Eddie Nketia (10.06) back in sixth.
Lewis broke her own 100m national record in the semi-finals then broke it again in the final, finishing third in 10.99.
New Zealander Zoe Hobbs (10.93) won gold ahead of England's Amy Hunt (10.98).
Kennedy's silver was Australia's first medal in the men's 100m since 1962, when Michael Cleary took bronze.
Kennedy broke 10 seconds and lowered his personal best twice on the night, including a 9.94 in his semi, despite wet and windy conditions.
"It's all about rising to the occasion," Kennedy said after a wet and windy night.
"I'm a big believer in you've got to bring your best when it matters most. That's what we've been training for all season.
"To be able to come out here in those conditions, against a field like that, and to be able to run a time like that just goes to show how much faith I have in my team and all the people supporting me back home."
There was also joy for Ky Robinson, who completed an unprecedented 10,000m double for Australia when he finished strongly to hold off several rivals and claim gold.
Robinson won in 27:48.93 ahead of India's Gulveer Singh and Isle of Man's David Mullarkey.
His triumph followed Rose Davies becoming the first Australian woman to win the event at the Commonwealth Games.
In the pool, Lani Pallister set a new Commonwealth Games record in winning her third gold of the meet.
Pallister won the 800m freestyle in a time of 8:11:12, beating New Zealand's Erika Fairweather.
Australia's Molly Walker took the bronze.
Pallister has now won the freestyle middle and distance triple - the 400, 800 and 1,500m freestyle.
In all, Australia won a total of five gold medals in the night session at Tollcross Swimming Centre.
