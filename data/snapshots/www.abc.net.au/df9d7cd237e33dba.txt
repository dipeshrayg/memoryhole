Fri, 18 Sept, 5:45 pm, Final score, Hawks 32, Kangaroos 25
North Melbourne's 32-game winning streak over after shock loss to Hawthorn
In short:
North Melbourne's record AFLW winning streak is over, with the Kangaroos falling to a shock 7-point defeat to Hawthorn in Cairns.
The defeat ended a run of 32 consecutive wins for North, an V/AFL and AFLW record.
Hawks midfielder Jasmine Fleming starred with 25 disposals in the absence of injured captain Emily Bates.
