Flydubai 'hero' pilot speaks from hospital bed
In short:
Captain Smit Machchhar, the 'hero' flydubai pilot has spoken with Indian prime minister Narendra Modi from his hospital bed in Abu Dhabi.
Mr Machchhar described the moment his co-pilot attacked him in an apparent attempt to down the commercial plane carrying hundreds of people.
Israeli prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu has also praised Mr Machchhar's actions and hailed the pilot a hero.
