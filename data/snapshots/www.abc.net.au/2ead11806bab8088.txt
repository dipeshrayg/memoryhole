Romanian prosecutors indict Tate brothers for trafficking minors, laundering charges
In short:
Romanian prosecutors has officially indicted manosphere influencer Andrew Tate alongside his brother Tristan.
Romanian prosecutors allege the Tates recruited their victims by seducing them before forcing them to produce pornographic content for a webcam business.
What's next?
