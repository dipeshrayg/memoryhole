Things are looking a little different tonight pre-game
Wait a minute, why have they announced the result of the coin toss an hour and a half before the start of the game? Well, things are going to look a wee bit different before the bounce for this one.
The coin toss has been done ahead of time, and teams will each go through their warm ups on the field well in advance of the game starting. All of this is to make the entrance onto the field more of an event, with a light show and music (not the club songs like usual) as the players run out.
Then they'll take their positions almost immediately and get the game underway. Hey, why not? Let's see how it looks.
