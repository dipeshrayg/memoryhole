Teen avoids custodial sentence for coward punch that left victim with brain injury
In short:
A teenager who pleaded guilty to two counts of basic assault causing harm over has avoided a custodial sentence in South Australia.
The offender had admitted to coward-punching an 18-year-old outside a Hindley Street nightclub, which left the victim with a traumatic brain injury.
What's next?
The victim's parents have asked prosecutors to appeal the sentence.
