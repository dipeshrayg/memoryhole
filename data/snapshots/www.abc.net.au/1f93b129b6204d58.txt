Youth who assaulted woman sleeping rough at Mitcham library pleads guilty to serious criminal trespass
In short:
A teenage boy has pleaded guilty to serious criminal trespass after he broke into several businesses in Adelaide's southern suburbs in November.
He previously pleaded guilty to causing harm with intent towards a woman who was sleeping rough.
What's next?
The 18-year-old man will remain in custody until his next court appearance in September.
