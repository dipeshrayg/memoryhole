WA government to appeal landmark native title ruling against Andrew Forrest's Fortescue
In short:
The WA government is appealing the decision by a Federal Court to award the Yindjibarndi native title group a record $150.3 million in compensation for the construction of iron ore mines on their land without permission.
The decision to appeal comes after the indigenous group announced they would also appeal the amount, which billionaire Andrew Forrest's company Fortescue is liable to pay.
The Yindjibarndi Ngurra Aboriginal Corporation says it is disappointed with the compensation while both the WA government and Fortescue had argued in court for a lower amount.
