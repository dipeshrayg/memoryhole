Snowy Mountains man jailed after admitting to abusing daughter and granddaughter
In short:
A Snowy Mountains man has been sentenced to 22 years behind bars after admitting to abusing his own family members between 2004 and 2024.
The court heard his offending only stopped after his wife witnessed him abusing his granddaughter.
What's next?
The man, who is aged in his 70s, will be eligible for parole in 2039.
