An Iranian drone strike on Kuwait left two Italians dead on Thursday, the Italian Foreign Ministry said.
What do we know so far?
The Italians were working for the Italian branch of German arms manufacturer Rheinmetall. Italian news agency ANSA said the victims were two men aged 36 and 65.
It said they had been carrying out maintenance work on detection antennas of the Skyguard air-defense system.
"An explosion caused by a drone attack... occurred at a site where two Italian technicians from the company Rheinmetall Italia were working," the statement from the Foreign Ministry said.
Kuwait's Defense Ministry said a Kuwaiti and Pakistani national were injured in the "criminal attack" on a "vital site" and taken to the hospital.
Iran denied that it had attacked Kuwaiti airspace.
Meloni extends 'heartfelt condolences' to victims of families
Italian Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni said she learned of the "tragic" deaths of two Italian citizens in Kuwait during a European Council meeting.
"On behalf of myself and the government, I express my deepest sorrow and extend my heartfelt condolences to the victims' families," Meloni posted on X. "My thoughts are with all those affected by the attack and their families."
European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen also said she was "saddened" by the news.
"We must step up pressure on Iran to put an end to these unacceptable attacks against its neighbors and our partners in the Gulf," von der Leyen said. "Today's events are yet another reminder that security in the Gulf is a matter of security for us all."
Kuwait, a US ally in the Middle East, has been frequently targeted by Iranian attacks since the start of the US-Israeli war against Iran in February.
