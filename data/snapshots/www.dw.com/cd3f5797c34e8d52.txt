Merz set to address Bavarian conservatives as party conference wraps up
German Chancellor Friedrich Merz is set to make an address at the Munich exhibition hall as the Bavarian Christian Social Union (CSU) closes its two-day party conference.
Merz's speech comes at a turbulent time for the CDU/CSU alliance and expectations among delegates for the speech by the leader of the conservative bloc are mixed.
The CSU is the Bavarian sister party of Friedrich Merz's Christian Democratic Union (CDU) and there remain a number of key issues that have many in the CSU concerned over the future of the party at a national level.
A point of contention is who from the conservative bloc will succeed outgoing President Frank-Walter Steinmeier, with there being no clarity as yet from Merz or CSU leader Markus Söder, as to who their candidate would be.
Coupled with that leadership question, there is discontent over the pace of implementation of reforms of Germany's retirement, nursing care and health insurance systems.
There is also concern over a strategy to deal with the rise of the far-right in the form of the Alternative for Germany party (AfD).
Hitler's Eagle's Nest retreat catches fire, police say
A fire broke out on Friday evening at the Kehlsteinhaus in Bavaria more commonly known as the Eagle's Nest and used as a retreat by Adolf Hitler and Nazi officials.
The Kehlsteinhaus was engulfed in flames, a police statement read, adding that a suspect has been arrested and the incident was being criminally investigated.
