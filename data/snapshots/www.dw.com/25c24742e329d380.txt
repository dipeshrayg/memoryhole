German economy records better-than-expected growth in second quarter
Germany's economy grew by 0.3% in the second quarter of 2026, slightly more strongly than initially estimated, official data shows.
The Federal Statistical Office, known as Destatis, revised its preliminary estimate up from 0.2%, saying exports and stronger-than-expected retail and wholesale activity helped drive the improvement.
Exports of goods and services rose by 2.0% from the previous quarter, with goods exports up 2.6%.
Imports increased by 1.5% overall. GDP was 1% higher than in the second quarter of 2025.
"The growth momentum in the German economy from the start of the year is continuing," Destatis President Ruth Brand said, adding that exports were again the main driver.
Despite the good news, investment remained subdued:
- Equipment investment fell 1.4%
- Overall fixed investment declined 0.2%
- Construction investment edged up just 0.1%
- Private and government consumption each increased 0.1%
Europe's largest economy has now grown for three consecutive quarters.
Third drone found after Leipzig airport attack - reports
German investigators have discovered a third drone and suspected explosives linked to an incident at Leipzig/Halle airport earlier this month, according to German media.
The drone was found on August 14, 10 days after the incident, west of the airport. Investigators reportedly discovered around 50 grams (1.8 ounces) of a substance suspected to be the military explosive hexogen.
The initial reports came from public broadcasters NDR and WDR and the Süddeutsche Zeitung newspaper.
On August 4, an explosives-laden drone was discovered near a Ukrainian Antonov cargo aircraft used to transport military supplies.
Investigators also suspect a second drone may have collided with a DHL cargo aircraft shortly after the airport was temporarily closed.
Security sources cited by the German media reports suspect Russian intelligence involvement.
Russia has denied accusations that it was behind the attempted attack.
Leipzig/Halle is one of Germany's most important cargo airports and also serves as a military logistics hub.
