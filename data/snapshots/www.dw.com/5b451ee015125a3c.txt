Germany's powerful IG Metall trade union said Thursday it has reached a pay deal with employers after more than 24 hours of talks.
What is in the deal?
IG Metall and employers agreed on the following points in their agreement:
- The deal foresees a pay increase in two phases: 2.0% from May 2027 and 2.3% from April 2028
- The agreement will also entail one-off payments in March 2027 of between €150 ($164) and €450
- The collective agreements will be in effect until November 30, 2028
What is IG Metal and what are the significance of the talks?
IG Metall represents 3.7 million workers in the German metal and electrical industry. It is Germany's largest trade union.
The talks come amid job cuts across multiple sectors in the German economy, such as the automotive sector.
IG Metall represents workers in major German companies such as Volkswagen, Mercedes-Benz, Siemens, ThyssenKrupp, and Salzgitter AG, among others.
