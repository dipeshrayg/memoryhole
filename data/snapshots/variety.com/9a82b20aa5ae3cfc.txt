Shawn Levy's 21 Laps Entertainment has signed a major overall film deal with Sony Pictures.
21 Laps lands at Sony after a competitive process with multiple studios bidding to get into business with the blockbuster filmmaker and his prolific production company. The rich new deal extends across all of Sony Pictures' labels and begins in fall 2027.
The pact also reunites Levy with Sony Pictures Motion Picture Group chairman and CEO Tom Rothman and Motion Picture Group president Sanford Panitch. The trio's relationship goes back more than two decades, boasting some of Levy's early successes as a director and producer, including the "Night at the Museum" movies, "Cheaper by the Dozen" and "Date Night."
"I go ALL the way back with Shawn. We made a pile of hits together before he went on to far greater successes, none of which surprised me in the slightest," Rothman said in a statement announcing the news.
"His talent as a producer, director and head of the coolest creative company around, is rivaled only by his unmatched enthusiasm," Rothman continued. "I feel lucky as hell to reunite, and excited for everyone at Sony to discover what I have long known - Shawn Levy is the real deal."
Levy, whose 21 Laps will celebrate its 21st anniversary next year, was equally enthused.
"Tom, Sanford and I have a long history together, and some of the most formative and rewarding experiences of my career came during the years we worked together at Fox," Levy said in a statement. "Tom saw the future of my career before I did, and he bet on me early and with a big-hearted (and loud!) conviction that is one of his trademarks, and for which I'll be forever grateful."
About the future of the company, Levy added: "21 Laps was built on empowering fellow filmmakers and helping them tell stories imbued with their unique voices and visions. Along with my entire 21 Laps team, I'm thrilled to begin this next chapter with the team at Sony, and am excited about the wide-open breadth of opportunity across Sony's many labels. I deeply value their commitment to theatrical filmmaking."
News of the Sony Pictures deal comes after Levy and 21 Laps set an overall TV deal at Disney's 20th Television in September, following a long tenure at Netflix. The watermark for the company's TV business is, of course, executive producing Netflix's groundbreaking and Emmy-winning series "Stranger Things." 21 Laps will executive produce the upcoming live-action prequel series "Other Worlds: A Stranger Things Adventure."
21 Laps films have grossed more than $5 billion at the worldwide box office. Recent successes include Marvel's "Deadpool & Wolverine," directed, produced and co-written by Levy, which grossed more than $1.3 billion worldwide, and A24's "Backrooms," which became the studio's highest-grossing release worldwide. Next year, Levy will debut the much-anticipated "Star Wars: Starfighter." The film, directed and produced by Levy and starring Ryan Gosling, will launch in theaters on May 28, 2027. Levy will then reteam with Gosling and Marvel for "Ghost Rider," which is slated for a July 28, 2028 theatrical release.
The company's slate of upcoming projects also includes Universal Pictures' romantic comedy "The Catch," starring Emma Stone and Chris Pine; Netflix's drama series "The Sticks," starring Michelle Monaghan; Amazon MGM Studios' supernatural horror film "Seasons"; Netflix's feature comedy "One Attempt Remaining," starring Jennifer Garner and John Cena; and an untitled Netflix spy feature starring Kevin Hart and Henry Cavill.
Levy and 21 Laps Entertainment are represented by WME and Wendy Kirk at JSSK.
