"SNL U.K." star Ania Magliano is adding more stand-up comedy dates across the pond.
The actress and comedian, who co-anchors "Weekend Update" on the British version of "Saturday Night Live," is bringing her stand-up show "Peach Fuzz" to U.S. audiences. She has added new dates in 2027 after already selling out several dates at New York's The Bell House and Los Angeles' Dynasty Typewriter.
Tickets will be available for pre-sale via Magliano's mailing list on Thursday, Nov. 12, at 10 a.m. local, followed by general on-sale on Friday, Nov. 13, at 10 a.m. local. All ticketing information can be found here.
According to a description of the show, 'Peach Fuzz' sees Magliano "once again deliver a stellar hour of stand-up comedy with her signature laidback, story-telling style and relatable humour. Armed with a microphone, Ania will explore everything and anything, sharing all of her hinged and unhinged inner thoughts - including musings on her own body's idiosyncrasies, experience in a flotation tank, Sabrina Carpenter (everyone's favourite pop girly) and Winston Churchill (everyone's favourite historical icon)."
