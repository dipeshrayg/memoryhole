As people traveled back home for Thanksgiving, a lot of moviegoers made a stop at Panem to return to the world of "The Hunger Games." The new prequel, "Sunrise on the Reaping," ruled the Thanksgiving box office with $11.6 million on Thursday.
Lionsgate's latest entry in the YA action franchise will dominate the box office for the second week in a row. It's on track to make $85 million over the five-day Thanksgiving break, bringing its domestic total to $233 million. Original "Hunger Games" stars Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson and Woody Harrelson (who appear in a cameo) let a new generation take the spotlight in this prequel, which focuses on a younger version of Harrelson's character Haymitch as he enters the 50th Games. The cast includes Joseph Zada, Mckenna Grace, Elle Fanning, Jesse Plemons, Ralph Fiennes, Kiernan Culkin, Glenn Close and more.
Another entry in a long-standing franchise, Universal's comedy "Focker-in-Law," came in second place with $7.7 million on Thursday. It will beat out this week's fellow newcomer, Disney's original animated movie "Hexed," and end the five-day frame with an estimated $40 million. Ariana Grande joins the Focker family fun in the new movie, which brings back Ben Stiller, Robert De Niro, Owen Wilson and Blythe Danner.
"Hexed," starring Hailee Steinfeld, Rashida Jones and Jodie Foster, made $4.5 million on Thanksgiving, putting it at third place. It's projected to make $34.4 million over the five-day weekend. Steinfeld stars as a teenage witch who discovers her powers and gets transported to a world of magic in Disney's latest animated offering.
Elsewhere at the box office, Paramount and Johnny Depp's "Ebenezer" is coming in at fourth place with an estimated $13 million through Sunday, and Amazon MGM's "I Play Rocky" is on track for fifth place with $8.8 million over the same time. Netflix's "The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth," starring Brad Pitt, is only releasing in Imax screens and projected to make $3.5 million in its first five days.
