Hulu is continuing the "Secret Lives" universe. "The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County" has been renewed for a 20-episode second season after a quite successful first season.
The debut season, which dropped on Nov. 12, drove 35 million hours globally in its first 11 days on Hulu and Disney+; the premiere landed 12% ahead of Season 5 of the flagship series. Overall, the franchise has driven over half a billion total hours globally.
The first season, consisting of eight episodes, followed the lives of dynamic young mothers in Orange County. A spinoff of Utah-set headline-making "The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives," this season's cast included Ashleigh Pease, Aspyn Ovard, Jen Affleck, Bobbi Althoff, Avery Woods, Salomé Andrea, McCall DaPron, Jordan McClanahan and Madison Bontempo. DaPron's sister, Mayci Neeley, is a cast member on the original "Secret Lives of Mormon Wives" and made a cameo on Season 1 of the spinoff; Affleck is also a cast member on both series.
While some of the women - DaPron and McClanahan, in particular - are current members of the Mormon church, not everyone in the group is on the same page about religion.
"Technically, I am baptized into the Mormon Church. I don't identify with being Mormon, but I've been to the Mormon Church a million times. There's so much Mormon influence in my life, even just with getting married at 19 and having kids young - the culture has shaped me so much, whether I like that or not," Ovard told Variety ahead of the Season 1 debut. "I think there's so much [the cast] can gain from each other. I mean, everyone is so different, and especially the girls who are active Mormons, there's so much that they can learn from us girls who are very progressive and very liberal... even though I'm not a Mormon wife, I feel like I definitely have a place on the show, and I think it makes sense that I'm there."
"The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives: Orange County" is produced by Jeff Jenkins Productions, with 3BMG and Walt Disney Television Alternative. Executive producers are Jeff Jenkins, Russell Jay-Staglik, Amanda Weinstein, Melissa Bidwell, Brandon Beck, Ross Weintraub, Reinout Oerlemans, Georgia Berger, Lisa Filipelli and Danielle Pistotnik.
