Tom Hardy is officially back on set for "MobLand" Season 3, with cameras rolling this week.
The actor, who plays fixer Harry da Souza in the Paramount+ crime drama, was rumored to have been booted from the show earlier this year after "creative differences" with producer David Glasser and writer Jez Butterworth amid claims he was late for set and had demanded script changes.
But as Variety reported at the time, reports of Hardy's axing were premature, with Guy Ritchie and Paramount+ eager to hold on to the "Mad Max: Fury Road" star, and the trio managed to patch things up soon afterwards with filming for Season 3 due to start at the end of this year. That has now come to pass.
Hardy's co-stars Helen Mirren and Pierce Brosnan also return as heads of the Harrigan crime family, playing Maeve and Conrad respectively.
The show also stars Paddy Considine, Joanne Froggatt, Lara Pulver, Anson Boon, Mandeep Dhillon, Jasmine Jobson, Teddie Allen, Emmett J. Scanlan, Johnny Flynn, Ophelia Lovibond, Janet McTeer and Toby Jones.
Exec producers are Glasser, Guy Ritchie, Jez Butterworth, Tom Hardy, Ivan Atkinson, Dean Baker, Ron Burkle, David Hutkin, Bob Yari and Keith Cox.
Season 2, which Mirren called "madder and badder," launched in Sept. with the tenth and final episode dropping today.
Hardy attended the Season 2 premiere in London alongside Glasser, Ritchie, Mirren and Brosnan where everyone made nice for the cameras.
Introducing the cast on stage inside the theater, Glasser called Hardy the "captain of our ship" saying he "stayed with it to the bitter end."
