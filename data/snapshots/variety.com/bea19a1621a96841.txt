Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim's buzzy Elizabeth Holmes doc "You Can See Everything" is set to launch into Italy from the Torino Film Festival with the directors expected in tow.
After world premiering in the U.S. at Telluride, and segueing to the New York Film Festival, the doc, which offers an intimate look at the American health-technology company Theranos founder's life a month before she began an 11-year prison sentence for defrauding investors, is launching internationally by hitting the overseas festival circuit, starting with stopovers at Amsterdam's IDFA documentary festival and the Stockholm International Film Festival in mid-November, followed by Torino.
"There are films that make me fall in love with cinema all over again and remind me why I've dedicated so much of my life to it," said Torino artistic director Giulio Base in a statement. "You Can See Everything" is one of them: a true masterpiece, bold and unsettling, that turns the boundary between truth and mise-en-scène into a dizzying visual adventure," the Torino chief added.
"You Can See Everything" - which has become one of the year's biggest box office surprises, opening to a staggering $15.6 million in the U.S., followed by a spectacular $19.5 million in its second frame this past weekend - is being released in Italy by Andrea Romeo's I Wonder Pictures which is one of the country's top distributors and has close ties to A24.
"You Can See Everything" will hit cinemas in the U.K. and Ireland on Nov. 20 with A24 is self-releasing the film. Sony Pictures Worldwide has acquired distribution rights for Latin America, Scandinavia, South Africa, Germany, Austria, Greece, the Middle East, Israel, India, South Korea and Southeast Asia, with specific dates to be announced soon.
I Wonder has also not yet set an Italian release date for the film. The 44th edition of the Torino Film Festival, the third under Base's guidance, is set to take place in Turin from Nov. 24 to Dec. 2, 2026.
The Torino Film Festival's full lineup will be announced on Thursday.
