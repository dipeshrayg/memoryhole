Issa Rae's new HBO comedy pilot has added two new cast members.
Variety has learned exclusively that Rafael Castillo and Branden Cook are both joining the untitled half-hour pilot. They will appear alongside previously announced leads Dasan Frazier and Shane Paul McGhie.
The pilot was originally picked up at HBO in August. The logline states that the show follows Menzel (McGhie), "a 28-year-old barber, aspiring sports manager, and professional best friend to JB (Frazier), a 27-year-old up-and-coming boxer, as they navigate ambition, insecurity, loneliness, and the double-edged sword of success within the high-stakes world of professional boxing in Las Vegas."
Castillo will play Evan, who is described as "Menzel's childhood friend and part of JB's crew. He is a Vegas native and an aspiring comedian stuck at a desk job." Cook will play Kenny, said to be "a real estate agent from Arizona and the only one of the four in a committed relationship."
Castillo has previously appeared in shows like "Black Lightning" at The CW, "Mo" at Netflix, "BMF" at Starz, and "High Potential" at ABC. His recent credits include the Netflix series "Nemesis" and the hit comedy "Abbott Elementary."
Cook most recently starred in the popular Hulu series "Tell Me Lies," appearing as Evan across the show's entire three-season run. He also appeared in the Apple TV World War II drama "Masters of the Air" as well as in shows like "Industry" and "Chicago PD."
Castillo is repped by Daniel Hoff Agency, Art Haus Alliance, and Goodman Genow. Cook is repped by Independent Talent Group, Range Media Partners, Independent Artist Group and Hansen Jacobson.
Rae serves as writer and executive producer on the pilot under her Hoorae banner, with Prentice Penny serving as showrunner, executive producer, and director. Jax Clark also executive produces for Hoorae along with Penny's Penny For Your Thoughts Entertainment as well as Jonathan berry and Dave Becky of 3 Arts Entertainment.
