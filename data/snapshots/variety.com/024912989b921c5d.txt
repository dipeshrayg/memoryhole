Former blockchain executive David Berns, who served as president and chief operating officer of Blockchains, LLC, and also helped create the digital media platform ETHNews, is doing something entirely different for his next project: Roller derby.
Berns has been named CEO of Global Roller Derby League, which is looking to revive the sport for TV audiences when it launches next year. GRDL is run by Berns, along with "Broadway" Danny Wolf, president of league operations; Elie Dekel, president of GRDL brands; and Dave Harding, executive in charge of production.
"Roller derby has a remarkable history and an enduring appeal," Berns said in a statement. "We see an opportunity to reintroduce this iconic sport, a classic piece of Americana, with an entirely new approach. We combine the athleticism and excitement of a sports competition with the personalities, rivalries and storytelling that today's audiences expect. The Global Roller Derby League is building a powerful brand that we believe fans will embrace well beyond game day."
Roller derby remains a popular pastime; in Los Angeles, outfits including Angel City Derby, Derby Dolls, SFV Roller Derby and Los Anarchists Junior Derby draw plenty of interest.
GRDL is hoping to take that one step closer and make roller derby the kind of draw it once was during the early days of television. The sports entertainment property is planning to recruit male and female skaters from around the world to compete on five co-ed teams representing New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Dallas and Miami. GRDL hopes to attract a wide range of demos with its weekly televised games, which will take place in front of live fans.
"Roller derby has always had something special - it's fast, physical, unpredictable and filled with personalities you don't see in other sports. I've spent much of my career around this sport, and I know firsthand what makes it so exciting for fans of all ages," Wolf said in a statement. "What we're building with GRDL respects that history while introducing a new generation to the energy, competition and larger-than-life characters that make roller derby so unique."
The GRDL plans to hold open auditions for participants in early 2027, and it's pitching the franchise to potential broadcast and streaming partners.
Berns' past credits also include serving as chief financial officer of blockchain software developer Berns Inc. He was also a business litigation and international commercial attorney, and later a partner at Berns Weiss LLP.
Wolf has a professional wrestling and roller derby background, and served as the TV voice of roller derby franchises the Los Angeles T-Birds and San Francisco Bay Bombers. He was the on-air host and a writer for TNN's "RollerJam" and is known for bringing Memphis's Continental Wrestling Association to cable.
Dekel's entertainment branding, marketing, merchandising and gaming experience includes Fox Entertainment, Legendary Pictures, Saban Brands and more, working on brands such as "The Simpsons" and "Mighty Morphin Power Rangers."
Harding is an Emmy Award-winning producer ("Wage Slaves: Not Getting By in America") and has worked on projects such as the Academy Award-nominated "Encounters At The End of the World" and the feature documentaries "Grizzly Man" and "Chasing Trane."
