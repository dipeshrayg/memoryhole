After an epic Money in the Bank event with Roman Reigns defeated LA Knight to retain the World Heavyweight Championship, WWE travels to Saudi Arabia for WWE Crown Jewel 2026, a premium live event on Saturday, Nov. 7.
The event features four bouts, including three title matches for the Crown Jewel Championship, Women's Crown Jewel Championship and World Tag Team Championship.
Last year, WWE was in the top 10 in nearly 40 country's for Crown Jewel. The company hopes that this year's major would bring in even more viewers. In addition, it's likely there would be celebrity fans in-attendance during the event, such as Logan Paul, Mike Tyson and others.
When Is 2026 WWE Crown Jewel? Date, Time
Crown Jewel takes place at ANB Arena in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia on Saturday, Nov. 7. The start time is 12 p.m. ET/9 a.m. PT.
