Ariana Grande is teasing a documentary or concert film of her "Eternal Sunshine" tour, posting a clip on social media that reveals the movie will air on Peacock.
The singer posted a clip on Monday morning from the moment in her "Eternal Sunshine" tour where she stands in the middle of the stage and loops her vocals from the song "Eternal Sunshine." As the clip fades, text appears on the screen stating, "Coming soon to... Peacock."
Fans suspected that Grande may be working on a concert film after she posted a photo to social media last week of her in the editing bay alongside director Christian Breslauer and editor Andrew Morrow. Breslauer has worked several times with Grande in the past, lensing the videos for "Yes, And?" and "We Can't Be Friends (Wait for Your Love)" and serving as co-director and co-writer of "Brighter Days Ahead" alongside Grande. Morrow, meanwhile, edited Beyoncé's "Renaissance: A Film by Beyoncé" and Lil Nas X's "Lil Nas X: Long Live Montero."
A representative did not immediately return Variety's request for further comment.
Grande embarked on the "Eternal Sunshine" tour earlier this year, kicking off the trek at Oakland Arena on June 6. Throughout the tour, she performed more shows in fewer cities than normal, including five concerts at New York City's Barclays Center and five in Los Angeles split across the Kia Forum and Crypto.com Arena. The tour came to an end with 10 performances at London's O2 Arena.
Grande is currently gearing up for the release of the film "Focker-in-Law," the fourth entry in the series. The film is set for release on November 25.
