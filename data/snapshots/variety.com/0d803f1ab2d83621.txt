Charli xcx is bringing the "Music, Fashion, Film" tour overseas.
The avant-garde pop star has announced six U.K. and Europe dates in February 2027, including in Dublin, Ireland; Glasgow, Scotland; Manchester, England; London, England; Amsterdam, The Netherlands; and Paris, France. "Sue Me" and "Bowling Alley" singer Audrey Hobert will open for all dates.
Charli is about to kick off the tour for her latest album - which saw her ditch her signature autotune and dance beats for something a bit more rock 'n' roll - in the U.S. and Canada. She plays Philadelphia on Friday night and then arrives in Brooklyn for two shows, hitting Toronto, Boston, Washington D.C., Atlanta, San Diego, Los Angeles (for two shows), Glendale and Las Vegas.
The sign-up for presale access is live now through Sept. 15 on Charli's website, with artist pre-sale tickets going live on Sept. 16 at 11 a.m. BST/12 p.m. CET. General on-sale begins Sept. 18 at 9 a.m. BST/10 a.m. CET.
Popular on Variety
"Music, Fashion, Film" was critically acclaimed, with Variety's Jem Aswad calling the album a "sonic palette cleanser to launch the post-'Brat' era in earnest - familiar but sufficiently distanced from the past, leaving her free to go basically anywhere she wants in her next chapter."
