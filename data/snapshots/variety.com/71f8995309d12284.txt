Stelena fans, unite. Nina Dobrev and Paul Wesley, who co-starred in one of The CW's biggest hits, "The Vampire Diaries," are reuniting for in a new three-episode audio romance for Quinn.
"The Haunting of Pendle Creek" follows exes and true-crime podcasters Scarlett (Dobrev) and Ezra (Wesley), who are investigating mysterious disappearances in Pendle Creek. "The deeper they dig, the more they begin to suspect that the chilling local legend of this picturesque New England town might be real," the synopis reads. "As a blood moon rises and the case turns far more dangerous than they could have expected, Scarlett and Ezra must confront a charming predator and the feelings they've never quite buried."
The female-led story was what attracted Dobrev. "We spend so much time with male protagonists so I really love the fact that this is female-centric," she says. "I was also excited to partner together with Paul again. It felt like old times!" He echoes that: "I was excited to work with Nina again - it's been a while. Also, I have such an appreciation for empowering female stories."
While Wesley says he tries to "avoid vampire shows at all costs," he quips that he brought his fangs to this shoot. "That's the first time I've done anything vampire-related since the show." He never saw a world in which he and Dobrev would be narrating erotica together, "but here we are smutting it up ... Even though we played romantic interests for so long, it's been a minute and now we're such good friends, so it was very funny. We had fun."
Naturally, that meant a lot of laughs happening during the recording process. "Being in the booth together was truly so much fun - to be able to see each other and react off of each other," says Dobrev. "We just kept laughing, especially in the sultry moments."
This marks the fourth Quinn Originals duet production, following "Ember & Ice" (Hudson Williams and Connor Storrie), "Rent Free" (Mika Abdalla and Stephen Kalyn) and "Olympus U "(Lili Reinhart and Tom Bateman).
"Stefan and Elena are one of the most iconic couples in TV history. Fans are always tuning back into the world of 'The Vampire Diaries,' especially in the fall," says Caroline Spiegel, founder and CEO of Quinn. "We were so thrilled to bring this reunion to life!"
"The Haunting of Pendle Creek" is available now on Quinn.
