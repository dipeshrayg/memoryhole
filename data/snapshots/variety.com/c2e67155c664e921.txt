Jesús López, one of the most important executives in Latin music history, will take on a new role at Universal Music Group as Chairman Emeritus, Global Talent Services, UMG's artist management and services company.
López continues a career encompassing five decades in music and 30 years in leadership positions at UMG, most recently as Chairman and CEO of Universal Music Latin America & Iberian Peninsula. He has been working with Lucian Grainge, Chairman and CEO of UMG, to put in place a succession plan, with details to be announced in the coming weeks.
Conceived by López, GTS was launched in 2007 and has grown into a leading Latin artist services agency, focused on management, booking, promotion, and brand partnerships, with offices in eight countries, and Narcís Rebollo as its President and CEO - and now López as chairman emeritus.
López will also continue his work with Julio Reyes Copello's Art House. UMG's collaboration with Art House Academy combines education, mentorship, and artist development, helping nurture talents including consecutive Latin Grammy Best New Artist winners Joaquina (2023) and Ela Taubert (2024).
Grainge said: "Jesús is an industry icon who has helped change the course of Latin music on the world stage. Throughout our industry's evolution he has been a tireless advocate for the creative community, building a team that has advanced the careers of countless superstars. I'm grateful for the care he's taken to prepare the business for the future and delighted that he'll help shape the future of GTS, a company born of his own vision for serving artists. I look forward to continuing our work together."
López said: "As I begin this next chapter within UMG, I'm incredibly proud of what we've built together: a company that believes in artists early, stays with them and helps their music travel further than anyone imagined. I look forward to welcoming my successor and supporting our teams as they build on that success. As Chairman of GTS, I will continue pursuing a passion that has guided my entire career: helping artists realize the full scope of their ambitions. I'm grateful to Lucian for his trust and support over so many years, and excited to continue working with him and our teams to create new opportunities for artists."
From his early leadership roles at BMG Mexico and UMG's Latin American operations, López has worked closely with many of the biggest artists in Latin music, including Mecano, Juan Gabriel, Juan Luis Guerra, J Balvin, Juanes, Karol G, Enrique Iglesias, Luis Fonsi, Daddy Yankee, Don Omar, Sebastián Yatra, David Bisbal, and Aitana.
In recognition of his contribution to music and culture, López received Spain's Gold Medal for Merit in Fine Arts, presented by King Felipe VI and Queen Letizia in April 2024.
