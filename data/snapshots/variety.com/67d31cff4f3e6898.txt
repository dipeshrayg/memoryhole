Rosé is set to perform at this year's LACMA Art+Film Gala.
The Los Angeles County Museum of Art event takes place Nov. 7. The evening, as previously announced, will honor "Dune" filmmaker Denis Villeneuve and artist Vija Celmins.
"The LACMA Art+Film Gala celebrates creative icons and the connections between all forms of creativity, all around the world," said museum trustee Eva Chow, who co-chairs the gala with Leonardo DiCaprio. "As a global pop superstar, Rosé will amplify the spirit of the evening with her incomparable talent and style."
Originally a member of Blackpink, Rosé earned over 10 billion solo streams to date with her first studio album "rosie." The Korean New Zealander's single "APT" with Bruno Mars earned three Grammy nominations.

LACMA Art+Film is one of Los Angeles's most glamorous and high-powered events of the year. Nearly 600 guests attended in 2025 when "Sinners" filmmaker Ryan Coogler and artist Mary Corse were honored and Doja Cat performed. The evening raised a record-breaking $6.5 million toward supporting the museum's initiative to make film more central to its curatorial programming, while also funding its broader mission.
Guests included Elle Fanning, Lorde, Angela Bassett, Cynthia Erivo, Samara Weaving, Benito Skinner, Tessa Thompson, Emma Roberts, Paris Hilton, Salma Hayek, Omar Apollo, Kerry Washington, Billie Lourd, Finneas, Matt Friend, Justine Lupe, Quinta Brunson, Jake Shane, Queen Latifah, Demi Moore, Ryan Destiny, Demi Lovato, Hannah Einbinder, Kristen Wiig and Cristin Milioti.
Past performers have included Charli XCX and Troye Sivan, Elton John, Lenny Kravitz, Annie Lennox, Beck, Stevie Wonder, Florence and the Machine, Anderson .Paak and Sting.
Gucci returns as the gala's presenting sponsor.
LACMA is the largest art museum in the Western United States with a collection of more than 155,000 objects.
