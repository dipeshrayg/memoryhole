Back in September, Blizzard Entertainment held its fan convention BlizzCon for the first time since 2023, just after Activision Blizzard was acquired by Microsoft in a blockbuster $69 billion transaction, which signaled a renewed investment in gaming by the software giant, from Sept. 12 to the 13.
BlizzCon drew 35,000 in-person attendees to the Anaheim Convention Center and racked up 12.5 million live viewers for the event. Less than two months later, Blizzard is celebrating the launch of "World of Warcraft Forever," a title first announced on stage during the live show in September to much fanfare.
For this week's episode of Variety's "Strictly Business," Jennifer Maas was on the ground at BlizzCon 2026 speaking with Blizzard Entertainment president Johanna Faries, "World of Warcraft Forever" game director Ion Hazzikostas, celebrity attendees including Simu Liu, T-Pain, and the members of K-pop sensation Le Sserafim about the festivities and what this moment means for the future of Blizzard.
