Variety announced today that Rosalía, Hilary Duff, and Slayyyter will receive accolades at Variety's 10th Annual Hitmakers Celebration in Los Angeles on December 5.
Rosalía, who is nominated for nine awards at the 2027 Grammys, will receive the Hitmakers Masterpiece of the Decade honor for her groundbreaking album "LUX," Hilary Duff will receive the Generational Icon Award, and Slayyyter will receive the Breakthrough Artist of the Year Award.
Other honorees include RAYE (Hitmaker of the Year), Olivia Rodrigo (Songwriter of the Year), Katseye (Group of the Year), and Sienna Spiro (Visionary Voice), with many more to be announced in the coming weeks. TikTok is a Premier Partner of the event.
A two-time Grammy and 13-time Latin Grammy Award-winning artist and producer, Rosalía is currently riding the success of her "LUX" album, released in 2025, which earned five Billboard No. 1 chart placements, entered the Billboard 200 at No. 4 and is nominated for nine 2027 Grammy Awards. Numerous outlets all named it among the year's best albums, and it has since picked up seven Latin Grammy nominations, while "La Perla" is nominated for best Latin song at the MTV Video Music Awards. Her earlier work laid the groundwork for that momentum: in 2022, she wrote, performed, recorded, and produced "Motomami," which debuted at No. 1 on Spotify's Global Album Chart, earned the year's highest Metacritic score, and fueled the sold-out Motomami World Tour. Before that, her breakthrough sophomore album, "El Mal Querer," reimagined flamenco for a new era and won a Grammy and eight Latin Grammys, including album of the year, while also making her the first Spanish-language artist nominated for best new artist at the Grammys.
Hilary Duff made a triumphant return to music this year with "luck... or something," her sixth studio album and first in a decade, via Atlantic Records. Co-written by Duff and produced by her husband, Grammy-winning songwriter and producer Matthew Koma, alongside Brian Phillips, the confessional pop record explores motherhood, marriage, and self-discovery in her 30s, led by singles "Mature," "Roommates," and "Weather for Tennis." The album debuted at No. 3 on the Billboard 200-marking her highest chart debut since 2007 and her sixth career Top 5 entry-while hitting No. 1 in Canada and Australia and earning "Best Albums of 2026" honors from Variety, Billboard, and Rolling Stone. A multiplatinum artist and 2026 TIME 100 honoree, Duff has also built a multifaceted career as an acclaimed actor (including an award-nominated role on "Younger"), producer, entrepreneur, philanthropist, and New York Times best-selling author. Following the surprise release of her re-recorded hits EP "(Mine)" and her new "Holiday Things" EP, Duff is currently in the middle of her sold-out global headline run, the lucky me tour-recently named Outstanding Pop Tour of the Year by Billboard - which has already expanded into 2027.
While her early releases established a devoted cult following, Slayyyter's "WOR$T GIRL IN AMERICA" marked her boldest reinvention yet. Her third studio album is authentically her. The 4x Grammy nominee revisited her upbringing in St. Louis, and decided to make "iPod music" - a free-for-all of feral punk, corroded pop, and raunchy rap - conjuring the "Midwest-core tweaker bar rat" characters of her youth. Early singles "BEAT UP CHANEL$," "CANNIBALISM!," and "CRANK " steadily built hype for the project ahead of its release, with fans fervently embracing the raw, swaggering SLAYYYTER that emerged from the ashes of her past selves. The album became her highest-charting album to date, debuting at No. 22 on the Billboard 200 and No. 1 on Top Dance Albums, while earning widespread critical acclaim from outlets including Rolling Stone, Billboard, Pitchfork, and many more. Its success also earned SLAYYYTER two 2026 MTV VMA nominations, including 'Best Dance' with "DANCE..." This year, she brought that momentum to major festivals such as Coachella, Governors Ball, Lollapalooza, with audiences doubling in size each time. Slayyyter's fourth studio album, "wor$t man in america" is scheduled for release on December 4.
"We're so excited to add these three stellar names to Hitmakers, whose music has soundtracked the past year in totally different ways," said Variety's Executive Editor, Music Jem Aswad. "Rosalía has set a new bar for artistry, ambition and vision with her incredible "LUX" album, video and tour. Hilary Duff chose the perfect time to return to music, invigorating fans old and new with "luck... or something" and her fantastic live shows. And Slayyyter's two albums show her versatility as a singer and songwriter - and her concerts prove that she is a straight-up rock star. We can't wait to honor them on December 5."
