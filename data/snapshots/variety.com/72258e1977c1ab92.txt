Ben McKenzie will moderate the premiere Q&A for Netflix's "The Altruists" with stars Julia Garner and Anthony Boyle and showrunner Graham Moore on Nov. 19 in New York, the same day the series premieres on the streamer.
Known now as an anti-crypto activist as well as an actor, McKenzie followed his successful runs on television shows like "The O.C." and "Gotham" by writing the New York Times bestselling novel "Easy Money," which compares cryptocurrency to the devastation of historic financial crashes. He then wrote, directed and produced the 2025 documentary "Everyone is Lying to You for Money," which chronicles his investigation into the crypto industry for the book, and includes a one-on-one sitdown interview with Sam Bankman-Fried, whose story is the subject of "The Altruists" (he's played by Boyle).
"Everyone is Lying to You for Money" premiered at SXSW London in June 2025 and played at several other festivals. Independent distributor The Forge acquired distribution and sales rights in March 2025, and the film had a limited theatrical run starting in April 2026. Variety's Owen Gleiberman described the film as "lively, knife-sharp, impeccably researched" and said it "answers every conceivable question you've ever had about crypto." The film is still looking for a streaming partner, but is currently available for rent via Eventive, C5.now, Amazon and Kanopy in the U.S.
"The Altruists" stars Boyle and Garner as Caroline Ellison. Premiering Nov. 19, the Netflix limited series follows the crypto Bonnie and Clyde through the rise and fall of FTX, the Bankman-Fried-founded cryptocurrency exchange and hedge fund.
At his peak, Bankman-Fried was the 41st-richest American on the Forbes 400 list. FTX was forced to go bankrupt in November 2022 after evidence of fraud surfaced. Bankman-Fried was arrested in December 2022 and has since been sentenced to 25 years in prison. Ellison was sentenced to 24 months in prison and served 14 before being released in January.
The eight-episode series was created by Graham Moore and inspired by the New York Magazine articles written by Kevin T. Dugan and Jen Wieczner. Executive producers include Moore, Jacqueline Hoyt, Barack and Michelle Obama, Jessie Dicovitsky and Vinnie Malhotra for Higher Ground Productions, Scoop Wasserstein for New York Magazine/Vox Media Studios, Garner, Tonia Davis, James Ponsoldt and Lauren Morelli.
