Fanatics Studios has tapped Jamal Henderson as its first-ever president of film and television.
Henderson joins the sports entertainment studio, a joint venture between Fanatics and OBB, after working with LeBron James and his SpringHill Entertainment company for more than a decade. Led by OBB Media founder Michael D. Ratner, Fanatics Studios is an offshoot of the massive sports conglomerate Fanatics, which is known for merchandise, trading cards, collectibles, gambling and live events like its annual Fanatics Fest.
In his new role, Henderson will oversee all of Fanatics Studios' content initiatives across film, television and digital, leading projects from development through production. He will be based in Los Angeles and report directly to Ratner.
Since launching earlier this year, Fanatics Studios has co-produced the 2026 ESPYs, "Fanatics Fest: All Access" for ESPN and "The Fanatics Flag Football Classic." Its upcoming projects include a documentary on Olympic gold medalist Suni Lee and the official Olympic film for LA28.
Henderson is an Emmy Award-winning producer whose credits at SpringHill include Netflix's "Starting 5," "Hutle," "Rez Ball," "On the Rise: JuJu Watkins," "Top Boy" and "Naomi Osaka: The Second Set," as well as the theatrical movie "Space Jam: A New Legacy." At SpringHill, Henderson also helped form long-standing relationships with talent and partnerships across networks, streamers and brands.
"There's never been a bigger opportunity in sports storytelling, and we intend to lead what comes next," said Ratner. "Jamal is one of the most respected and well-connected executives in sports and entertainment, with a proven ability to turn ambitious ideas into premium content. With Jamal leading film and television for us, we can create original franchises with global appeal while making the studio a powerful part of the broader Fanatics flywheel."
"Fanatics Studios is in a powerful position to rethink what a modern entertainment studio can be, with access to some of the biggest names and stories in sports and culture," said Henderson. "Michael (Ratner) and Michael (Rubin) have built incredible momentum in a short amount of time, and I'm excited to build on what's already in motion. Now it's about translating that early success into original content and franchises that break through at a scale few studios can match."
