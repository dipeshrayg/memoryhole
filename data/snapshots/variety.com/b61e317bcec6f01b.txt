Adil El Arbi and Bilall Fallah, the Belgian directing duo behind 2020's smash hit sequel "Bad Boys for Life" starring Will Smith and Martin Lawrence, have invested in award-winning production company "Caviar."
The two have taken a minority stake in the fellow Belgian outfit, joining as shareholders through their banner Los Morros.
Over the last 20 years Caviar, also based in Belgium, has worked in both the scripted, non-scripted and branded space, and is best known for "Sound of Metal," which was nominated for six Oscars in 2020, including best film, winning best sound and best film editing. Other projects include Zach Woods' upcoming "The Accompanist," which premiered at Tribeca, and "The Best Immigrant," exec produced by both Arbi and Fallah. The company currently generates around 200 million Euros ($219 million) in annual revenue and aims to double that in the coming years.
Arbi and Fallah have a long history with Caviar, which produced their breakthrough feature "Black." Since then, the pair directing "Bad Boys for Life" ($426M worldwide) and its sequel "Bad Boys Ride or Die" ($405M), plus "Ms. Marvel" for Disney+, "Snowfall" for FX and "Rebel," which premiered in Cannes. They also directed "Batgirl," which was controversially shelved while in post-production at Warner Bros. as part of David Zaslav's cost-cutting.
As shareholders at Caviar, Arbi and Fallah will be involved as creative producers on select projects, while remaining independent directors and creators. They are part of Caviar's commercial directing roster and are represented by Entertainment 360, CAA and Ken Lambrechts Artists Management.
"More Belgian companies should think ambitiously and scale globally, like we do. We're proud to support Caviar on this journey while continuing our own work as directors," said Arbi, with Fallah adding: "Caviar shows that creativity and scale can grow together. We want to help keep that momentum going."
Added Caviar CEO Bert Hamelinck: "Caviar is and remains a talent-driven production company. Welcoming Adil and Bilall strengthens our creative core and supports our growth strategy. We're delighted to take this step together."
The investment is made through Los Morros BV, Arbi and Fallah's Belgian production company. Financial terms were not disclosed.
