On the opening night of his 30-concert residency at Madison Square Garden, pop superstar Harry Styles paid tribute to the late, great Dolly Parton. And at the tail end of Saturday night's show, he shouted out his ex-girlfriend Taylor Swift's wedding to Travis Kelce.
"We're at Madison Square Garden, home of the world champion New York Knicks!" Styles announced to loud applause, before adding, "And they also do weddings."
Styles famously dated Swift from November 2012 to January 2013 and served as the inspiration for Swift's chart-topping 2014 song "Style." According to Variety, Styles even "received an invitation" to Swift's wedding to NFL star Travis Kelce on July 3 at Madison Square Garden, but sources said that "Styles is currently on tour and will not make it to MSG." However, Styles' fiancé, Zoe Kravitz, did end up attending the Swift-Kelce mega-wedding.

There appears to be no bad blood between the two exes. Styles even gave a cheeky nod to Swift's wedding during his record-breaking Wembley residency on July 3, performing his 2017 song "Two Ghosts," which is believed to be his response to her tune "Style," as he sings: "Same lips red, same eyes blue / Same white shirt, couple more tattoos."
