No, Channing Tatum is not running an Instagram burner account to troll his ex Zoë Kravitz - but he does find the idea pretty funny.
"Yo I truly can't believe y'all think this is what I'm doing with my free time," Tatum wrote on his Instagram Story on Monday night after days of fan speculation. "Fuckin hilarious."
Tatum's response comes after rumors circulated that he may be behind the Instagram account @bidness_nonya, which fans noticed had been leaving snarky comments on posts about Kravitz and her fiancé Harry Styles. Things escalated when a screenshot allegedly showed Tatum as the account's only follower, leading to the "Magic Mike" actor's statement.
"Also, why would I follow a burner account if I didn't want people to connect me to it?" Tatum continued in his Story post. "Wtf come on. But if I really need to say it, I only have one IG account @channingtatum."
Popular on Variety
Some fans have floated the theory that @bidness_nonya could be a former Tatum fan account that changed its name, hence explaining Tatum's follow.
Tatum and Kravitz started dating in 2021 and got engaged in 2023 before ending their relationship the next year. Kravitz was first linked to pop star Styles in August 2025, and in April of this year it was reported that the two were engaged.
As it turns out, Tatum doesn't seem to have much free time, with several high-profile projects on his docket. He's been busy promoting his critically acclaimed indie film "Josephine" - which is set to hit theaters on Nov. 20 - and also has blockbuster "Avengers: Doomsday" coming up in December, in which he reprises his "Deadpool & Wolverine" character Gambit. Plus, he's in production on "Isle of Man," a drama about the annual motorcycling race event that he's also producing alongside Brad Pitt.
