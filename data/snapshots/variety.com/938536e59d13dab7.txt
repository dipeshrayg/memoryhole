Hallmark Media is gearing up for new expansion into podcasting - aiming to add another vector for connecting with its fanbase.
The media company in recent years has stepped up its brand extensions, from cruises and live tours to movies and series centered on heartwarming storylines and holiday themes. Now it wants to engage with fans through a new slate of podcast programming.
Hallmark next week will premiere "Hallmark Moments With Melissa Peterman," a video podcast featuring "authentic conversations" between the TV personality and stars from the Hallmark family - and beyond - that "break the fourth wall," the company said. The video version of the podcast will be available on YouTube, with the audio version available on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and other podcast platforms.
Peterman has starred in Hallmark's "Haul Out the Holly" franchise and NBC sitcom "Happy Place." The first episode of her podcast, dropping Monday, Nov. 30, features her convo with guest Lacey Chabert, another featured star of Hallmark productions. New episodes will debut every Monday through Feb. 8, 2027.
Additional guests on the podcast are scheduled to include Sharon Lawrence (2025's "A Grand Ole Opry Christmas") on Dec. 7; Jonathan Bennett ("Save the Date for Christmas") on Dec. 14; and B.J. Britt ("Hearts All Aglow") and Andrew Walker ("Christmas in Blue Dog Valley") on Dec. 21, to be filmed on-site during the Hallmark Christmas Experience this weekend.
A Hallmark Media rep says more original podcasts are in the works, as Peterman's show signals the company's "reentry" into the podcast space.
The new podcast series "brings Hallmark's signature joy, heart and connection to a format that feels fresh, lighthearted and relatable," according to a company rep. In addition, the show is designed to bolster the original content lineup on Hallmark+ streaming service.

"Hallmark Moments With Melissa" is produced in partnership with Daylight Media. Peterman serves as an executive producer.
"Hallmark has truly become family to me, so I jumped at the chance to executive produce and host this podcast," Peterman said in a statement provided to Variety. "I have so much fun with the Hallmark family so it just felt right to set up a microphone and let the fans listen. It's a chance to get to know another side of your favorite stars and see their heart and passion beyond the resume. No topic is off limits and we laugh a lot!"
"Hallmark is about the moments that connect us, and this podcast brings that spirit to life," said Michelle Vicary, head of programming at Hallmark Media. "Melissa Peterman has a gift for making people feel at home, so we hope fans listen and feel like they're right there at the table, hearing the stories behind the stars they love."
News of the podcast initiative comes ahead of Peterman's appearance at the Hallmark Christmas Experience this weekend in Kansas City, where she will co-host the upcoming one-hour holiday special, "The Hallmark Christmas Experience: A Hometown Holiday" and record the podcast episode with B.J. Britt and Andrew Walker.
