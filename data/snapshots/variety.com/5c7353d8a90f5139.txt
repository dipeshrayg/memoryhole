Sandra Hüller will receive the Spotlight Award for her performances in films including "Digger" and "Project Hail Mary" at this year's Palm Springs International Film Festival, which runs from Jan. 8 to Jan. 18, 2027. The awards will take place on Jan. 7.
"Sandra Hüller has an extraordinary ability to disappear into a character while bringing something entirely her own to every performance," said festival chairman Nachhattar Singh Chandi. "This year, she commanded the screen in four different films: 'Digger,' 'Fatherland,' 'Project Hail Mary' and 'Rose,' showcasing her remarkable range and depth across genres. It is a privilege to celebrate this exceptional year of work with the Spotlight Award, Actress."
Hüller appears in four diverse films this year. Directed by Phil Lord and Christopher Miller, she stars opposite Ryan Gosling in "Project Hail Mary" as the mission controller Eva Stratt. In "Digger," she joins an ensemble alongside Tom Cruise, Riz Ahmed and John Goodman. She also stars in Paweł Pawlikowski's "Fatherland," which premiered at the Cannes Film Festival. She portrays Erika Mann as she drives her father, Thomas Mann (Hanns Zischler), across newly divided postwar Germany in 1949. In Markus Schleinzer's "Rose," Hüller plays a 17th-century war veteran living as a man.
The Academy Award-winning actress joins this year's previously announced honorees, including Andrew Garfield, Seth Rogen, Olivia Wilde, Penélope Cruz, Edward Norton, "La Bola Negra" directors Javier Calvo and Javier Ambrossi, as well as actors Guitarricadelafuente, Miguel Bernardeau, Martin McDonagh, Inde Navarrette, Matt Damon, John Leguizamo, Samantha Morton, "The Odyssey" casting director John Papsidera and Andrew Scott.
Past recipients of the Spotlight Award include Amy Adams, Jessica Chastain, Danielle Brooks, Helen Hunt, Allison Janney, Julia Roberts, Kristen Stewart and Yuh-Jung Youn.
