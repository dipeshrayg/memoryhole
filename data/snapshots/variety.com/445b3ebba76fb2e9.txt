Grammy-nominated rapper GloRilla will make her acting debut in the upcoming movie "Don't Open the Door."
Armani Greer ("Why Did I Get Married Again?" "The Drop: A Snowfall Saga") is confirmed to star in the film, described as an "elevated horror-thriller" from writer-director Deon Taylor ("Traffik," "Black and Blue," "The Intruder," "Meet the Blacks"). The project is the latest film from Deon Taylor and Roxanne Avent Taylor's independent studio, Hidden Empire Film Group.
"Hidden Empire was built on taking the bolder swing, and this cast is our clearest example yet," said Taylor, announcing the two major castings. "Pairing Armani with GloRilla in her acting debut gave us something audiences haven't seen before. You can't fake that kind of energy, and I can't wait for people to feel it."
Greer and GloRilla (aka Gloria Hallelujah Woods) are the latest to join the cast alongside Tyrese Gibson (the "Fast & Furious" and "Transformers" franchises), Ron Livingston ("The Conjuring," "Office Space"), Beverly D'Angelo ("National Lampoon's Vacation" franchise, "American History X") and Omar Benson Miller ("Sinners," "Ballers").
Production is currently underway in Mississippi, with Maxime Alexandre ("The Nun," "Annabelle: Creation," "Crawl") on board as the film's cinematographer. Plot details are being kept under wraps, with filmmakers only revealing that the film "focuses on real-world trauma." The "Don't Open the Door" movie is also intended to launch a graphic novel-inspired universe that pushes the story beyond the screen.
To produce the film, HEFG is partnering with NFL star Cameron 'Cam' Heyward of the Pittsburgh Steelers and Oscar-nominated producer Shannon McIntosh, known for her longtime collaboration with Quentin Tarantino on films like "Once Upon a Time in Hollywood" and "The Hateful Eight." McIntosh is producing through her McIntosh Films banner and Heyward via his Iron 97 Productions label.
Since her debut in 2019, GloRilla has become one of hip-hop's most dynamic new stars, lauded especially for her distinct Memphis style and flow. After breaking out with the Grammy-nominated track "F.N.F. (Let's Go)," and under the guidance of CMG founder Yo Gotti, Glo signed with CMG and Interscope Records in 2022. In 2024, she released her debut album "Glorious," which notched the biggest first-week sales for a female rapper on the Billboard 200 that year, and ultimately earned three more Grammy nominations, bringing her total tally to six. Her latest single, "Jiggly Bow Wow," featuring Sexyy Red, debuted earlier this month.
On the business side, GloRilla recently launched GloVerse, an on-demand beauty marketplace and mobile app, designed to connect consumers with trusted, culturally fluent beauty professionals. She is represented by The Team, Paradigm and the Lede Company.
Greer is a rising talent who, most recently, delivered a standout performance as Jasmine in Tyler Perry's "Why Did I Get Married Again?" Her credits also include "The Drop: A Snowfall Saga," "72 Hours" and "The Waterboyz." In "Don't Open the Door," filmmakers say she "brings a compelling combination of emotional depth, intensity and authenticity to the film's central role, anchoring the story while bringing a fresh perspective" to the project. Greer is represented by The Talent Connect and LaPolt Law and Strategic Public Relations.
Deon Taylor, Roxanne Avent Taylor and Hidden Empire Film Group are represented by CAA.
