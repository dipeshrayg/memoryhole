The American Film Institute established its first full-ride scholarship dedicated to production design.
The AFI World Builder Scholarship for Production Design will be awarded to an admitted fall 2027 Production Design Fellow and cover tuition across the four-semester, full-time program.
"The AFI Conservatory has championed visual storytellers and worldbuilders for nearly 60 years, empowering thousands of filmmakers to express their unique vision," said Susan Ruskin, Dean of the AFI Conservatory. "We're thrilled to expand our scholarship opportunities with the first-ever AFI World Builder Scholarship for Production Design, highlighting the invaluable artistic contribution of production designers who create stunning visuals and transport audiences to new worlds."
The AFI Conservatory hosted a conversation with production design alumni as part of the announcement. Tom Hammock (AFI Class of 2004, "Weapons," "Resident Evil") and Claudia Ethridge (AFI Class of 2018, "Euphoria," "The SpongeBob Movie: Search for SquarePants") discussed why they pursued production design as a career, their experience at the Conservatory and their advice for aspiring filmmakers.
"It really is this amazing place where independent filmmaking and studio filmmaking come together and prepare you to take either path and go off into the world and really try and create worlds for films to take place in," Hammock said.
Said Ethridge, "I felt very confident stepping into the film industry knowing that I went to AFI and I had created this background there that I could pull from in whatever job that I was working on."
The AFI Conservatory offers a two-year MFA in production design to prepare fellows for professional careers. Fellows collaborate on projects, design a thesis production and build a professional portfolio while in the program.
The priority deadline to apply for the AFI World Builder Scholarship for Production Design is Dec. 1. Further information can be found on the AFI website.
