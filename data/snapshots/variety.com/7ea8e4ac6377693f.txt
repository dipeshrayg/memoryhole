After Natalia Silva defended Wang Cong in an unanimous decision to win the UFC Women's Flyweight Championship during UFC 332 in early October, the Ultimate Fighting Championship travels from Salt Lake City to Abu Dhabi for UFC 333.
For the main event, Australian Alexander Volkanovski (28-4-0) defends his UFC Featherweight Championship belt against Russian fighter Movsar Evloev (20-0-0) in a five-round title match on Saturday, Oct. 24.
The UFC 333: Volkanovski vs. Evloev event takes place at Etihad Arena in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates. The event starts at 10 a.m. ET/7 a.m. PT, while the main card is set to begin at 2 p.m. ET/11 a.m. PT.
For the main event, Volkanovski is considered the slight favorite to win retain the title because of his striking style and powerful technique. However, Movsar Evloev, as the minor underdog, has the ability to lay down tough grapples and strikes with a background in wrestling, so there's a chance he could upset to claim victory for himself.
