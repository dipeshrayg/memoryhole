Propagate Content, the production and talent powerhouse founded by Ben Silverman and Howard T. Owens, and The Natural Studios have launched "Adventure Garage With Bear Grylls," an eight-episode adventure series featuring survivalist Bear Grylls and "Top Gear's" Rutledge Wood.
In the new series, Grylls and Wood transform ordinary vehicles into extreme adventure machines, then push them to their limits in wild and unpredictable missions across some of the world's most rugged terrain.
The series is produced by The Natural Studios (a Banijay joint venture), Banijay's Workerbee, Sovereign's Capital and Tablehouse.

Ben Simms serves as showrunner and executive producer, with Liz Schulze as executive producer. Executive producers include Grylls, Delbert Shoopman and Ben Simon.
Propagate is bringing the series to market. The company's track record of unscripted hits includes Food Network's "Chopped" franchise and Netflix's Emmy-nominated "Untold" franchise.
The show reunites Propagate with Grylls following the success of "Running Wild With Bear Grylls," the long-running adventure series where Grylls took celebrities on supervised wilderness expeditions. The series has run for multiple seasons across NBC, National Geographic and Fox.
Grylls is represented by UTA.
