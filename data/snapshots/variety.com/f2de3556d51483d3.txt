Michael Urie, the two-time Emmy nominee, will host the 79th annual Writers Guild Awards New York ceremony on Saturday, Feb. 13, 2027, Variety has learned exclusively.
Urie served as a presenter at the 2026 New York ceremony and clearly made an impression on the guild's leadership.
"Michael was an absolute delight as a presenter at the 2026 Writers Guild Awards New York Ceremony, and I can't wait to see what he has in store as this year's host," says Tom Fontana, president of the Writers Guild of America East and the veteran showrunner behind HBO's "Oz" and NBC's "Homicide: Life on the Street."
Urie broke through as the deliciously acerbic fashion assistant Marc St. James on ABC's dramedy series "Ugly Betty," itself a Writers Guild Award winner and has spent the years since stealing scenes on comedies including "Modern Family," "Younger," "Workaholics," "Hot in Cleveland" and "Night Court," plus legal dramas "The Good Wife" and "The Good Fight."
These days, he's doing some of the best work of his career on "Shrinking," the Apple TV comedy series about a grieving therapist who starts breaking the rules. As Brian, the lovable best friend of Jason Segel's Jimmy, Urie earned back-to-back Emmy nominations for supporting actor in a comedy series in 2025 and 2026, competing both years alongside co-star Harrison Ford.
Urie was recently announced as joining the next season of the HBO comedy series "Rooster," and in the spring, he returns to Broadway in "Lost Lear," opposite fellow Juilliard alum Jessica Chastain and Corey Stoll.
The New York ceremony, the East Coast half the guild's annual bicoastal celebration, lands in the thick of Oscar season, when the WGA's screenplay winners are read as tea leaves for the Academy's writing races.
Behind the scenes, Ann Cohen returns as head writer of the 2027 New York show. Bonnie Datt and Shannon Walker serve as executive producers, with Marsha Seeman and Caroline Waxler producing.
