Outlook and Strategic Assumptions
- Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance raised to $5.20-$5.80, reflecting lower interest expense from debt reduction and a modest net benefit from tariff refunds.
- Management anticipates adjusted gross margins will reach the 34% to 35% range in the second half of the year, driven by productivity and shifting production from China to North America.
- Guidance assumes new Section 301 tariffs will be implemented at levels similar to previous rates, neutralizing temporary tailwinds from lower interim rates.
- Persistent inflationary pressures in battery metals, tungsten, and oil are expected to necessitate a price increase by 2027 to maintain the margin trajectory.
- The company remains on track for a net debt to adjusted EBITDA ratio of approximately 2.5x by year-end, prioritizing debt reduction and opportunistic share repurchases.
Non-Recurring Items and Risk Factors
- Second quarter results included a $0.17 net EPS benefit from Phase 1 tariff refunds, which was partially offset by variable compensation and growth investments.
- The divestiture of the Aerospace Fasteners business enabled a $1.7 billion debt reduction and $250 million in share repurchases during the quarter.
- Outdoor organic revenue decreased 7% due to weather-related demand softness and fewer replenishment orders.
- Management explicitly excluded potential second-half tariff refunds from guidance due to high uncertainty regarding timing and amounts.
