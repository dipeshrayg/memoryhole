US stocks were mixed on Tuesday, with the Nasdaq Composite (^IXIC) notching a second record high close in a row as oil prices eased. Speaking to the UN General Assembly, President Trump stated that a US-Iran deal would occur after the US midterm elections in November.
The S&P 500 (^GSPC) was unchanged, while the Nasdaq increased 0.4%. Meanwhile, the Dow Jones Industrial Average (^DJI) fell 0.3%.
Stocks kicked off the week with strong gains as confidence in artificial intelligence and falling oil prices spurred a rally on Wall Street.
US and Chinese officials wrapped up talks ahead of a capstone meeting between President Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called the preliminary talks "very successful," lifting hopes of cooperation on artificial intelligence.
Oil prices sank on Tuesday after Trump gave little new information on a breakthrough in the US-Iran conflict. Earlier in the week, reports surfaced that Iran may reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days if the US takes steps to ease military pressure. Brent crude futures (BZ=F) and WTI crude futures (CL=F) fell 3%.
Meta (META) shares slipped slightly after popping 11% on Monday as the company's Muse AI agent app reached the top spot on the App Store, sending a bullish signal for chip demand.
Shares of AutoZone (AZO) rose after the auto parts retailer reported earnings earlier Tuesday morning, beating profit expectations while missing revenue forecasts. KB Home (KBH) also reports quarterly results today amid a light week for economic data and earnings.
