Security Council LIVE: Deadly gang attacks spike in Haiti
Following a deadly spate of gang attacks on civilians in Haiti and amid reports of illicit weapons slipping into the Caribbean country, the Security Council has called an emergency meeting at 3pm (local time) to hear and discuss the latest developments. UN News app users can follow our coverage here.
What you need to know:
- On 23 August, gangs killed at least 47 people, injured 22 others and kidnapped more than 52 people in Kenscoff, near the capital, Port-au-Prince
- 1,408 people have been killed and 656 injured in the context of gang-related violence and 326 people had been subjected to sexual violence between 1 April and 30 June, according to the latest report of the UN Integrated Office in Haiti (BINUH)
- Illicit flows of firearms and ammunition into Haiti continued to bolster and empower armed gangs, according to the UN Human Rights Office (OHCHR)
- Reports indicate a gang operating in Port-au-Prince displayed on its social media account this week what seemed to be a new shipment of weapons arriving from abroad, despite the UN Security Council arms embargo on Haiti, in place since 2022
- To address gang violence in Haiti, the Security Council authorised the creation of a multinational security support mission in 2023 and a new gang suppression force in 2025
