Anthony Joshua dedicates knockout victory to friends who died in car crash
- Anthony Joshua secured a knockout victory against Kristian Prenga in his boxing comeback fight in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia.
- It came after Joshua was knocked down twice in the first round by the lesser-known Albanian opponent.
- He recovered in the second round, delivering a barrage of blows that sent Prenga to the canvas, leading to the referee stopping the fight.
- The win, Joshua's 30th in 34 fights, is expected to pave the way for a highly anticipated 'Battle of Britain' against Tyson Fury later this year.
- An emotional Joshua dedicated the win to two close friends, Sina Ghami and Latif Ayodele, who died in a car crash in December.
