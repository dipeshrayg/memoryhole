Chechen warlord Kadyrov wins election 'nearly 100% of vote'
Chechen warlord Ramzan Kadyrov has been re-elected to a fifth term, with almost 100 percent of the vote.
A close ally to Vladimir Putin, Kadyrov has been allowed to run the southern, mainly Muslim region of Chechnya, as a criminal state.
Human rights organisations have long accused his brutal regime of torture, enforced disappearances, and extrajudicial killings.
Russia's Central Electoral Commission said Kadyrov took 99.85 percent of the vote in Chechnya, slightly up from 99.73 percent in 2021.
International observers have in the past sharply criticised the conduct of elections in the small mountainous region.
Kadyrov, who rejects such criticism as Western-backed attempts to undermine his rule, regularly stresses his personal loyalty to Putin and has championed a hard line on Ukraine, where he says his Chechen forces have been fighting since 2022.
