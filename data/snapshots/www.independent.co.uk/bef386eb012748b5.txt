Violent fare-dodger laughs after leaving police officer bloodied in attack
A London fare dodger laughed in a police interview after punching the officer who challenged him so hard in the face that teeth marks were left on his fist.
Shemar Robinson, 24, of no fixed address, was confronted as he pushed through ticket barriers at Dollis Hill Station on Thursday, 19 November.
He reacted by attacking a police officer, who required hospital treatment including stitches.
Robinson was arrested at the scene on suspicion of causing grievous bodily harm, assault of an emergency worker and possession of a class B drug.
When interviewed, Robinson laughed and said he was deliberately trying to hurt the officer and had "no remorse." He added: "I should have hit him harder".
Robinson pleaded guilty to GBH, possession of a class B drug and fare evasion at Willesden Magistrates' Court on Friday, 20 November. He was remanded in custody and will be sentenced at a later date.
