US warned Russia about planned terror attack on Moscow
- The US Embassy in Moscow issued an urgent alert warning Russian authorities of an imminent terror threat targeting the Tsaritsyno Museum Reserve in the capital.
- Russian domestic security agency the FSB confirmed receipt of the intelligence and evacuated staff and visitors from the historic estate.
- Neither US nor Russian officials disclosed specific details regarding the suspects or operational motives behind the alleged plot.
- The intelligence sharing comes amid a warming relationship between US President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin following a recent diesel supply deal.
- The incident occurred shortly before planned trilateral peace talks between Russia, Ukraine, and the US in Abu Dhabi.
