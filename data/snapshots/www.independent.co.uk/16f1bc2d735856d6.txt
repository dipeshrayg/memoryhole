Girl, 16, was stabbed to death by 'jealous and obsessed' love rival
- Eighteen-year-old Kayla Smith has pleaded guilty to murder at Leeds Crown Court after fatally stabbing 16-year-old Chloe Watson Dransfield five times in the neck and back during a fight over a boy in Leeds.
- Smith initially admitted to manslaughter but changed her plea to murder on the second day of her trial, with Mr Justice Cavanagh KC warning her that she faces a mandatory sentence of life imprisonment.
- The court heard that Smith was jealous and obsessed over her boyfriend Blake Dowling's relationship with Chloe, having threatened to stab Chloe in the throat during a phone call shortly before the attack.
- Dowling booked a taxi to take Chloe to Smith's home, where CCTV captured Smith emerging with a knife, leaving Chloe fatally injured; an ambulance was not called until 37 minutes after the stabbing.
- The trial continues for two co-defendants, Blake Dowling, 18, and Archie Rycroft, 20, who allegedly filmed the fight, as both deny charges of murder and manslaughter.
