Morrisons Christmas advert reimagines Goldilocks and the Three Bears
Morrisons has released its 2026 Christmas advert reimagining Goldilocks and the Three Bears .
The advert follows parents Mummy and Daddy Bear's frantic preparations after a last-minute text from Baby Bear which reads: "Home for Xmas! Bringing someone xx".
Daddy Bear heads to Morrisons for the food shop and Mummy Bear decorates the tree.
But the biggest surprise comes when Baby bear arrives home with his new girlfriend, Goldilocks.
