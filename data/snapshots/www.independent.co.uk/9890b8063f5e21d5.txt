Trump resorts to body-shaming after hecklers interrupt rally at least 7 times
- President Donald Trump headlined a rally in San Antonio, Texas, to boost the campaign of Republican Senate candidate Ken Paxton ahead of the midterm elections.
- The event was repeatedly interrupted by demonstrators, prompting Trump to mock the hecklers and accuse them of being paid protesters.
- "Now I'm telling you they pay them for this. But do we have a good time?" Trump said, before pursing his lips. Interrupted again, Trump lashed out: "That guy's got to lose a lot of weight." At one point, Trump appeared to become so frustrated he made a direct appeal to any further opponents in the crowd. "If there are any more paid protesters, please raise your hand and get the hell out of here," he said.
- Trump praised Paxton while launching personal attacks against Democratic opponent James Talarico during his address.
- The visit marked Trump's third trip to Texas in less than a month as Republicans invest heavily to defend the state in a competitive Senate race. During his own speech, Paxton made a brief verbal blunder when he began asking the crowd for deliverance from capitalism before correcting himself to communism.
