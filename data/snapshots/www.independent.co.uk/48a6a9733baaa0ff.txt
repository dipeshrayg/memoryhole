George Russell downplays Max Verstappen feud ahead of Qatar Grand Prix
- George Russell has brushed off his ongoing feud with Max Verstappen following their on-track contact and verbal spat at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.
- Verstappen won the race in Las Vegas while Russell finished ninth after crashing into a barrier late on while trying to overtake his teammate.
- Speaking ahead of the Qatar Grand Prix, Russell labelled Verstappen's critical post-race comments as "amusing" and praised his rival's performance.
- The Mercedes driver admitted that his "risk dial" is currently set too high as he attempts to secure major results before the season ends.
- Russell sits fourth in the drivers' championship standings behind Verstappen and Lewis Hamilton with just two races remaining in Qatar and Abu Dhabi.
