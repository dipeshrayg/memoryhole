Xabi Alonso explains how Morgan Rogers and Cole Palmer can shine together at Chelsea: 'I have a plan'
Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso has lauded new signing Morgan Rogers as an "ideal" acquisition, predicting an exciting midfield partnership with Cole Palmer.
The England international, who registered an assist for Anthony Gordon in the World Cup semi-final defeat by Argentina earlier this month, joined the west London club last week from Aston Villa on a contract extending until 2033.
The reported £117 million transfer fee makes the 24-year-old the most expensive British player in history, a significant investment for a club looking to rebuild after finishing 10th in the Premier League last season.
Speaking ahead of Chelsea's pre-season friendly against Western Sydney Wanderers, the 44-year-old manager stated: "In that position, we needed an important player, and I'm sure that there were not many more better options than Morgan. It means a lot for everyone."
DONE DEAL! Manchester United sign new goalkeeper
Manchester United have announced the signing of young goalkeeper Kit Margetson from Swansea.
The 19-year-old did not make a senior appearance for the Swans but went on loan to Welsh Premier League side Connah's Quay Nomads last season, where he kept 10 clean sheets in 34 appearances.
The Wales youth keeper, the son of former international Martyn Margetson, made his Under-21s debut for his nation against Belarus in March.
Margetson will move to Old Trafford subject to registration, after the two clubs agreed a compensation package.

Andrea Pirlo to Italy off after Russian betting link
Andrea Pirlo is no longer in line to coach crisis-hit Italy due to political concerns over a sponsorship deal with a Russian betting firm.
Pirlo himself confirmed that he was out in an Instagram post Monday, saying he "heard last night that I'm no longer the candidate for the Italy national team job."
Several Italian politicians had raised concerns about Pirlo's contract to be an ambassador for Fonbet, saying the national team coach should not have conflicting views on Italy's support for Ukraine in the war with Russia.
World Cup final referee Slavko Vincic retires days after controversial Spain vs Argentina clash
Slavko Vincic, the referee who officiated the recent World Cup final, has announced his retirement just a week after the tournament's conclusion.
The Slovenian official faced widespread criticism for his handling of Argentina's physical approach during their 1-0 extra-time defeat to Spain, a match in which he sent off Chelsea midfielder Enzo Fernandez in stoppage time.
