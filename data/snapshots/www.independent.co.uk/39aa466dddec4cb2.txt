Passengers stranded as flights suspended at Bristol Airport
- Bristol Airport suspended all flights on Friday evening due to a defect on its runway, leaving passengers stranded.
- The airport announced that operations would remain closed until at least 8am on Saturday to allow for assessment and repair of the issue.
- Numerous flights were delayed, including early morning services to Paris and Faro.
- Passenger Paul Smith expressed frustration over the lack of communication and support from the airport and airlines.
- Airlines, such as Jet2, said that their flights were delayed rather than cancelled, awaiting the runway's reopening.
