Frugal readers, your time has come. Black Friday 2026 is here, and from now until Cyber Monday (30 November), the likes of Apple, Ninja, Boots, John Lewis, Argos, Amazon, Zara and hundreds more are delivering some of their biggest offers of the year. We've spotted some jaw-dropping discounts already today, but some of the hottest offers have already sold out.
With hundreds of misleading discounts out there, identifying the actual bargains among the duds is no mean feat. That's where The Independent's crack team of consumer experts and I come in. With decades of Black Friday experience between us, we know how to spot a genuine Black Friday deal, scrutinising historical prices, comparing retailers and drawing on our extensive product testing experience to bring you the very best savings.
From record-low prices on Apple's AirPods Pro to hefty savings on Dyson appliances, Nintendo Switch 2 bundles and beauty advent calendars, we'll be bringing you the best offers live as they drop. Look out for our 'cheapest price' sticker, awarded to products we've independently verified are at their lowest-ever recorded price. Gladiators, ready? Let's go bargain hunting.

Is Lush doing Black Friday this year? Here's what you need to know
Home to bath bombs, shower gels and some sweet-smelling gift sets, Lush is a hot spot for all things self-care. But I'm afraid you won't find any savings at the retailer this Black Friday.
The cosmetics brand is continuing its tradition of shunning the sales in favour of raising money for charity. You can't knock it.
From today until Cyber Monday (30 November), Lush shops across the UK will be fundraising for charity with the brand's Charity Pot body lotion (£11, Lush.com).
Lush says that 75 per cent of the price of the body lotion will go towards grassroots organisations working to protect human rights, animals and the environment.
.There is one nice perk for shoppers, though (beyond rubbing your conscience, I mean). Over the Black Friday weekend, Lush Club members will earn double points on any purchase that includes a pot of its Charity Pot body lotion. Win-win.
