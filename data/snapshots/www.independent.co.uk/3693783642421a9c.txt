I'm a Celebrity 2026: What we know so far from rumoured line-up to start date
- I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! is returning for its 26th series this autumn, with Ant McPartlin and Dec Donnelly hosting from Australia.
- Paralympian Ade Adepitan is rumoured to be joining the cast, potentially becoming the first wheelchair user to compete on the show.
- High-profile figures reportedly heading to the jungle include former footballer Wayne Rooney, Gavin & Stacey star Joanna Page, and Kaiser Chiefs frontman Ricky Wilson.
- Television presenters and personalities including AJ Odudu, Chloe Burrows, Claire Sweeney, and Mariella Frostrup are also tipped to participate.
- The rumoured line-up is completed by comedian Leigh Francis, model Leomie Anderson, YouTube creator Sharky, and stand-up comedian Jack Skipper. The show is rumoured to start on or around November 15.
