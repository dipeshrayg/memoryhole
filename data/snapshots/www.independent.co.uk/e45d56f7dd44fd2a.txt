How to save on Hyperoptic broadband
There are several ways to cut the cost of your Hyperoptic broadband, even without a promo code. These include:
- New customer offers: Look for introductory discounts on selected broadband plans.
- Switching offers: Eligible customers can get up to £300 towards the cost of leaving their existing provider.
- Student discounts: Students can access reduced monthly prices on selected plans.
- Fair Fibre: Customers receiving certain benefits may qualify for significantly reduced broadband prices.
- Referral rewards: Existing customers can earn rewards when friends sign up through their referral link.
- Price matching: Hyperoptic may match a cheaper like-for-like broadband deal from another provider, subject to its terms.
Hyperoptic switching offer: get up to £300 in credit
If you're dissatisfied with your current contract with another broadband provider, you may be eligible for the 'switch now' promotion. This offers you £300 credit to cover exit fees if you decide to switch to Hyperoptic while mid-contract. Best of all, Hyperoptic will speak to your ex-provider to cancel your current contract and handle any administrative work.
This offer qualifies for 150MB, 500MB or 1GB broadband packages on a 24-month contract. You'll need to contact telesalesteam@hyperoptic.com with your final bill from your old provider to receive your credit.
Hyperoptic student discounts
Students can access exclusive broadband offers through Student Beans, including 12-month Fastfibre broadband from £16 a month or a 12-month broadband and phone plan from £19 a month. The prices and eligible packages can change, so check the current offer after signing in to your student account. You'll generally need to obtain the relevant code or offer through the student platform and confirm that it applies to your address and chosen contract.
Hyperoptic referral rewards
Existing customers can use Hyperoptic's referral scheme to earn a reward when an eligible friend signs up. The amount, reward type and qualifying conditions can change, so check the current referral terms before sharing your link. Make sure your referral completes the required steps before expecting a reward.
Price match guarantee
Hyperoptic is so confident in its value, that if you find the same package cheaper elsewhere, it'll match it, provided it meets its criteria. It can pay to double check the prices of competitors in your area, to see if you qualify for the Hyperoptic price match. The promise covers broadband or broadband and phone services, with the same service specifications at your residence.
To claim this offer, you'll need to contact Hyperoptic's customer service via phone, email or raise a ticket in your My Account within the first 30 days of service.
The Fair Fibre plan
Hyperoptic has a Fair Fibre plan, which offers discounted prices for those who need it most. Those on Universal Credit or other qualifying benefits could be eligible for fast 150Mb for just £13 a month, or superfast 1Gb for £20 a month.
Hyperoptic prices explained
Hyperoptic broadband prices typically start from around £19 per month for entry-level fibre plans, rising to £36+ for ultrafast 1Gbps speeds. Unlike many providers, Hyperoptic focuses on full-fibre connections, meaning faster and more reliable speeds.

Is Hyperoptic broadband worth it?
Hyperoptic is worth considering if you want full-fibre broadband with fast upload and download speeds. Its packages include options from 150Mb to 1Gb, while the Fair Fibre plan and monthly rolling contracts may appeal to customers who need a lower-cost or more flexible option.
There is one catch: Hyperoptic only serves selected properties, so you can't assume it's available in your town or postcode. Check your address first, then compare the speeds, contract terms and total cost against other providers that can connect your home.
Hyperoptic FAQs
Does Hyperoptic offer discount codes?
You can usually find a Hyperoptic promo code offering a gift card incentive for new customers. Most other savings come from discounted monthly pricing, bundle offers and limited-time deals on specific broadband packages.
Where can I apply my Hyperoptic promo code?
Hyperoptic enables you to apply your code before you check out. Look for the 'Do you have a promo code' text under the broadband plans section. You can also add a code later on, under the basket summary.
Is there a Hyperoptic NHS discount?
No, there is nothing directly offered through Hyperoptic. However, Health Service Discounts does run Hyperoptic promotions for NHS staff and keyworkers.
