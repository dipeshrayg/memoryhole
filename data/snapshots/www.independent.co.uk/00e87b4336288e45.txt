A hectic end to the summer transfer window could be in store as Premier League clubs scramble to get deals done ahead of Tuesday night's deadline.
Cody Gakpo is set to leave Liverpool and join Manchester City in a shock move with Bradley Barcola's switch from Paris Saint-Germain now confirmed. If Gakpo departs, the Reds could sign another winger, with previous interest in Ismaila Sarr from Crystal Palace having moved on from Brighton's Yankuba Minteh.
Atletico Madrid remain steadfast on keeping Julian Alvarez or moving him to a club that is not Barcelona - and Arsenal could yet make a late push for the Argentina striker.
Manchester City remain in talks to sign Enzo Fernandez from Chelsea, who brought in Emiliano Martinez over the weekend to solve their goalkeeping question. If Fernandez does leave, Chelsea may target a new midfielder, with previous interest in Bournemouth's Alex Scott.

Bradley Barcola's first words as a Liverpool player
"It's a great pride to be able to play for Liverpool. I'm very happy.
"It took a long time but I'm really happy today to be able to play for Liverpool and all I want is to be on the pitch and be able to play.
"I hope to achieve very big things. First of all, winning the Premier League - it's one of my biggest dreams in my life.
"It is to do that and to stay in this team in the long term - that's why I signed a long contract - and I hope I can do it.
"I had the chance to play twice in this stadium and I saw the atmosphere and how the fans were behind the Liverpool players.
"When I knew about Liverpool's interest in me, I didn't ask questions and I knew it was a very big club for me."
Liverpool complete statement signing of Bradley Barcola from PSG
Liverpool have completed the signing of Bradley Barcola from Paris Saint-Germain in a marquee addition to Andoni Iraola's squad.
The French forward arrives on a five-year deal for an initial fee of £106m, which could rise to £123m.
The 23-year-old has been a Liverpool target throughout this summer, and desired a move to Merseyside even while attracting interest from other clubs.
Barcola continues Liverpool's heavy investment over the past two years, with Alexander Isak and Florian Wirtz brought in for more than £100m last summer.
Capped 28 times by France, Barcola was part of the PSG side that won back-to-back Champions League crowns in 2025 and 2026, featuring off the bench in both finals.

Manchester City complete signing of £32m winger as spending spree continues
Allan Elias said it was surreal and the proudest moment of his life to join Manchester City after completing his £32m move to the FA Cup winners.
The uncapped Brazilian has signed a five-year deal with City, who could pay Palmeiras a further £2m in add-ons.
The 22-year-old becomes one of the replacements for Savinho and Omar Marmoush, who have gone to Tottenham in deals that could earn City up to £145m, and may be the first of two wingers recruited at the end of the transfer window.
Michael Carrick says Man Utd will 'wait and see' as transfer deadline looms
Michael Carrick admits Manchester United could be active before the transfer deadline and says Marcus Rashford feels like a new signing after the returning forward helped secure a 5-2 win against Ipswich.
Asked about potential transfer activity, United boss Carrick said: "It's always difficult to say, to be honest, when there's so little time left.
"I think the situation that we're in, I think the business that we've managed to do, I'm pleased with. I think we're a better group, a better squad, potentially a better team.
"Adding Marcus into that feels like a new signing, so in that respect we're quite happy.
"We're always open to it if there's something else to be able to add, so I wouldn't shut that down. But as it stands, we'll have to wait and see."

West Ham sign Viking forward
West Ham have signed Norwegian winger Edvin Austbo on a four-year contract with a further one-year option.
The 21-year-old, who joins from Viking FK, scored six goals and added seven assists last season in their Eliteserien-winning campaign, and was also named the Eliteserien Young Player of the Year.
Austbo said: "Obviously, the ambitions of the club are big and it's also for me as a player. The talks I had with Nuno and Nils Koppen were amazing and I just felt from the start that this was a good match and that I really wanted to come here.
"I watched English football on TV when I was a little kid. Everyone in Norway knows West Ham. It's such a big club and a club that belongs on the highest level in England."
Chelsea's Enzo Fernandez replacement?
Widespread reports suggest that Chelsea have identified Lamine Camara of Monaco as a possible midfield target to replace Enzo Fernandez, should the Argentine leave. The Senegal international is just 22 but already has plenty of experience in Ligue 1 and internationally.
Juventus 'lead chase' for Nick Woltemade
The striker merry-go-round could spin at quite the pace before tomorrow's deadline. The Telegraph reports that Juventus want Newcastle's Nick Woltemade, though a number of clubs are keen on a player who could be available.
