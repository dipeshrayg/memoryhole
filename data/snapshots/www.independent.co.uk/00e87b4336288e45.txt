Transfer deadline day has arrived with two of the biggest sagas of the summer still to be resolved: will Arsenal sign Julian Alvarez and could Enzo Fernandez be swapping Chelsea for Manchester City before tonight's 11pm deadline?
Premier League champions Arsenal have pursued Alvarez all window but are running out of time to submit an offer to Atletico Madrid, despite selling Gabriel Martinelli and Gabriel Jesus to free up space for the 26-year-old forward. It means the Gunners could be left short in attack if they do not meet Atletico's €150m asking price and so have explored alternatives - though they have turned down a possible deal for Lyon's Malick Fofana, who may now join Sunderland.
Manchester City, meanwhile, will need to submit a bid that is higher than £120m to sign Fernandez from Chelsea and complete their midfield rebuild. Fernandez has been left out of Chelsea's last two squads and Xabi Alonso's side will not sell the Argentina international without a suitable replacement, with Monaco's Lamine Camara, Roma's Manu Kone and Bournemouth's Alex Scott all on their radar.
Manchester United have still not signed a left back, while Liverpool's business appears done after signing Bradley Barcola and rejecting Manchester City's approach for Cody Gakpo. City have agreed a £60m deal with Everton to buy Iliman Ndiaye. That could have an impact on Everton's pursuit of Richarlison, with Tottenham still wanting to sign a new forward too.
Follow all of the latest news, rumours and done deals below
DONE DEAL! Chelsea sign Italian wonderkid Ahanor
Chelsea have agreed a deal to sign Italian international Honest Ahanor from Serie A side Atalanta.
The 18-year-old will not join the Blues until 2027, however, meaning the Italian club will loan the player out this season.
One for the future, Chelsea have spent around £40m on the defender.
Wrexham sign ex-Forest and Burnley defender Joe Worrall
Wrexham have signed defender Joe Worrall from Burnley, bringing an end to a frustrating two-year spell at Turf Moor where he struggled for appearances.
The 29-year-old helped Nottingham Forest earn promotion to the top flight in 2021/22 and will be hoping to rediscover his form at Wrexham.
He has signed a two-year deal for an undisclosed fee.
He said: "Everything that Wrexham's about, I'm going to throw myself into it head-first and give my all for this badge."
Everton to sign Maitland-Niles
Everton's busy day continues.
The Toffees have agreed a deal with Lyon for Ainsley Maitland-Niles.
The 29-year-old can play at full-back and in midfield.
Man City agree £60m deal for Iliman Ndiaye as Everton's pursuit of Jack Grealish progresses
Man City turned to Ndiaye after an approach for Cody Gakpo was rebuffed by Liverpool.
Fernandez to City dependent on replacement
The Independent's Chief Football Writer, Miguel Delaney, says that Enzo Fernandez's move to Manchester City is dependent on whether Chelsea can secure a guaranteed replacement before the deadline today.
The two options on the table for the Blues are Lamine Camara and Manu Kone of Monaco and Roma respectively.
Chelsea would need a purchase guaranteed for at least one of the midfielders before sanctioning the sale of Fernandez to City.
The Blues have seen a bid for Camara rejected at £38.5m while they continue to pursue Kone.
Monaco reject Chelsea bid of Camara
Earlier we brought you news that Chelsea had submitted a bid for Monaco midfielder Lamine Camara who is seen as a replacement for the probably departing Enzo Fernandez.
Now an update has been given by Sky Sports who say that the bid, believed to be £38.5m, has been rejected by the French club.
Chelsea are still in talks with Roma over a £52m deal for Manu Kone.
DONE DEAL! Villa sign Mbaye
Aston Villa have completed the signing of PSG's Ibrahim Mbaye.
The deal is reportedly worth around £47m for the 18-year-old winger.
Ipswich to sign Burnley's Flemming
This is a less flashy transfer story but Ipswich look on track to sign Zion Flemming from Burnley for around £17m on deadline day.
Flemming would bolster Ipswich's attacking line and Gary O'Neill has been in the market for a centre forward.
Flemming scored 11 goals for Burnley last season in the top-flight and will bring Premier League experience. He is travelling to Ipswich to complete a medical and the formalities.
What is does do is leave Burnley a bit short for their Championship campaign. The Clarets would have hoped to bounce straight back up but they've lost two and drawn one of their opening three matches and sit in the second division relegation zone.
Woltemade set for Newcastle exit
Nick Woltemade is set to leave Newcastle just one year after signing for a record £69m from Stuttgart.
The forward is flying to Turin to complete a medical at Juventus on deadline day ahead of a probable loan move.
The forward wanted to remain at Newcastle but isn't seen as crucial to Matthias Jaissle's plans and Juve are willing to stump up a £3.5m season-long loan fee.
Napoli, Borussia Dortmund and other Bundesliga clubs also wanted the 24-year-old on loan.
Arsenal cool interest in Malick Fofana
Arsenal have cooled their interest in Lyon's Malick Fofana, who has been a target for multiple clubs this summer, with Mikel Arteta not viewing the winger as worth the €60m fee the French club want.
But The Guardian reports the 21-year-old now looks set to join Sunderland for an initial £25m rising to £31m.
