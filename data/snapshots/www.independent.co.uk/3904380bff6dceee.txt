F1 heads to its first new circuit in three years as a street circuit in Madrid hosts the Spanish Grand Prix for the first time.
Kimi Antonelli is riding the highest of waves after his sensational victory from 19th on the grid in Monza. The Italian now has a 66-point lead over Mercedes teammate George Russell in second, with a scheduled 10 rounds remaining of the 2026 season.
Lewis Hamilton cut a frustrated figure post-race in Monza after his sixth-place finish and declared himself out of the title race. Nevertheless, he remains in third place, 76 points off Antonelli, and will be eyeing a return to top form this weekend. Max Verstappen completed the Monza podium, as he continues to chase his first victory in 2026.
Madrid's new street track, the Madring, has 22 corners and an iconic turn 12, christened 'La Monumental', with menacing steep banking. It will represent a home race for Carlos Sainz and Fernando Alonso and replaces Barcelona as the host of the Spanish GP on a 10-year deal.
