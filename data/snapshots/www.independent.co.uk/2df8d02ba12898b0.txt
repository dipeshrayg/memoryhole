Russell Brand has been found guilty of one count of rape and three sexual assaults following a dramatic four-week trial.
The comedian closed his eyes as he was told he had been convicted of raping a young fan after one of his shows in 2009.
He stood upright in the dock and showed limited emotion as the verdicts were delivered at Southwark Crown Court on Thursday.
Judge Mr Justice Bennathan told Brand the charges meant he faced: "a custodial sentence of some length."
The jury of eight men and four women took just under two days to return their verdicts.
He was convicted of carrying out the attacks during a period when he soared to "staggering fame", the court was previously told.
Judge tells Brand he believes he should be remanded in custody
Mr Justice Bennathan said: "You've been convicted of a number of offences including rape.
"The consequence of that is inevitably a significant custodial term.
"My current view is that I therefore have to remand you into custody."
Brand taken to cells ahead of bail arguments
Russell Brand has been taken down to the cells until 3pm, when arguments over bail will be made.
He showed no emotion and said loudly, "yes my lord" before was led to cells by dock officers.
Judge tells Brand he faces 'custodial sentence of some length'
After the verdict, Mr Justice Bennathan said: "Sit down Mr Brand.
"Mr Winter, Ms Karmy-Jones, just on what happens next given the verdict on count six at least, obviously a custodial sentence of some length will result."
The judge said the case was "very well prepared", adding the sentencing hearing would take place on Monday.
Which charges did jury find Brand guilty of?
Brand has been found not guilty of raping a woman he met at a cabaret show on the fringes of the 1999 Labour Party conference in Bournemouth.
The jury found him not guilty of trying to drag an MTV worker in to a toilet to indecently assault her in 2001.
He is guilty of groping another MTV worker during a party at a Soho bar in 2004, but not guilty of going on to orally rape her in a toilet.
Jurors found Brand guilty of sexually assaulting a Capital Radio worker after encountering her in a corridor in 2005.
He was found guilty of raping a 21-year-old fan after meeting her at a 2009 stand-up show at the Hammersmith Apollo.
And Brand was found guilty of sexually assaulting a Channel 4 worker during a forced massage in 2009.

Breaking: Jury reaches verdict in Russell Brand trial
The jury in the trial of Russell Brand has returned a verdict after almost two days of deliberations.
The comedian and actor, 51, is on trial at Southwark Crown Court and denies three charges of rape, three counts of sexual assault and one count of attempted indecent assault against six women, in allegations spanning 10 years from 1999 to 2009.
