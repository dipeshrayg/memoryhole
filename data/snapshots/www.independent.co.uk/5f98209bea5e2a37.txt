Thousands of discounts, dozens of retailers and still 22 days until Black Friday 2026. There's are already plenty of savings to sift through, with Superdrug, PlayStation and Samsung among the latest retailers to launch early offers. In fact, research commissioned by PayPal predicts Brits will spend a whopping £6.7bn across Black Friday and Cyber Monday this year. But with so many supposedly unmissable bargains appearing weeks before 27 November, how can you tell which are genuinely the best Black Friday deals?
That's where our IndyBest experts come in. Having covered Black Friday for more than a decade, we've built up our own archive of deals from previous years, allowing us to check whether today's discounts truly stack up against those we've seen before. We also use price-monitoring tools, compare offers across retailers and prioritise products we've personally tried and tested. For more info on why we're reliable sales correspondants, head to our guide on how The Independent's consumer experts choose Black Friday deals.
Of course, there's an argument for getting your Christmas shopping out of the way sooner rather than later. According to Ian Bendelow, senior analyst at The British Retail Consortium, "Black Friday is no longer confined to a single weekend, with many retailers offering deals throughout November. Shopping earlier gives customers more opportunity to find the products they want at a good price, particularly popular items which may sell out, while also allowing them to spread their spending ahead of Christmas."
Whether you're after laptops, mattresses, Ninja air fryers, coffee machines, skincare or perfume, we're back for a second day of live coverage, scrutinising the latest reductions and highlighting what's worth buying now - and what might be worth waiting for.
