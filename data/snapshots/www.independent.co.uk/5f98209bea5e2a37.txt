When is Black Friday 2026?
This year, Black Friday lands on 27 November. Black Friday always takes place the day after Thanksgiving in the US. That means the calendar date changes each year, but it will always fall on the same day of the week.
Of course, it's no longer just a one-day event - brands and retailers have turned Black Friday into a weeks-long shopping festival. Black Friday deals are already starting to roll in, with retailers such as AO, Currys and Boots launching their offers ahead of the main event. That means you have plenty of time to shop around and compare prices before the big day itself.
When does Black Friday end?
Black Friday ends on Cyber Monday, which is 30 November this year. Some of the biggest deals will disappear at midnight, like Cinderella in her pumpkin carriage. That said, last year, some retailers continued offering discounts well into December, with Lookfantastic extending its sale until 3 December last year. A number of retailers also launched "Cyber Week" promotions, extending the sale even further into the first week of December. It goes on and on.
When is Cyber Monday?
Cyber Monday is on 30 November 2026.
Cyber Monday is the online shopping event that follows Black Friday. It takes place on the Monday after Thanksgiving weekend in the US. It was originally created to encourage shoppers to buy online. But given that everyone shops on the internet, the concept of Cyber Monday is slightly redundant. It's now just an extension of Black Friday, giving you a final chance to save before Christmas.
Some brands and retailers have tried to popularise other shopping events, but most have had about as much success as Gretchen Wieners trying to make "fetch" happen. There's Travel Tuesday, for example, which takes place on the day after Cyber Monday and focuses on deals for flights, hotels and package holidays, and Small Business Saturday, which encourages shoppers to support independent retailers instead of the big chains.
How can I tell if a Black Friday deal is genuine?
The easiest way is to check a product's price history. If it's been the same price for weeks before Black Friday, or the discount is only a few pounds, it probably isn't as impressive as it looks. Make sure to write a list of everything you want and need, noting down the current price so you can check the discount in November. I also recommend comparing prices across multiple retailers. Just because something has a big percentage off doesn't automatically make it a good deal.
