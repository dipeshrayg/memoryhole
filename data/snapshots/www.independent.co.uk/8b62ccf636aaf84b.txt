17 people have been killed after a military transport aircraft crashed in the Russian region of Kemerovo on Tuesday.
The incident happened in southwestern Siberia near the village of Yagunovo and killed everyone on board, according to local news.
Details about the circumstances of the crash were not immediately available.
Local Governor Ilya Seredyuk reported a plane crash near the village of Yagunovo on his official channel on the MAX messaging platform, but did not identify the aircraft involved.
It is reported that the plane is an Antonov An-12.
