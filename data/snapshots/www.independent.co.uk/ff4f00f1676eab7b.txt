1. Sugar Rush 1000
Provider: Pragmatic Play
Sugar Rush 1000 heads a list ranked by RTP because it ties for the highest published figure of the six. It uses a 7x7 grid with a cluster pays mechanic, where winning clusters leave a marked spot and each further win on that spot doubles its multiplier, up to 1,024x. In the free spins, the multipliers stay put.
This game is the higher-ceiling follow-up to the original Sugar Rush, with the same RTP at 96.53%. The volatility is between high and very high (depending on sources), while the maximum win is 25,000x stake, making it a good choice for readers who want a higher win potential and more volatility on a familiar and popular game.
2. Sweet Bonanza 1000
Provider: Pragmatic Play
Sweet Bonanza 1000 is another Pragmatic Play title, and the 2024 sequel that lifts the original's top multiplier from 100x to 1,000x (you can find out more about where to play slots from the Sweet Bonanza studio, Pragmatic Play casinos with our guide.
Though the RTP is the same as Sugar Rush 1000, this is slightly less popular in terms of search demand, so we have placed it second.
The game uses a 6x5 grid with a 'pays anywhere' mechanic, where eight or more matching symbols anywhere on the grid win, with no paylines. It is a high volatility title with a 96.53% RTP and a 25,000x max win (though lower RTP builds (95.52%, 94.51%) exist, so check the info panel before playing).
Overall, the slot suits a reader who wants the Sweet Bonanza feel with a much higher ceiling, and those who are willing to accept long, quiet stretches.
3. Sugar Rush
Provider: Pragmatic Play
The Sugar Rush slot is the 2022 original in the series, the lowest of the three Pragmatic cluster games here and the most searched of the six.
The game operates a 7x7 grid and a cluster mechanic, meaning it has no wild symbol and relies on multiplier spots and tumbles (you can find out more about mechanics with our guide to how slot games work for new players).
The Sugar Rush slot is a high volatility game with an RTP of 96.50% and a max win of 5,000x stake, though note that builds of 95.50% and 94.50% exist, so check the info panel before playing.
4. Candy Blitz Bombs
Provider: Pragmatic Play
Candy Blitz Bombs is the 2024 sequel to Candy Blitz, with a 6x5 pays-anywhere grid. There's multiplier reel beside the grid that climbs by 1x with every tumble and bombs add values from 3x up to 500x, while the reel does not reset in the free spins.
The game is a high volatility title with an RTP of 96.49% and a max win of 5,000x stake, so overall we can say that the Candy Blitz slot suits a reader who wants a candy game with a lower ceiling and a more frequent win (note that hit frequency is quoted at about 33%).
5. Sweet Bonanza
Provider: Pragmatic Play
Sweet Bonanza was launched in 2019, but it is now the game the genre is measured against. This title uses a 6x5 grid and the pays-anywhere mechanic, with lollipop scatters and multiplier bombs that add together and are applied to the tumble win.
Sources do differ on the slot's RTP and max win, with figures around 96.48% and 21,100x to 21,175x quoted. As for volatility, it is quoted between medium and high, while the AnteBet can change the RTP to 96.51%.
Nevertheless, the Sweet Bonanza slot has now become one of the classics of the genre, and it suits experienced players looking for high win potential.
6. Sweet Alchemy 2
Provider: Play'n GO
Sweet Alchemy 2 is the one non-Pragmatic game on the list, with this title produced by Play'n GO. It uses a 5x5 grid that grows to 9x9, with winning clusters that break chocolate blocks and striped wilds that clear whole rows or columns.
This Sweet Alchemy title is yet another high volatility game, with an RTP of 96.20% and a top win ceiling of 10,000x stake, and overall it suits a reader who wants a candy game with more going on than tumbling symbols.
Reading the RTP column on this list
RTP is a long-run average measured over millions of spins, so it says nothing about a single session. It is simply a way to compare two games, not a prediction about either.
RTP describes the average return while volatility describes how the returns arrive, whether that's as many small wins or a few large ones. You can find out what the volatility figures actually change with our slot volatility guide.
The max win spread on our recommended best candy slots varies ranges from 5,000x to 25,000x, though remember that a bigger ceiling does not mean a better average return. For example, Sweet Bonanza 1000 quotes max win odds of 1 in 71 million.
