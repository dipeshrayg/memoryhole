Rod Stewart cancels all US and Mexico tour dates due to heart surgery
- Sir Rod Stewart has cancelled the remainder of his 2026 US and Mexico tour following a coronary stent procedure.
- The 81-year-old singer has been advised by doctors to take four weeks off to recover and regain full fitness.
- Affected shows include performances across Ohio, Illinois and Mexico, alongside a six-night residency at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas.
- Stewart reassured fans on Instagram that he is already feeling better, on the mend, and eager to return to the stage soon.
- The decision follows previous postponements earlier in the year caused by a sinus infection and laryngitis.
