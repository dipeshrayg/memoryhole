Chelsea appear set to strike a surprise double deal for England veterans Danny Welbeck and Jordan Henderson as Xabi Alonso seeks to add experience to his squad. The Brighton striker, 35, and Brentford midfielder, 36, could continue an intriguing summer of incomings at Stamford Bridge after the marquee addition of Morgan Rogers earlier in the window.
Arsenal, meanwhile, are plotting an audacious move for Real Madrid superstar Vinicius Jr and want to sign the Brazil winger this summer. The Premier League champions are preparing to offer the 26-year-old the biggest contract in their history but Real Madrid may yet offer the Brazilian fresh terms.
It could be a busy few weeks for Mikel Arteta's side as they also look to sign Newcastle captain Bruno Guimaraes and add another centre-back, with England internationals Ezri Konsa and John Stones both of interest - though the latter appears bound for Italy. Newcastle manager Eddie Howe confirmed that he wants to keep Guimaraes, but added that his future at the club is out of his control.
Elsewhere, Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola has set out his targets for the transfer window and has confirmed the Reds "definitely need to sign a winger", while France international Bradley Barcola is reportedly open to leaving Paris Saint-Germain this summer. PSG, who are set to miss out on the signing Yan Diomande to Real Madrid, would demand a record fee, however.
Manchester United are still interested in adding another midfielder and have "reached an agreement in principle" with France international Manu Kone, according to reports in Italy. Aurelien Tchouameni remains a target, too.

Mohamed Salah's move to Besiktas breaks down
Also on the hunt for a new club is Mohamed Salah - though it appears Besiktas won't be his next employers. Media in Turkey report that club director Onder Ozen has paused negotiations having been unhappy over the demands of Salah's agent.
John Stones in talks with Inter Milan
John Stones is available on a free after reaching the end of his Manchester City contract, and the Telegraph believe Inter Milan may be his next club. Arsenal had been keen on the experienced centre-half after William Saliba's injury.
Manchester United in talks to loan striker
Manchester United are in advanced talks to send Joshua Zirkzee, 25, on a season-long loan to Juventus, according to The Sun. The reported deal for the Netherlands forward includes an option to buy for £30m.
Man City continue talks with Ayyoub Bouaddi as Josko Gvardiol nears contract extension
Manchester City are still in direct talks with Lille over a move for World Cup breakout star Ayyoub Bouaddi, according to Fabrizio Romano.
City are the club most interested in the Moroccan but could opt for him to join in 2027 rather than this summer.
Meanwhile, Josko Gvardiol is set to sign a contract extension at the Etihad until 2031.
Leeds close in on James Trafford
James Trafford's search for a new club could soon be over with Leeds set to announce his signing. The goalkeeper's return to Manchester City lasted just one season, and a fee of £40m has been mooted by The Athletic.
Liverpool learn Bradley Barcola price
Widespread reports suggest that Paris Saint-Germain are open to selling Bradley Barcola - but only for a fee of around £145m or more. The Times suggest that Arsenal and Liverpool both have interest, though may be unwilling to pay the terms PSG desire with the French capital club using the Morgan Rogers and Elliot Anderson deals as a benchmark.
Negotiations continue for Yan Diomande
Real Madrid appear to have won the race for the in-demand Yan Diomande, though negotiations continue over the fee for the RB Leipzig winger. Sky Sport in Germany believe a deal is not yet totally done for the Ivorian, a top talent who was strongly linked with Liverpool earlier in the year.
Manchester City braced for offer for Rodri from Real Madrid
Real Madrid have been strongly linked with World Cup winner Rodri this summer, and Manchester City are said by The Guardian to be bracing for an offer for the Spanish midfielder.
Real Madrid to hold contract talks with Vinicius Jr amid Arsenal interest
Could Real Madrid yet see off Arsenal's interest and keep Vinicius Jr? Sky Sports reports that new contract talks are planned with the Brazilian's representatives with hope of a breakthrough.
Chelsea target Jordan Henderson to add experience to their squad
Widespread reports also suggest Chelsea are hoping to sign Jordan Henderson, who may be available on a free transfer from Brentford. The 36-year-old was part of England's World Cup squad and would add more experience to Xabi Alonso's squad.
