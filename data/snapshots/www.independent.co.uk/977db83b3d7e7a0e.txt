Bradley Barcola 'agrees to join Liverpool'
Paris Saint-Germain winger Bradley Barcola has agreed to join Liverpool, according to a report in the French sports daily L'Equipe.
It is undoubtedly a boost to Liverpool ahead of potentially facing competition from Premier League rivals Arsenal for the France international, but a deal remains some way off.
That's because PSG have set a price tag of £140m on the 23-year-old, and do not want to sell - especially as Real Madrid look likely to sign RB Leipzig's Yan Diomande.
As previously reported, PSG are using the Morgan Rogers and Elliot Anderson deals as a benchmark, while Barcola remains under contract with the European champions.
