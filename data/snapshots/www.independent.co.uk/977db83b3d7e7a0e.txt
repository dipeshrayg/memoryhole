Barcelona in for Junior Kroupi
Bournemouth sensation Junior Kroupi has attracted the interest of numerous big hitters, and now Barcelona have joined the likes of Arsenal and Tottenham in the race for the Frenchman.

Italy turn to Premier League winners to solve managerial crisis
Having been knocked back by Pep Guardiola, and forced to move on from Andrea Pirlo after a backlash, Italy finally have a new manager. He'll be joined by Claudio Ranieri as two Premier League winners unite - Ranieri replaces Paolo Maldini, who stepped down over the Pirlo decision, as technical director.
Micky van de Ven excited by Tottenham's summer spending spree as he makes promise to fans
Tottenham Hotspur defender Micky van de Ven believes the club is demonstrating "new ambition" following a substantial £237m summer spending spree, and has promised a more entertaining brand of football under Roberto De Zerbi next season.
The significant investment comes after a turbulent period for Spurs, which saw them narrowly avoid relegation on the final day of the 2025-26 Premier League campaign.
Majority owners the Lewis family subsequently pledged to be "all in" for a necessary rebuild, leading to six new arrivals. The club has twice broken its transfer record, first for £85m midfielder Mateus Fernandes, then securing Sandro Tonali from Newcastle in a £100m deal.
Inter Milan interested in Cristian Romero
Cristian Romero is expected to leave Tottenham this summer and Inter Milan want to sign the Argentina defender.
Spurs would be willing to let Romero leave if they receive the right offer, according to Sky Sports, following a tumultuous season.
Inter's sporting director Piero Ausilo has confirmed the Italian club's interest in the player, who previously played in Serie A with Alatanta.
"It's definitely a deal we are trying to sign with the player and the club [Tottenham]," Ausilo said. "These things take some time but there's definitely interest. Let's see."
