Jeremy Clarkson reacts to new Top Gear host
- Gordon Ramsay is set to host a new series of Top Gear, which will begin filming next year and air on BBC One in 2028.
- Former host Jeremy Clarkson highlighted the irony of Ramsay taking on a car show while Clarkson himself has recently opened a restaurant.
- Richard Hammond disclosed that he was invited to return to the show but turned the offer down to allow Top Gear to move in a new direction.
- Former presenters James May and Tiff Needell also reacted to the news, with May joking that Ramsay's role might improve motorway service food.
- The BBC show was previously placed on hiatus following a near-fatal crash involving former presenter Freddie Flintoff while filming in 2022.
