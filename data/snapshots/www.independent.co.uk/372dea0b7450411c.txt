Celtic failed to get revenge over Rangers as they lost 1-0 in the first Old Firm clash of the Scottish Premiership season, for a second defeat at the hands of their bitter rivals in a week
The Gers powered to an impressive 3-0 victory in the League Cup quarter-finals at Ibrox last weekend, and followed that up with another dominant display, although the scoreline was closer this time around.
Ryan Naderi slammed home a rebound on 33 minutes for the decisive goal and completed a week that has surely given Derek McInnes lift-off after a difficult start to life in Glasgow.
Away fans are banned from matches between the Old Firm teams in the Premiership and League Cup this season, so Celtic Park was exclusively green and white but that didn't help Martin O'Neill's side, who also endured a deflating defeat to Ferencvaros in the Europa League in midweek and have now lost three on the spin with the clouds of crisis beginning to gather over Parkhead.

Rangers racked up a second win over Celtic in eight days as boos rung around an all-green Parkhead following a 1-0 defeat by the Light Blues.
Celtic's 74-year-old boss Martin O'Neill went into the game under the spotlight after two successive losses, including a bruising 3-0 Scottish League Cup loss to Gers at Ibrox last Sunday.
To silence inside a stadium populated with home fans only, Gers striker Ryan Naderi opened the scoring in the 33rd minute and it was enough for another huge victory for Derek McInnes's men who will feel they should have added to that goal after the break.
It was a first Scottish Premiership defeat of the season for the home side, who are just two points ahead of their Old Firm rivals at the top of the table going into the international break with more pressure on O'Neill.
