How to save on your holiday with Agoda
10 per cent first booking discount
Agoda is offering 10 per cent off your first trip when you book via its dedicated app, making it even easier to bag a last-minute deal on hotels, resorts and holiday rentals worldwide. Simply download the app and create an account to get your instant discount.
Agoda members save up to 25 per cent
To access the best deals throughout the year, you may want to sign up to Agoda's VIP loyalty programme. This is free to join - all you need to do is make two or more bookings in two years and you'll automatically qualify for the programme. After that, the idea is simple: the more bookings you make, the higher your status will rise and the higher the discounts you'll unlock.
Memberships are divided into four tiers, each with its own perks. Here's what to expect:
- Member: Entry-level status doesn't give you any additional discounts, but you will gain access to AgodaCash, which you can use to get money off future bookings.
- VIP Silver: Once you've made two bookings, you'll find discounts of up to 12 per cent on selected listings in your account.
- VIP Gold: If you make five bookings within 24 months, you'll be upgraded to VIP Gold status and enjoy 18 per cent off selected destinations.
- VIP Platinum: After 10 bookings, you'll enter VIP Platinum status, where you'll be given up to 25 per cent off hotels and accommodation around the world.
Up to 30 per cent off with Agoda's international deals
Agoda's international hotel sale is offering savings of up to 30 per cent. Whether you're planning a city escape, beach retreat or cultural adventure, these limited-time rates aren't to be missed.
Seasonal savings at Agoda in 2026
With summer travel in full swing, there is still plenty of time to save on your next getaway. Whether you're planning a last-minute beach holiday or looking ahead to city breaks for autumn, Agoda often offers seasonal discounts and flash sales to keep you saving throughout the year. Expect discounts of up to 30 per cent on select hotels and destinations during the colder months.
Booking in advance and checking the "today's deals" section can help you unlock even better rates. For exclusive codes, sign up for Agoda's newsletter or enable app notifications to stay updated on limited-time offers.
