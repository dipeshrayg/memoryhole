Jannik Sinner has undergone knee surgery and is now likely to miss the Australian Open in January.
The five-time grand slam champion has not played since winning Wimbledon in July and announced he would be ending his season in October.
The 25-year-old had planned to return in late December, ahead of the 2027 season, but it was decided that surgery was required to address the issue.
According to reports in Italy, Sinner could be sidelined for up to three months after undergoing an operation on the iliotibial band (IT band) in his right leg, although he did not specify a timeline for his recovery when announcing the news.
The world No 2 posted on X on Tuesday: "Over the past few months, together with my team we've tried everything to treat my injury without surgery.
"After some very encouraging progress early on, my knee unfortunately didn't respond as we'd all hoped, that's why today I underwent a minor surgery to improve the condition of my iliotibial band.
"I really miss being on court and will now begin my recovery process together with my team. Thank you for all your support. See you soon!"
