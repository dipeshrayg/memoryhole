Arsenal are hoping to secure the signing of Julian Alvarez to cap off a productive summer of incomings and boost their forward line as they look to defend their Premier League title. Alvarez is said to be open to the move having initially favoured a switch to Barcelona and he is the Gunners' priority signing after their enquiries over Kenan Yildiz where shut down by Juventus.
Liverpool, meanwhile, are targeting a new winger with the need for more attacking options evidenced by their dramatic 2-2 draw with Newcastle on Sunday. They continue to pursue PSG's Bradley Barcola who was left out of the squad for their game against Rennes while the Reds have seen two bids for Brighton's Yankuba Minteh turned down. This is perhaps a move closest to happening with Minteh having reportedly agreed personal terms. Real Madrid's Endrick is also of interest.
Elsewhere, Manchester City have agreed a €100m deal with Lille to buy midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi while Omar Marmoush and Nico Gonzalez were part of the squad to face Bournemouth despite interest from Spurs and Newcastle respectively. Interestingly both Ollie Watkins and Emi Martinez were also absent from Aston Villa's matchday squad amid rumours that both players could be preparing for imminent exits.

Julian Alvarez met with deafening jeers on Atletico Madrid return amid Arsenal interest
Julian Alvarez was met with a chorus of jeers by aggrieved fans on his return to Atletico Madrid amid his attempts to force a move away from the club.
All eyes were on Atletico's team sheet to face Villarreal on Sunday to see whether Alvarez would be involved as the club played their first home match of the season.
And while the 26-year-old was named on the bench, this did not mean things had been smoothed over - especially with the Atletico faithful, who sent him a brutal message as soon as he stepped onto the turf.

Mohamed Salah breaks Trabzonspor duck
Mohamed Salah scored his first goals for Trabzonspor with a brace in their 2-1 win over Istanbul Basaksehir yesterday.
The former Liverpool forward joined the Super Lig side on a two-year deal at the start of August and made his debut for the club from the bench in their 1-1 draw at Kasimpasa last week.
Salah was named in the starting line-up for Sunday's clash with Basaksehir and made an instant impact, opening the scoring in the eighth minute.
Basaksehir levelled in the 19th minute when Eldor Shomurodov slotted home from the penalty spot, but Salah restored his side's lead just three minutes into the second half.
Is Ayoubb Bouaddi the 'new Rodri' Enzo Maresca is after?
Manchester City have agreed a €100m deal to sign Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille, potentially providing an answer to Enzo Maresca's mission to find a replacement for Rodri.
The Golden Ball winner joined Barcelona in a sizeable blow for the new City manager, who had spoken ahead of his league opener against Bournemouth about the job of filling such a void.
"How do we solve that problem? We try to find the new Rodri, it is what it is. [Before] Rodri arrived here, he didn't win the Ballon d'Or, he didn't win the Champions League, he didn't win the Club World Cup. He joined the club, then he achieved many important things," Maresca said.
"Now the target for us is to try to find the new Rodri. I was thinking on Thursday night, when David Silva was here, everyone was worried and Kevin [De Bruyne] arrived. When Sergio [Agüero] was here, everyone was worried [when he was going to leave] and Erling [Haaland] arrived. Fernandinho? Rodri arrived. It is just about this. When they leave, you need to be good at recruiting the next one.
"They are all similar, David, Kevin, Fernandinho, Rodri, Erling - it is normal, it is change, it is not a problem, go again and try to do the right things."

ICYMI: Bradley Barcola left out of another PSG squad amid Liverpool and Arsenal interest
Bradley Barcola was left out of another Paris Saint-Germain squad as the France international continues to be linked with a move to Liverpool and Arsenal. The 23-year-old was not involved in PSG's victory over Aston Villa in the Super Cup or the defeat to Lens in the French Super Cup and has been left out of Luis Enrique's 21-man squad for their Ligue 1 opener against Rennes.
Liverpool are in the market for a winger they and PSG remain far apart in their valuations of Barcola, with the Champions League winners wanting £145m. Liverpool have also been linked with PSG's 18-year-old forward Ibrahim Mbaye, who like Barcola is not part of the squad to face Rennes on Sunday. Arsenal, meanwhile, want to strengthen the left side of their attack and were recently explored a move for Juventus forward Kenan Yildiz.
Barcola has played a role in PSG's back-to-back Champions League titles under Enrique but faces an uncertain future after the European champions strengthened their attack this summer. PSG have signed three attackers this summer in Spain's World Cup winner Ferran Torres from Barcelona, Belgian winger Mika Godts from Ajax and France international Maghnes Akliouche from Marseille.
ICYMI: Crystal Palace in chaos as Jean-Philippe Mateta argues contract invalid
Jean-Philippe Mateta has called in Fifa as he attempts to argue that his Crystal Palace contract is invalid, according to the BBC.
The striker, who started the 2-0 defeat at Everton on Saturday, has entered the final 12 months of his deal but is reportedly arguing that a 12-month extension, triggered by Palace in 2024, should be void.
It Fifa rule out Mateta's side, it would make the striker a free agent this summer. The France international previously pushed to join AC Milan in January but the move fell through due to a knee issue.
ICYMI: Julian Alvarez would welcome Arsenal move?
Julian Alvarez would welcome a move to Arsenal this summer - if his dream move to Barcelona falls through. That's according to the Standard, who report that the Argentina international's stand-off with Atletico Madrid is beginning to turn sour as he continues to push for a move away. Alvarez is not for sale this summer, according to Atletico, who play Villarreal this afternoon.
Alvarez has been in contact with Mikel Arteta and Arsenal sporting director Andrea Berta, according to reports in Spain. AS report that Alvarez has come round to the idea of joining the Gunners if Atletico refuse to sell to Barcelona and the door to joining the Premier League champions remains open.
Arsenal want to a sign a marquee attacker this summer, but Juventus have closed the door on forward Kenan Yildiz joining the Premier League champions. Luciano Spalletti told reports that the Turkey international is not for sale and Juventus want to keep the 21-year-old for the upcoming season.
