Newcastle agree £52m deal for Manchester City midfielder as Bruno Guimaraes replacement
Newcastle have agreed a £52m deal with Manchester City for Nico Gonzalez and the midfielder is set to become their biggest summer signing.
The 2025 Carabao Cup winners will pay an initial £48m for the Spaniard with a potential £4m in add-ons.
It could give City a small profit after signing Gonzalez from Porto in January 2025 for £49m.
Gonzalez, while only 24, will become the oldest player Newcastle have signed this summer after they decided they need to add more experience following the departures of captain Bruno Guimaraes and Sandro Tonali from the midfield.
Tottenham complete £85m signing of Man City winger Savinho
Savinho has completed his £85m move from Tottenham to Manchester City as Spurs have taken their spending this summer past £300m.
The Brazilian becomes City's record sale - replacing Julian Alvarez, who moved to Atletico Madrid for £82m two years ago - and one of the three most expensive players in Tottenham's history, along with his new teammates Sandro Tonali and Mateus Fernandes.
Spurs will pay an initial £75m, with a further £5m of add-ons guaranteed and the potential for £5m more on top of that.

Nottingham Forest agree £50m deal for Liam Delap
Nottingham Forest have agreed a club record deal of £50m to sign Chelsea striker Liam Delap.
The former Ipswich man has been displaced in the No 9 shirt at Stamford Bridge by Joao Pedro and the Chelsea hierarchy were open to letting him leave after a disappointing first campaign at the club.
Chelsea will make £20m profit on a striker they signed for £30m, and who scored just two goals in 41 appearances.
The 23-year-old is expected to complete a medical on Wednesday.
Man Utd receive Balde boost
Spanish publication Marca are reporting that Alejandro Balde has decided that he wants to leave Barcelona in a huge boost to Manchester United's hopes of signing him.
The report says that Joao Cancelo's arrival and the fact that Balde was left out of Barca's matchday squad for the game against Elche has changed Balde's mind about remaining at the club and fighting for his place.
United are the frontrunners for his signature but they are said to want a loan deal instead of a permanent transfer as previously reported.
