Burnham says Britain is "100 per cent behind" Ukraine during first Kyiv visit
- Prime Minister Andy Burnham has arrived in Kyiv for Ukraine's 35th anniversary of independence, assuring the country of 100 per cent British backing for as long as it takes.
- During his first foreign trip, Burnham will chair a meeting of the coalition of the willing and receive an award recognising the UK's support on behalf of the British public.
- The UK government has permitted defence firm MBDA to share classified information regarding components for long-range Scalp missiles to aid local assembly in Ukraine.
- The Kremlin accused London of deliberately escalating the conflict through proxy warfare, warning that the UK will inevitably face consequences for its actions.
- Diplomatic relations between Moscow and Downing Street remain severely strained, with Russia yet to appoint a replacement for its former ambassador to the UK.
