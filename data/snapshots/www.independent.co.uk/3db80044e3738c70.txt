Melania Trump receives 18ft Christmas tree at White House ahead of decor reveal
- First Lady Melania Trump accepted delivery of the 2026 White House Christmas tree on Monday ahead of her decor reveal.
- She received the tree, an 18-foot white fir from Silent Night Evergreens in Wisconsin, on the North Drive of the White House.
- The first lady previewed her annual White House holiday decorations, set to be unveiled later this month, in a Fox News interview.
- Host Brian Kilmeade joked, "This year will be more gold," referring to the president's recent redecoration of the Oval Office.
- Melania flatly replied, "Uh, not really," highlighting a contrast in her and her husband's design preferences.
