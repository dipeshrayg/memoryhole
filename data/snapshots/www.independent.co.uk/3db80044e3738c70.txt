Melania Trump receives 18ft Christmas tree at White House ahead of decor reveal
- First Lady Melania Trump accepted delivery of the 2026 White House Christmas tree on Monday.
- She received the tree, an 18-foot white fir from Silent Night Evergreens in Wisconsin, on the North Drive of the White House.
- Melania is due to join military spouses at Joint Base Andrews to make care packages later on, with Usha Vance set to join her.
- The first lady previewed her annual White House holiday decorations in a Fox News interview.
- When co-host Brian Kilmeade joked about adding more gold to match recent changes in the executive mansion, Melania flatly replied, "Uh, not really," highlighting a contrast in their design preferences.
