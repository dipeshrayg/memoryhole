Israel slams UK plan to sanction West Bank settlements as 'discrimination'
- US Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee has strongly condemned expected UK sanctions on illegal West Bank settlements, branding the trade restrictions an irrational action and discrimination against Jewish people while warning of potential economic damage.
- Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband is set to announce a reset of UK-Israel relations in Parliament, outlining targeted measures and potential trade restrictions on goods originating from illegal Israeli settlements in the West Bank.
- Work and Pensions Secretary Pat McFadden rejected allegations of discrimination, clarifying that the policy targets illegal settlement expansion specifically and does not amount to a wider boycott of Israeli trade or security co-operation.
- Political figures and commentators, including Shadow Foreign Secretary Tom Tugendhat and Labour MP Peter Prinsley, warned the move could undermine vital counter-terrorism intelligence sharing with Israel and risk escalating attacks against the British Jewish community.
- Israeli ministers responded with threats of retaliation, with Foreign Minister Gideon Saar stating Israel would act against Britain if sanctions were imposed, while Public Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir called on Benjamin Netanyahu to recognise Argentine sovereignty over the Falkland Islands.
