Why Icelandic voters just rejected European Union negotiations
- Icelanders have rejected a proposal to restart negotiations on joining the European Union by a margin of 52.8% to 47.2%.
- The referendum was brought forward by the government following comments from US President Donald Trump regarding neighbouring Greenland.
- The capital city of Reykjavik was the only region in the country where a majority of voters supported resuming accession talks.
- Opponents, spearheaded by the fishing industry, argued that EU membership would sacrifice sovereignty and open national waters to foreign trawlers.
- Supporters claimed joining the EU would bring economic stability, lower inflation, and strengthen ties with European partners.
