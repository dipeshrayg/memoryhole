Buc-ee's reveals its expansion rule - and blue states may not like it
- Buc-ee's co-founder and CEO Arch "Beaver" Aplin III announced that the travel center chain will focus future expansion on conservative, business-friendly states.
- Speaking at a store opening alongside Arkansas Governor Sarah Huckabee Sanders, Aplin emphasized that the brand's values align best with red-state leadership.
- Sanders supported the chain's decision, stating that businesses and residents are fleeing blue states for conservative ones like Arkansas.
- The strategic focus comes as Buc-ee's faces growing public backlash and calls for boycotts over its aggressive trademark lawsuits against small businesses.
- Comedian John Oliver and Ohio Governor Mike DeWine recently criticized Buc-ee's legal battle against an Ohio corner store using a beaver mascot.
