NBA legend Shaquille O'Neal set to be focus of bizarre new game show
- Basketball legend Shaquille O'Neal is set to star in a new ABC hide-and-seek game show titled Can You Hide... Shaq?, which will be hosted by comedian Nicole Byer.
- The series features eight contestants competing in teams across Dallas to conceal the 7-foot-1-inch sports star in plain sight while undertaking fast-paced challenges.
- Teams will attempt to complete missions with the fewest spottings to earn immunity and build a cash prize pool known as the Shaq-pot for one ultimate winner.
- The unusual premise of the series has already prompted online amusement, with social media users comparing the concept to a satirical television pitch from the sitcom 30 Rock.
- Can You Hide... Shaq? will premiere on Thursday, 3 December, with a double-episode launch on ABC and the ESPN app, followed by streaming availability on Hulu the next day.
