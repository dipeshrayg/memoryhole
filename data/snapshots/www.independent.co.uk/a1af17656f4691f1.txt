Washington Commanders sell throwback merch with controversial 'Redskins' logo
- Limited-edition throwback merchandise featuring the retired Washington Redskins name and logo sold out at the Washington Commanders' team store in less than 48 hours.
- The collection included apparel honoring former Washington players Art Monk, Darrell Green, and Sean Taylor.
- A Commanders spokesperson stated the team released the items to maintain legal control over its old trademarks and prevent unauthorized commercial use by others.
- The franchise emphasized that it has no plans to permanently bring back the retired name or logo, which was dropped in 2020.
- The Association on American Indian Affairs condemned the sale, stating that reviving the racist imagery for nostalgia and profit perpetuates harmful stereotypes.
