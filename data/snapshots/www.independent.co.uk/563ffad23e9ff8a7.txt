A busy year of international action continues for Australia and South Africa as the two sides collide in an intriguing one-off Test in Perth.
The Springboks are heavily changed from the side that secured a 3-1 Rugby's Greatest Rivalry series success in their last outing two weeks ago, with Rassie Erasmus taking the opportunity to assess the depth of his squad. A trip to Western Australia is valuable preparation for next year's World Cup, too, with the defending champions likely to play twice in Perth during the course of that tournament.
The Wallabies, meanwhile, will relish an opportunity to test themselves against the best having made an encouraging start to life under Les Kiss. Time is tight to find their groove before a World Cup on home soil but a statement win here could prove just the jolt they need with little more than 12 months to go.
