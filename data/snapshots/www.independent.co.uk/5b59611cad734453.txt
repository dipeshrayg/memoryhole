Why you can't buy a PlayStation 5 Pro
In April 2026, Sony hiked the price of all three PS5 consoles by a whopping £90, blaming global RAM shortages and higher manufacturing costs due to global tariffs. The PS5 Slim with the disc drive increased to £569.99, the PS5 Slim digital edition rose to £519.99, and the PS5 Pro went from £699.99 to an eye-watering £789.99. The latter is also out of stock everywhere.
When it comes to shopping for PS5 deals in the Black Friday sales this year, there are some small savings on PS5 bundles, but the PS5 Pro remains completely sold out, thanks to RAMageddon and increased demand due to the launch of GTA 6.
The cheapest way to play 'GTA 6'
As The Independent's gaming correspondent, Jake Brigstock, explained in his GTA 6 article, there are two versions of the game available to buy: the Standard Edition and Ultimate Edition. "Basically, the Ultimate Edition allows access to more shops, vehicles, weapons, and garages," wrote Jake. "However, none of this is truly essential to get the most out of GTA 6." While the Ultimate Edition will set you back £89.99 (Playstation.com), the Standard Edition is £20 cheaper, at £69.99 (Playstation.com).
Jake's advice is: "If you want to enjoy GTA 6's story, explore, and complete activities without being too fussed about customising Jason and Lucia's appearance, then the Standard Edition is the best bet." He added that, even if you do opt for the Ultimate Edition, "a lot of the upgrades unlock as you play through the story. In other words, not all of this extra content is accessible from the very beginning."
