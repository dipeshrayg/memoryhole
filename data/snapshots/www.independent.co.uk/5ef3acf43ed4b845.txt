What is the Aspinal of London returns policy?
If you have changed your mind about your Aspinal of London item, you can return it within 30 days for a full refund or exchange. Outlet items have a shorter returns period of 14 days. Simply head to the returns page with your email address and order number to start the process.
What is the Aspinal of London warranty period?
All Aspinal of London products, excluding phone cases and covers, come with a one-year manufacturer's warranty as standard. Phone cases and covers come with a six-month warranty. This covers your item for a replacement if there are any manufacturer faults during this period. For other damage (excluding normal wear and tear), you can still get a free repair under the warranty period.
Does Aspinal of London offer a repair service?
All products can be sent off for a free repair (including stitching, zips, metalwork, handles and corners) within the warranty period. Outside of this, you can book a chargeable repair online.
