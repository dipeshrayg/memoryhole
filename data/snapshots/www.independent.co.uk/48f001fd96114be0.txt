Rita Wilson is coming clean about her massive back tattoo.
The actor and singer, 70, surprised fans in September when she posted a picture on Instagram of what appeared to be an enormous cross on her back that matched that of her 36-year-old son, Chet Hanks.
However, she laughed as she confessed Monday on CBS Mornings: "It's not real."
"It was a prank that I did for my son's birthday who has a lot of tattoos," she said, explaining that Chet's tattoo is the real deal.
She continued: "I used to threaten him with getting a tattoo. I would say, 'You better watch out. One day we're going to be on the beach and I'm just going to take off my [shirt].'"
Chester "Chet" Marlon Hanks is the oldest son of Wilson and actor Tom Hanks, who also share 30-year-old son Truman. Wilson is also the stepmother to Tom's two children from his previous marriage to his late ex-wife Samantha Lewes: 49-year-old Colin and 44-year-old Elizabeth.
The Sleepless in Seattle star said that she has no tattoos of her own.
"My daughter said the new tattoo is not having a tattoo," Wilson joked. "Definitely a different look [for me]."
Wilson, who put out her sixth studio album, Sound of a Woman, in May, had first shared the fake tattoo in an Instagram post while promoting her new song, "Stranger with Age."
"STRANGER WITH AGE? Who? Me? I'm lovin' it!" she captioned a carousel of pictures at the time. "Who else is stranger with age? STRANGER WITH AGE song is out everywhere."
The massive tattoo of the Celtic cross was seen on Wilson's back in the photo. The picture also showed another design of a sun on Wilson's arm that matched one of Chet's permanent arm tattoos, as well as ink on her arm that appeared to say "Happy birthday Chet."
Wilson opened up about raising her children in an interview with The Independent earlier this year and spoke about protecting her family's privacy.
"You're trying to make sure they can grow up in a world where they're able to be themselves, without the burden that comes with being a public figure," she explained in April.
She said about Chet during the interview: "Oh my God, he's a character! He's so eccentric. You know what? He is the person I could never have been at that age... he's just out there being himself, take it or leave it. I love that."
