Ukraine to intensify Russian oil strikes as Putin plans to expand attacks
- Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky announced plans to intensify strikes against Russian oil refineries after intelligence revealed Vladimir Putin authorised a new doctrine expanding attacks on Ukrainian non-military infrastructure to drive citizens out of major cities.
- Despite US President Donald Trump requesting an end to attacks on Russian energy sites to prevent global fuel price rises, Zelensky maintained that targeting Russian oil revenues is vital while reiterating that Ukraine will not attack civilian targets.
- Ukraine's Vivaldi counteroffensive has successfully reclaimed approximately 140 square kilometres in the Donbas region as the Trump administration attempts to reboot diplomatic efforts to resolve the conflict.
- To combat daily Russian attacks from fast, low-cost jet-powered Shahed drones and long-range missiles, Ukraine is developing five indigenous interceptor drone models and expediting long-range domestic ballistic missiles capable of striking Moscow.
- European allies and Ukrainian negotiators have successfully secured financial backing to bridge a $27 billion shortfall in Kyiv's current defence budget and ensure drone funding through to early 2027.
