Russell Brand found guilty of one rape and three sexual assaults
- Russell Brand has been convicted of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault following a four-week trial at Southwark Crown Court.
- A jury consisting of eight men and four women deliberated for just under two days before returning their guilty verdicts.
- The convictions cover a ten-year period between 1999 and 2009 during a time when Brand was rising to staggering fame.
- The rape conviction relates to an attack on a young fan following one of his live shows in 2009.
- Brand stood upright in the dock and closed his eyes, showing limited emotion as the verdicts were delivered. He was found not guilty of two further rapes and an attempted indecent assault.
