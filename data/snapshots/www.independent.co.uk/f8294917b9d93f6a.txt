Russell Brand found guilty of one rape and three sexual assaults
- Comedian and TV presenter Russell Brand has been found guilty at Southwark Crown Court of one count of rape and three counts of sexual assault following a trial lasting nearly four weeks.
- The jury deliberated for eight and a half hours before convicting the 51-year-old of raping a fan in 2009, alongside sexually assaulting three women employed by MTV, Capital Radio, and Channel 4 between 2004 and 2009.
- Brand was cleared of three other charges, comprising two counts of rape and one charge of attempted indecent assault, which related to allegations from 1999, 2001, and 2004.
- During the trial, Brand denied the allegations and claimed to be the target of a media conspiracy, but faced testimony from six women alongside evidence from his own 2007 autobiography regarding his sexual conduct.
- Following the jury's verdicts, Mr Justice Bennathan informed Brand that a substantial custodial sentence would be imposed, with the formal sentencing hearing scheduled for Monday.
