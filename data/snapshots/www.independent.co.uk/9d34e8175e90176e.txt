Celia Imrie warned Richard E Grant of Traitors chaos before his blunder
- Celebrity Traitors star Celia Imrie revealed she warned actor Richard E Grant before his appearance on the BBC show that filming is very strict and it is hard to keep your brain working at all hours.
- Grant threw the first episode of the series into disarray when he inadvertently exposed his secret identity as a traitor to his fellow contestants at the very start of the game.
- Former BBC presenter Amol Rajan, who was the first celebrity killed off by Grant and co-traitor Maya Jama, disclosed that the actor admitted he just went mad during filming.
- Host Claudia Winkleman attributed Grant's early blunder to sheer giddiness, explaining that he was so thrilled to be selected as a traitor that he took playing the game to the next level.
- Grant and Jama, alongside their newly recruited traitor James Acaster, must now work to eliminate the remaining 17 faithful contestants without being detected.
