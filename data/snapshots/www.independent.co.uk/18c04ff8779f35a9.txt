Skipper Dan Neil grabbed a glorious double as Derek McInnes claimed his first Old Firm victory as Rangers boss with an emphatic 3-0 Scottish League Cup win over Celtic.
With no away fans inside Ibrox at the order of the authorities amid a ticket allocation spat between the two Glasgow giants, the whole stadium exploded in blue in the 22nd minute when midfielder Neil, signed from Sunderland in the summer, guided in his first Gers goal from the edge of the box.
New striker Kevin Kelsy fired in a second in the 59th minute, his first in Light Blue, with Neil adding a third with another fine finish in the 75th minute as the hosts marched into the semi-finals.
It was a first domestic defeat of the season for Martin O'Neill's side, who rarely threatened the Gers goal - although they will not have long to wait for revenge as the two sides meet in the Scottish Premiership next Sunday where again visiting fans are banned, although after five wins in a row for Rangers, they will travel with confidence.

Derek McInnes with dream Old Firm debut
It's not been the smoothest start to life as Rangers boss for Derek McInnes (*cough* Jagiellonia Bialystok *cough*) but this will massively help.
One Old Firm derby, one victory for the ex-Hearts man. It was a tactical masterclass as well. His decision to go with three at the back and overload the midfield afforded Rangers complete control.
How will Martin O'Neill respond in seen days' time?
Just seven days until the next Old Firm
Today was great. Fancy doing it again? Of course you do!
Luckily there's only seven day to wait for the next Old Firm clash, with Celtic and Rangers going head to head in the Scottish Premiership next Sunday.
This time, the game is at Celtic Park and there won't be any Gers fans in attendance due to the ongoing ban.
Can Celtic bounce back or will Rangers use this victory to kick on in the league?
Rangers find out semi-final opponents
The semi-final draw for the Scottish League Cup has just been completed, so we now know that Rangers will play Aberdeen at Hampden Park for a place in the final.
That means it's an Edinburgh derby in the other semi as Hearts square off with Hibernian. That's a couple of fun ties.
Scottish League Cup semi-final draw
- Hearts v Hibernian
- Rangers v Aberdeen
Ties to be played on weekend of 31 October / 1 November
Dan Neil: 'We were ready to run through brick walls'
A captain's performance from Dan Neil today and he spoke to Premier Sports after his two goals.
"Brilliant," said Neil. "It was a great team performance. We fully deserved the victory out there.
"I don't score many goals. I thought the big man [Kevin Kelsy] was excellent today.
"In the last few games we've done well without the ball. Today we wanted to use the crowd and get after them - the lads executed it perfectly.
"We all believed in the plan. The gaffer is good before games and he made sure we were ready to run through brick walls. That togetherness and cohesion has really come on recently."
