Trump lashes out at heckler during Florida rally: 'Shut up dope'
Donald Trump lashed out after a heckler interrupted his rally in Florida on Friday (30 October).
The president was speaking at an event in Daytona Beach. He had several people removed from the crowd whom he claimed were "paid protesters."
"These are the most important midterm elections ever. And by the way, there's never been such excitement for a midterm... Shut up, dope. Shut up. Loser. What a loser. Get him out of here," Trump said.
It comes as early voting has jumped in key Senate battleground states as the president begins his final push before the highly consequential midterm elections.
