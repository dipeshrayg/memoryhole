It was the end of the road for Traitor Richard E Grant in the third episode of Celebrity Traitors, with the Oscar nominee ousted before two major plot twists saw a new Traitor recruited and a Faithful banished.
Grant, who was selected by host Claudia Winkleman to be one of two Traitors alongside Maya Jama in the series' first episode, caused waves of suspicion almost immediately last week when he - for slightly inexplicable reasons - declared to Faithful Joe Lycett during a gab in the kitchen that he was, in fact, a Traitor. This ended up getting him banished.
But it wasn't over yet: Maya and fellow Traitor James Acaster decided to recruit Hannah Fry to their side, with Hannah then using her newfound powers to throw suspicion onto Industry star Myha'la. Her fellow stars seemingly took the bait, and Myha'la was banished. We left the series with yet another cliffhanger ending, as Maya, James and Hannah plotted to murder one of the three Faithfuls they've marked for death: Bella Ramsey, Jerry Hall and Sebastian Croft.
Follow along with all the latest Celebrity Traitors updates in our live blog below, with the next episode starting at 8pm on Friday on BBC One.

On Celebrity Traitors: Uncloaked, Richard E Grant tried to explain why he quickly announced that he was a Traitor, and repeated his oft-mentioned excuse that he was just too excited.
Myha'la
Newly banished Myha'la has discovered who the Traitors are - and it's safe to say she was shocked.
Poor Maya Jama
Is anyone else getting the sneaking suspicion that this year's "baddie" Maya Jama is getting fed up with her timid accomplices?
She has herself to blame - Amol Rajan would have quite evidently lapped up the chance to be a Traitor, and she opted for James Acaster instead. And picking Hannah Fry put her in a hell of a position, considering she was up for "murder" - so everyone would naturally think she could've been recruited.
Here's hoping she can bring the evil out of them a little bit in the episodes to come...
Hannah Fry - right or wrong choice for recruitment?
The Traitors had a choice to "murder" Hannah Fry or Jerry Hall, or recruit somebody, and they opted to recruit... Hannah Fry.
"It was a question of kill me or recruit me," Hannah said, acknowledging that she is "the biggest threat" to the existing Traitors.
But was this the right choice? Fans are divided, with some believing they should have chosen another random Faithful who wasn't up for murder.
However, it could be a genius move, considering there's some suspicion on Hannah and Maya Jama and James Acaster would have an easy sacrifice on their hands should they need it...
Who will get 'murdered' tonight?
In the running for "murder" tonight are Bella Ramsey, Sebastian "Bash" Croft and James Blunt.
I'd rule Blunt out considering he's an absolute master at orchestrating tactics during the tasks - a sure-fire way to insure you remain on the show for a while - leaving Bella and Bash.
I think Bash would be the logical choice here because A) he's clearly a Faithful and B) I think it might throw suspicion onto Bella, considering they're close friends and people will think it's a classic attempt at misdirect.
ICYMI: James Blunt has gone viral
James Blunt has somewhat surprisingly become the star of the annual Traitors shower shot.
Myha'la blames Richard E Grant for banishment
Industry star Myha'la became the second celebrity to be banished - and she blames Traitor Richard E Grant for her fate.
"I immediately got close to Richard, just because I feel like we're twin flames. Really, I shouldn't trust him as far as I could throw him, which is ultimately, I think, my demise," she said.
Myha'la added that she's "annoyed" Richard couldn't stay in "because I feel like he could have brought me to the turret and I would have gladly led him to glory".
"There's an alternate universe in which he and I get to the final," the Faithful - who desperately wanted to be a Traitor - said.
