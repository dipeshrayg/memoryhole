Shark kills 65-year-old female swimmer in South Africa - report
- A female swimmer has died following a shark attack off Orient Beach in KuGompo City, South Africa.
- Buffalo City Metropolitan Municipality has closed both Orient Beach and Eastern Beach until further notice.
- Beachgoers have been urged to refrain from entering the water and to exercise extreme caution across affected areas.
- Safety warnings are being issued at other nearby beaches, with the public advised to remain vigilant.
- Relevant authorities remain on site following the tragic incident.
