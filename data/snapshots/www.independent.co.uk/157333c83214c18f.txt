Who are Iran's new wartime commanders?
Iran's Supreme Leader Ayatollah Mojtaba Khamenei has appointed six senior commanders to key military and security posts, reshaping the wartime leadership after several top commanders were killed.
The appointments came a day after former IRGC chief Mohsen Rezaee was named secretary of the Supreme National Security Council.
- Ali Abdollahi: Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces, overseeing coordination across the military.
- Kioumars Heydari: Deputy Chief of Staff.
- Ahmad Vahidi: New IRGC commander, tasked with boosting deterrence and readiness for "powerful offensive operations".
- Mostafa Izadi: Vahidi's deputy, responsible for operational readiness.
- Ali Ozmaei: New IRGC Navy commander, a key role amid tensions around the Strait of Hormuz.
- Hossein Taeb: New Basij commander, focusing on mobilisation and domestic intelligence. Basij is Iran's paramilitary volunteer militia within the IRGC.
British couple jailed in Iran over espionage end their hunger strike
British couple Craig and Lindsay Foreman, who have been jailed in Iran on widely criticised espionage charges, have ended hunger strikes lasting 93 and 84 days respectively.
The East Sussex couple, who deny the charges, were arrested in January last year while travelling by motorcycle from Europe to Australia and sentenced to 10 years in prison in February.
Craig Foreman's sentence was later reportedly extended after he spoke to the media.
They began the hunger strike in May after prison authorities stopped them from contacting their families.
They agreed to end it after guards promised they could resume direct contact, though their family said Monday that contact had not yet been restored.
