Blood banks in France were hit by a cyberattack on Monday, the French health ministry has said, the latest in a series of online hoaxes and cyber attacks across Europe.
The incident follows attacks that forced stores in eight European countries to throw away vast quantities of food after hackers tampered with their fridges on Monday, mirroring hacks that swept dozens of supermarkets in France three weeks ago.
But there was no damage to blood products stored in the centres.
"Several regional facilities of the French Blood Establishment (EFS) were affected by a cybersecurity incident that impacted certain cold storage room monitoring and control systems," the ministry's statement said.
"The situation is now under control and is being closely monitored ... The safety of the blood supplies was preserved thanks to the facilities' monitoring and alarm systems, as well as precautionary measures immediately implemented."
Investigations into the incident and equipment potentially affected are underway as authorities look at a timeline for a safe return to service.
