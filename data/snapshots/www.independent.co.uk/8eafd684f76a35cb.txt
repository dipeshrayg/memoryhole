Donald Trump's diesel deal with Vladimir Putin is a "very risky choice" that will undermine efforts to bring Moscow to the negotiating table, Giorgia Meloni has said.
In the most critical comments yet issued by a European leader, the Italian prime minister said that the agreement for Russia to supply 300,000 metric tons of diesel to the US would have an "uncertain" outcome.
"It provides Moscow with legitimacy as its bombs continue to fall on Ukrainian cities, generates new revenue to finance the war, and implicitly weakens the effectiveness of sanctions," she told the Italian parliament ahead of an EU summit.
In other remarks, Meloni said the 27 EU countries should appoint "an authoritative figure" to negotiate for Europe over the war.
It came as Russia struck a number of energy facilities in Ukraine overnight, Kyiv mayor Vitali Klitschko said, with president Volodymyr Zelensky accusing Vladimir Putin of "disrespecting" Trump's so-called energy ceasefire.
Wednesday morning's strikes came hours before Zelensky was due to meet prime minister Andy Burnham for talks at No 10.
Full report: Russia arrests Japanese journalist and accuses him of spying
Russian authorities have charged a Japanese journalist with espionage offences after arresting him for allegedly collecting information on the nation's defence capabilities, in the latest sign of rising tensions between Moscow and Tokyo.
Yuki Imai, a reporter for Hokkaido Shimbun, was detained in the far eastern region of Sakhalin, Russian foreign ministry spokesperson Maria Zakharova said.
The Russian intelligence agency FSB published a video showing masked personnel storming into a hotel room and pinning Mr Imai to the floor before handcuffing him.

Russian Ppesident Vladimir Putin will discuss cybersecurity with top officials on Wednesday, the Kremlin said.

Ukraine attacks Russia's biggest data centre
Ukraine has launched an attack on Russia's biggest data centre, owned by the country's largest bank and technology company, Sberbank, a senior company executive said on Wednesday.
"We confirm that the data processing centre was attacked. At the same time, all of Sberbank's systems and services are operating normally and without disruption," Sberbank First Deputy CEO Alexander Vedyakhin told reporters.
The strike in the Saratov region on the Volga River followed three attacks in the past week on data centres owned by Russian tech group Yandex. Ukraine says it is responding to Russian attacks on its own data sites.
Along with Yandex, its main domestic rival, Sberbank is Russia's leading developer of artificial intelligence. The domestic market is shared between Sberbank's GigaChat model and YandexGPT.
Trump 'blindsided' his own Ukraine peace envoys by agreeing diesel deal with Putin: report
The United States promised Ukraine extra post-war security guarantees just before Donald Trump agreed to lift sanctions on Russia for diesel exports and upended the negotiations, according to reports.
Volodymyr Zelensky has been emphatic about the need for security assurances in any deal agreed with Vladimir Putin amid concerns that Moscow is already ignoring a supposed energy ceasefire announced by Trump earlier this week.
During six-hour talks in Miami last weekend, special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner suggested that the president was willing to strengthen protections for Ukraine as part of a revised peace plan, according to The Telegraph.
However, the discussions were frustrated after Trump agreed to allow Putin to export diesel to the US and globally amid rising fuel costs worldwide.

Russia admits striking energy infrastructure in massive overnight attack
Russia's defence ministry has confirmed its forces struck seven Ukrainian energy infrastructure facilities, as well as a data centre, in a massive overnight attack.
Moscow claimed it had targeted facilities which benefited the Ukrainian military, saying the attack was carried out with ground-launched missiles and long-range strike drones.
The strikes are a clear violation of a "ceasefire" announced by Donald Trump on energy targets on Sunday, which Ukraine has said it will observe. Russia has not previously commented directly on the claimed truce.
Russia's defence ministry said in a statement that its forces had struck two heat and power plants in Kyiv, the Ukrainian capital, another such plant in the wider Kyiv region along with a thermal power plant and an electricity substation, and a heat and power plant in Kharkiv along with a thermal power plant which it said supplied the Ukrainian military in the southeast of the Kharkiv region.
