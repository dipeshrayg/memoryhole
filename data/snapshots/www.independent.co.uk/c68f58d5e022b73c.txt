What to know about Amazon drone deliveries with service set to expand
- Amazon is seeking permission from the Civil Aviation Authority to extend its Prime Air drone delivery service across the whole of Darlington.
- The tech giant launched a limited UK trial in County Durham in February, completing over 500 deliveries with a best time of 39 minutes.
- David Carbon, vice-president of Prime Air, stated that the ultimate goal is to roll out the rapid drone delivery service across the entire UK.
- Eligible customers with a garden, yard, or driveway can receive items weighing under 5lb within two hours at no extra cost under Amazon Prime.
- Amazon defended its safety record following a minor incident in July when a drone landed safely after making contact with an object extending from a vehicle.
