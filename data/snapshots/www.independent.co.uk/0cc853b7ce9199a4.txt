Trump donor turns on Kimberly Guilfoyle as $100K scandal explodes
- Kentucky attorney and former MAGA donor Eric Deters announced plans to sue U.S. Ambassador to Greece Kimberly Guilfoyle and her legal team for defamation.
- The legal dispute follows reports that Guilfoyle asked Deters for $100,000 to pay her American Express credit card bill shortly before her July 2025 confirmation hearing.
- Deters strongly denied claims by Guilfoyle's lawyers that the money was for unpaid consulting fees, alleging she asked him to fabricate documentation to cover the payment.
- President Donald Trump and Secretary of State Marco Rubio are reportedly considering whether to fire Guilfoyle over the scandal.
- Guilfoyle's legal team issued a statement denying any wrongdoing, though a senior U.S. official indicated a decision on her future is expected this week.
