President Donald Trump is set to deliver his "closing argument" to midterm voters at the iconic Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool in Washington, D.C. - a site dogged by the administration's botched repair job.
The monument has been largely sealed off to the public after a $14 million renovation caused the pool's "American flag blue" coat to peel. Pieces of the blue coating were seen floating in the water, while algae blooms turned the pool an unsightly green in the summer.
Trump has chosen to speak at the site for his final pitch so that he can also tout his efforts to renovate landmarks across the nation's capital, a White House official told Axios.
The president is due to hold nine telerallies in addition to the 2 p.m. speech in D.C. in a final sprint to the finish on Election Eve.
Elsewhere, in Michigan, the Senate race has been dominated by Democratic Rep. Haley Stevens' stunning endorsement of Republican Mike Rogers over Democratic nominee Abdul El-Sayed. Stevens has been accused of an enormous "betrayal" after she endorsed Rogers at a church service in Detroit Sunday.
Over 50 million Americans have already voted in this year's midterms, outpacing 2022's early turnout rate.

Top story: Democrats rocked by 'despicable betrayal' on eve of midterms vote
Senior Democrats rounded on Rep. Haley Stevens Sunday after the defeated Michigan primary candidate broke with her party to endorse Republican Senate candidate Mike Rogers on the eve of the midterms.
Stevens announced her endorsement in a video over the weekend, withdrawing her support for Abdul El-Sayed, the Democratic nominee and her former rival, after a falling-out over Israel.
"As you may have seen, our Democratic nominee for U.S. Senate, Abdul, said I was no longer welcome in his campaign and that I should stay away, and he's right," Stevens said. "And that's why I am endorsing Mike Rogers for the U.S. Senate today."
Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer reacted with fury, writing on X: "This is a despicable betrayal from Rep. Stevens. Senate Democrats are united in our support for Abdul El-Sayed, because we know he will fight for the economic interests of working families in Michigan, unlike Mike Rogers, who is a tool of Donald Trump and billionaire donors."
Former House speaker Nancy Pelosi was also angry, posting on the same platform: "Haley Stevens has chosen to dangerously betray Michigan families' healthcare, freedoms and future with her horrific endorsement of Mike Rogers. Abdul El-Sayed must win - because Michigan and our country deserve better."
It did not go well for Stevens on the streets of Detroit either.
