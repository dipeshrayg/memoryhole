Donald Trump was left to hide out in his private residence at the White House as the midterm results flooded in late on Tuesday, handing his administration a humiliating verdict on the last two years in office.
The Democrats took control of both the Senate and the House of Representatives as voters in conservative bastions shunned Trump's Republican party, with even Texas turning blue after decades of GOP dominance.
"It's very, very dark in the residence," one senior official told The Daily Mail of the mood, as top advisors and allies gathered for a watch party in the East Room. "It's a dark scene."
Another source close to the president told the newspaper that Trump had gone silent by 11 p.m., incensed by the blue wave sweeping away his strongholds.
"He's watching Fox News barely uttering a word and paralysed into silence," the source said, as the president took to social media to blame the network for giving too much airtime to his opponents.
Trump's initial response to the results had been to blame the candidates themselves, before posting a picture of a MAGA-style red hat with "VETO" written on it.

Karoline Leavitt makes surprising Trump admission after disastrous midterms for Republicans
Karoline Leavitt conceded that rising gas prices impacted the Republican Party's position in the midterm elections as Democrats secured dramatic victories in the "Blue Tsunami."
The US president had promised low gas prices during his last presidential campaign, insisting costs would come down to $2 a gallon.
However, fuel costs have skyrocketed since Trump joined forces with Israel to attack Iran in February, leading to a collapse in the US president's approval ratings in the weeks leading up to the vote.
Speaking to Fox News, the former White House press secretary said: "Nearly every incumbent president party loses the midterm elections... But I do believe undeniably that the history and the environment was worsened by the prices at the pump."
