England and New Zealand collide in a crunch Nations Championship clash as the All Blacks look to respond to a shock defeat to Wales.
Dave Rennie's tourists were beaten in Cardiff for the first time in 73 years, but bring back a host of stars for this visit to Twickenham as they seek a response. The head coach admitted that he and his squad had "let the country down" in that Wales performance, heightening the pressure as they take on an England side relatively untested this November.
The hosts have swept aside Australia and Japan in impressive fashion across the last two weeks, but know the intensity will ratchet up significantly with the All Blacks in town. Having beaten their opponents this time last year, England are seeking to conquer New Zealand in consecutive games for just the second time in history - and the last side to do that went on to win the World Cup...

Lewis Moody joins England to stare down All Blacks haka
The former England captain Lewis Moody joined Maro Itoje and his squad to stare down New Zealand's haka in a spine-tingling pre-match display at Twickenham.
Moody unveiled his latest charity challenge this week to raise money and awareness having been diagnosed with motor neurone disease last year..
The England squad are wearing personalised boots in support of the former flanker against the All Blacks, and were joined by the 2003 World Cup winner as their visitors laid down their traditional pre-match Maori challenge.
