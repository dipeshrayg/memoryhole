After a closely fought battle by several Hollywood stars, Robert Pattinson has officially replaced Daniel Craig as James Bond.
The actor, whose credits include The Batman, the Twilight franchise and The Odyssey, will add the British spy to his list of credits, calling the opportunity "the honour of a lifetime".
In a statement shared by Amazon, who will release the new film, Pattinson said: "James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honour of a lifetime. I'm deeply grateful to everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me.
?To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn't be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team."
The actor's casting brings to a close years of feverish speculation over who will play 007, after Daniel Craig formally announced his departure from the role in 2017. Names including Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Henry Cavill, Theo James, Idris Elba, Paul Mescal, James Norton, Callum Turner and Jacob Elordi have all circled the role in the past near-decade.
