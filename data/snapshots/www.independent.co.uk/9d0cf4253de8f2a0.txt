Could Andy Burnham be Britain's last ever prime minister? The UK's devolved leaders think so
- Leaders from the SNP, Plaid Cymru and Sinn Féin have signed a joint agreement in Cardiff pressuring Downing Street to permit independence referendums.
- Scottish First Minister John Swinney declared Prime Minister Andy Burnham would be the last UK leader, calling for a referendum within five years.
- The signed memorandum asserts that Westminster's control is ending and demands the British government prepare for constitutional change.
- Downing Street rejected the pact, stating prime minister Andy Burnham is focused on the cost-of-living crisis rather than constitutional debates.
- Sinn Féin vice president Michelle O'Neill highlighted comments by Donald Trump supporting a united Ireland as evidence of momentum for change.
