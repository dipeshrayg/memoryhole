Dripping with Christmas decs and resplendent with marble, this hotel boasts one of the most impressive entries in the capital, plus 334 elegant rooms and grand suites, and a tucked-away cocktail bar
Location
You are right in the heart of Bloomsbury, surrounded by famous squares and gardens (learn more about these in the beautifully illustrated books in your room), and close to the shops of Oxford Street. The British Museum is a five-minute walk through leafy Russel Square. Russel Square tube station is right outside the hotel, and the major transport hub, King's Cross, is a 19-minute walk or an 11-minute taxi ride away.
The vibe
This is one of the finest hotels in London, and you feel it as soon as you walk through the marbled entryway. Built in 1900 by architect Charles Fitzroy Doll, who went on to work on the interiors of the Titanic, the impressive French Renaissance style of the building was inspired by Paris's Château de Madrid and features decorative thé-au-lait terracotta cladding. Inside, a monochrome Zodiac mosaic floor and a sweeping Pyrenean marble staircase set the tone for this luxury stay, while the lobby area is a riot of multicoloured marble columns, with quiet nooks set with tables and velvet banquettes that make the perfect spot to stop for a coffee or a Martini.
By the lobby lifts, a trio of impressive glass chandeliers hang, and throughout the hotel, enormous marble fireplaces stand. This is one of the capital's grand dame hotels.
A model of the hotel's impressive Victorian red brick facade sits in the lobby; it was created last year in support of the children's charity Coram, and guests helped meet the £10,000 target.
Service
Front desk staff were efficient and friendly, and waiting staff were professional. There was a wedding going on during our visit and it was busy but staff always made time to see to our needs and requests.
Bed and bath
There are 334 light-filled rooms, including 39 suites. These are all done out in elegant neutral colours with a light-touch 1920s vibe, with luxurious fabrics, plush sofas in the suites and pops of navy. Rooms go from snug singles to family rooms with double and single beds, and interconnecting options. Bathrooms have industrial-style Crittall shower doors, chrome fittings and white subway tiles.
Suites feature Carrara marble bathrooms with brass fittings, rainfall showers and standalone roll-top baths. For a special stay, book a Bloomsbury Suite overlooking leafy Russell Square Gardens. These feature a unique roll-top bath situated in the corner turret, almost entirely surrounded by shuttered windows.
All rooms and suites have a Nespresso machine and tea-making facilities, minifridges with complimentary soft drinks, beer and crisps, and the chargeable drinks include pre-mixed cocktails, miniature wines and spirits.
Food and drink
Fitz's Russell Square is at the heart of the hotel and encompasses three venues. The grand Fitz's Brasserie, where breakfast, lunch and dinner are served in a light and airy setting; Fitz's Parlour, accessed through the bar or brasserie, which is a more low-key spot for an after-work drink, serving bar snacks, wines, cocktails and more; and the glamorous, late-night spot, Fitz's Bar, which is tucked behind a secretive curtain off the lobby and features an enormous glittering disco ball, grand fireplaces and cosy velvet-lined nooks and the main event - a backlit bar. The current "Spectrum" menu at Fit'z Bar features 12 unique expertly crafted cocktails made with inventive ingredients, each conceptualised around a single colour. The lobby area also serves a Martini and unlimited fries menu, with the house Martini costing £17, which for a five-star London hotel, feels like good value.
Breakfast at Fit'z Brasserie includes a hot and cold buffet, with all the ingredients to build your own full English breakfast, plus pastries, yoghurt, fresh fruit, nuts and granola, plus continental meats, cheeses and salads. You can also order eggs any way. There is also a hot food menu, including smoked salmon, Eggs Benedict, and more.
At lunch and dinner, guests can order bar snacks; starters, including charred pumpkin or steak tartar and mains, including various cuts of 28-day aged steaks, slow-cooked lamb shank, a classic beef burger, Meantime beer-battered haddock, korma, and other elevated British classics.
A Titanic-inspired afternoon tea is served in the brasserie, which was designed by the hotel's architect, Charles Fitzroy Doll, in 1898. He then recreated the look of the Kimpton's brasserie for the Titanic's first-class dining saloon.
Facilities
There is no spa at the hotel, but in-room complimentary Stay Well wellness menus boast LED face masks, Theraguns, and Recovery Wave Boots, which you can order from the front desk staff. There is also a list of paid-for extras that can be ordered, including face masks, and guests can access a Swell Sound Therapy app and a Technogym app, which lets guests access Yoga and Pilates workouts and more.
Free wifi throughout and laundry service (chargeable). The hotel has a 24-hour gym with Technogym equipment and Peloton® bikes, and there are yoga mats here and in the suites.
The hotel owns two Raleigh bikes, which guests can borrow to explore the local area.
Accessibility
Kimpton Fitzroy is better than most for accessibility. They offer the following amenities and services: accessible self-parking, accessible entrances and public spaces, accessible rooms and restrooms, accessible showers and baths, communication features, accessible restaurants, accessible meeting spaces, an accessible exercise facility, and closed captioning or decoders. Contact the hotel to find out more.
Pet policy
The hotel policy is that as long as it fits in the lift, it's allowed! Guests often bring along their dogs, cats, rabbits and even birds. Kimpton partners with Marleybones, which includes pet room service for dogs.
Family-friendly?
Extra roll-out beds are £50 for kids up to 12, but cots for babies are free. The hotel provides kids' bathroom robes, slippers and plush toys and is very welcoming to families, but has more of an adult vibe.
Check-in / Check-out
Check-in from 3pm; check-out at 12pm.
