White House uses Breaking Bad and The Simpsons clips to promote Trump TV
The White House has released a promotional video for Trump TV featuring clips from popular shows and films, including The Simpsons, Family Guy, Home Alone, Modern Family, Breaking Bad, and many more.
Officials said on Monday, 21 September, that Trump TV is now streaming 24/7 "with top past moments, announcements, and the latest and greatest from the administration all in one place."
It comes after Trump banned reporters from CNN, Politico and MS NOW from the White House, triggering a lawsuit from the outlets, which argues the ban violates the Constitution.
The White House television pool also said on Monday it would suspend coverage of presidential events in response to the restrictions.
