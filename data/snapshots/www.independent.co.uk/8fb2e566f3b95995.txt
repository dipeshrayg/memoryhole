Strictly Come Dancing 2026 has reached its halfway point, with just 10 couples left in contention for the Glitterball trophy.
Following the elimination of Emmerdale star Lawrence Robb last week - after the show's first-ever bottom three dance off - the competition is tighter than ever, with six different couples topping the leaderboard so far.
Things are particularly tense at the bottom as Jaime Winstone, who has been in several dance-offs, will automatically make it through to the next round due to an injury in rehearsals.
This week's performances will see a smorgasbord of dances, including a Cha Cha by Delta Goodrem, a Rumba by Lacey Turner, a Quickstep by presenter Will Best and a Paso Doble by current favourite Tabby Stoecker.
Follow live updates below...
Jaime Winstone to miss tonight's episode
Actor Jaime Winstone is set to miss this weekend's shows after tearing an abdominal muscle.
The BBC announced the news on Wednesday (4 November), sharing that doctors have advised the actor to rest this week.
"In line with the rules of the show, Jaime and Carlos will receive a bye through to next week when it is hoped they will be able to dance again. We wish Jaime the very best as she continues her recovery," a spokesperson said.
Results show to honour Remembrance Sunday
With this week's results show occurring on Remembrance Sunday (8 November), Strictly will mark the occasion with a themed dance celebrating the women of the Second World War.
