Strictly Come Dancing 2026 has reached its halfway point, with just 10 couples left in contention for the Glitterball trophy.
Following the elimination of Emmerdale star Lawrence Robb last week - after the show's first-ever bottom three dance off - the competition is tighter than ever.
Things are particularly tense at the bottom as Jaime Winstone, who has been in several dance-offs, will automatically make it through to the next round due to an injury in rehearsals.
This week's performances featured a smorgasbord of dances, including a Cha Cha by Delta Goodrem to a Jennifer Lopez hit, a Rumba by Lacey Turner, a Quickstep by presenter Will Best and a fiery Paso Doble by current favourite Tabby Stoecker.
Meanwhile, Dani Dyer's famous father, Danny Dyer, made a cameo as she and her professional partner Nikita visited the family's caravan park, before wowing the judges with their American Smooth and receiving their first "10" of the competition.
Dani and Nikita ended up topping the leaderboard with a total of 36 points, while John and Katya, Cach and Maddie came in joint last with 26 points each.
