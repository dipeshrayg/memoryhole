'Several women' can't speak out about Russell Brand because of NDAs
- Harvey Weinstein whistleblower Zelda Perkins has urged media companies to dissolve non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) following the conviction of Russell Brand.
- Brand was earlier this week sentenced to 10 years and six months in prison at Southwark Crown Court after being found guilty of rape and three counts of sexual assault.
- Perkins told the News Agents podcast that she is in contact with several women who remain unable to speak out due to NDAs signed with Brand and media organisations.
- She suggested that executives proactively release victims from these contracts privately, allowing them to seek therapy and confide in friends or family.
- The Metropolitan Police have confirmed they are investigating additional allegations against the comedian following his trial.
