Body of man who had head severed remains missing despite arrests
- Mary Fitzmaurice, 45, and her mother Georgia Fitzmaurice, 75, have been arrested in California in connection with the murder of 73-year-old Paul Fitzmaurice.
- The victim's severed head was discovered inside a plastic bag in a wooded area in September 2022, but he was never officially reported missing.
- Investigators identified the remains in 2024 following advanced forensic genetic genealogy testing and assistance from the FBI.
- Authorities allege the women hid his death from family and neighbours to continue collecting his pension and Social Security payments.
- Police executed a search warrant at the family home in Citrus Heights on Tuesday, though the remainder of the victim's body has yet to be found.
