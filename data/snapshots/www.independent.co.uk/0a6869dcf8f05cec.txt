Clara Amfo speaks out on Strictly audition: 'They gave us code names'
- BBC star Clara Amfo has revealed details of an intense audition process to become a presenter on Strictly Come Dancing.
- The tryouts followed the departures of long-standing hosts Tess Daly and Claudia Winkleman.
- Speaking on her podcast, Clara & Munroe Are Into It, Amfo explained that she auditioned as a pair alongside an unnamed woman.
- She described strict secrecy measures, including assigned code names such as "Glitter" and "Sparkle" and transport in secret cars using fake show names.
- Amfo praised the new hosting team of Emma Willis, Johannes Radebe and Josh Widdicombe.
