UK weighs in on EU's Rwanda deportation plan for asylum seekers - and doesn't rule out joining
- Five EU member states are set to unveil plans to deport rejected asylum seekers to Rwanda.
- Downing Street has refused to rule out joining the controversial scheme, stating the UK will watch developments "with interest" as it explores innovative approaches to illegal migration.
- Unlike the UK's previous Rwanda policy, the proposed European model applies exclusively to migrants who have exhausted all legal avenues to remain.
- The original British Rwanda initiative was scrapped by Sir Keir Starmer after costing £700 million and resulting in only four voluntary removals.
- Human rights organisations have warned of significant legal and humanitarian risks, questioning whether such a move will act as an effective deterrent.
