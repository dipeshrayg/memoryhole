Barcelona fined for Julian Alvarez transfer approach
- Barcelona have been fined €602 (£503) by the Spanish FA after approaching Julian Alvarez without notifying Atletico Madrid in writing.
- FIFA dismissed Atletico Madrid's separate complaint regarding Barcelona's conduct, saying it had no jurisdiction over the matter.
- Julian Alvarez apologised to Atletico supporters for expressing a desire to join Barcelona in an interview during the 2026 World Cup.
- The Argentine striker remained at Atletico Madrid despite interest from Barcelona and Premier League club Arsenal during the summer.
- Alvarez scored the winning goal against Deportivo La Coruna to move Atletico within two points of La Liga leaders Barcelona.
