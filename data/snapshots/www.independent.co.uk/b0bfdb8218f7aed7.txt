Up to 15 per cent off with the Europcar student discount
Make that dream road trip a reality or make moving flats more manageable with Europcar. It works directly with Student Beans and Grad Beans to offer students and recent graduates up to 15 per cent off car hire. A 10 per cent discount is also available in Spain, Italy, France, Portugal, Germany, Belgium, Australia and New Zealand.
Students need to verify their eligibility with their university email address. Graduates from the past five years can qualify by providing documentation of a recognised course of at least 12 months, dated within that period.

Get 25 per cent off premium protection insurance
Saving money on car hire is smart, but making sure you're properly covered is smarter. Europcar's premium protection insurance safeguards you from accidents or theft - and if you book in advance, you can save up to 25 per cent on your coverage.
Drivers aged 26 and older qualify, with collision damage and theft waivers reduced to zero excess, according to Europcar.
Rent by the hour
Whether you need a one-way ride to the airport or a luxury car for a night out, renting by the hour gives you flexibility. Europcar also offers hourly van hire, which is ideal if you're staying close to home or quickly picking up large items that don't fit in your vehicle.
Free cancellation
Most bookings come with free cancellation up to 48 hours before your pick-up time at Europcar - any later incurs a fee of £45. The only packages that don't come with free cancellation are light packages.
