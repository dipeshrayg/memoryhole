Celia Imrie has admitted feeling "slightly cross" with her former Better Things co-star Mikey Madison, suggesting the actor has failed to properly acknowledge creator Pamela Adlon for "discovering her".
In the US comedy-drama series, Imrie featured as grandmother Phyllis "Phil" Darcy, while Madison played her granddaughter Max Fox, and Adlon starred as Imrie's screen daughter Sam Fox.
Following her role in the show, Madison, 27, joined the Scream franchise and earned a Best Actress Oscar for her performance in the 2024 indie comedy Anora.
Appearing on the Table Manners podcast, Imrie described meeting director Quentin Tarantino, who instantly recognised her from Better Things, prompting her to reflect on Madison's career.
Detailing the interaction, she recalled: "I went up to him because you ought to say something if you meet a hero and he turned around and he said, 'Why is your daughter so horrible to you?'
"And I thought, there you have it, and that's where he saw Mikey (in Better Things).
"And I don't mind saying this actually, but I just was sad that Mikey didn't give Pamela more credit. I'm slightly cross with her."
She added: "Because she hasn't given proper credit to darling Pamela, who discovered her."
Tarantino later cast Madison in his 2019 release Once Upon A Time In Hollywood.
During the podcast, Imrie also spoke about her stint on the debut series of The Celebrity Traitors, alongside participants including Stephen Fry, Charlotte Church, Joe Marler, and eventual winner Alan Carr.
Characterising the reality competition as a "cruel" concept, she observed: "Even though we watched it, I suppose I didn't quite realise what I was going into; funnily enough, I asked (host) Claudia (Winkleman) because, you know, I think it's quite cruel, but it's a brilliant game."
Madison's representatives have been contacted for comment.
