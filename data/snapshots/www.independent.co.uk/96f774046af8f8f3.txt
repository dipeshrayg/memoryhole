Lidl is cutting the price of several vegetables to just 8p as the supermarket rolls out a series of festive food deals aimed at helping shoppers keep the cost of Christmas dinner down.
The discount chain says that households can put together a traditional Christmas meal for £1.36 per person, with offers on vegetables, turkey and popular trimmings arriving in stores ahead of the big day.
The deals will be available from 19 December until Christmas Eve, giving shoppers a limited window to pick up some of the staples of a festive roast at reduced prices.
Among the offers is a 3kg Birchwood British Small Turkey for £8.85, alongside a selection of vegetables priced at just 8p each.
The supermarket says the discounts are part of its efforts to make festive food more affordable while maintaining the prices paid to its suppliers.
Which Christmas vegetables are 8p at Lidl?
Shoppers will be able to pick up four different vegetables for just 8p each as part of Lidl's Christmas price cuts.
The offers include 2kg of white potatoes, 500g of sprouts, 1kg of carrots and 500g of parsnips, all priced at 8p.
The deals could help households planning to serve a traditional Christmas dinner, with vegetables often among the many items shoppers need to buy for a festive meal.
Lidl is also reducing the price of several other accompaniments. A 170g pack of sage and onion stuffing mix will cost 39p, while 300g of meat gravy granules are priced at 89p.
A 15-pack of Yorkshire puddings will be available for 40p, providing another option for shoppers looking to fill out their Christmas dinner without spending as much.
How much will a Christmas dinner cost at Lidl?
Lidl claims its festive promotions mean shoppers can serve a full Christmas dinner for £1.36 per person.
With the cost of Christmas food, gifts and other seasonal spending adding up for households, supermarkets have increasingly used festive promotions to attract shoppers looking for value.
The retailer says its promotional pricing is entirely funded by the supermarket itself, meaning farmers will continue to receive the same full, fair contracted price throughout the season.
According to Lidl, the offers form part of its promise to remain competitive on price without compromising on the quality of its products.
The Christmas food deals will be available in stores from 19 December through to Christmas Eve.
