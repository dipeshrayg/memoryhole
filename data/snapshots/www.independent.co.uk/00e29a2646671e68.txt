Another Saturday meant another brand new episode of Strictly Come Dancing, with more show-stopping routines, more sequins and more fake tans - we wouldn't have it any other way.
With no theme, week three delivered a truly eclectic collection of dances, including a Charleston to Gina G (yes, really) and a quickstep to "I'm a Believer". And there was a huge range when it came to the scoring too, with 18 points between the best- and worst-performing celebrities.
At the top of the leaderboard, Delta Goodrem scored 33 for her Disco-themed Salsa, which landed the first nine of the series from Motsi Mabuse.
Cach Mercer also impressed with his ambitious Couple's Choice set to Raye's "Beware... The South London Lover Boy" before Tabby Stoecker closed the show with a sensual Argentine Tango that left Craig Revel Horwood almost speechless.
At the other end of the leaderboard, and possibly in trouble in tonight's results show, were Melanie Walters - who struggled to master the Charleston - and Shaun Wright-Phillips, whose quickstep didn't include many quick steps. So who will be facing off in the dance off tonight?
