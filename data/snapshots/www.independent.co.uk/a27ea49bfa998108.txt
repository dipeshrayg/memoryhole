Reverse Brexit: New campaign launched to get Britain back into the European Union
- A new umbrella campaign named Get In! is set to launch to lead efforts to take Britain back into the European Union.
- The citizen-led campaign brings together major pro-EU organisations, including European Movement UK and Best For Britain.
- Get In! serves as a direct counter to Stay Out, a pro-Brexit campaign group recently launched by Lord Michael Gove.
- The group plans to highlight economic growth, proposing a slogan claiming rejoining the EU could yield £520 million a week for UK public services.
- The push comes as debate over Britain's relationship with Europe re-emerges following recent UK-EU agreements on trade barriers and youth mobility.
