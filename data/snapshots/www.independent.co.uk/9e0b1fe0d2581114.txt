UK energy firm EnQuest exits North Sea to pivot focus to south-east Asia
- UK energy firm EnQuest has agreed to sell its UK North Sea upstream business to Neo Next+ for up to $960 million (£729 million) as it shifts focus to south-east Asia.
- The producer cited an uncertain UK investment environment and high taxation as key reasons for transitioning away from the low-return basin.
- The exit follows similar moves by other major energy companies, including BP, which put its North Sea assets up for sale earlier this year.
- Shadow energy secretary Andrew Bowie criticised the Labour government's policies, claiming extended windfall taxes and licensing bans are driving energy firms away.
- EnQuest chief executive Amjad Bseisu said the transaction will allow the firm to focus on lower-cost, high-growth opportunities across Malaysia and south-east Asia.
