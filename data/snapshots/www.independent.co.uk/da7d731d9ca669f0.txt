Whether you're run down, jet lagged or feeling a cold coming on, the best greens powders could be the vitamin boost your body (and immune system) is in desperate need of.
The "greens" in questions are often a blend of nutrient-dense vegetables, fruits, and algae such as spirulina, wheatgrass, and kale powders in these plant-based supplements. From there, many formulas are fortified with iron, zinc, and vitamins C and B12, alongside digestive enzymes, gut-supporting probiotics, and adaptogenic mushroom extracts. While whole foods remain vital for health, daily consumption of a green powder can aid with digestion, alleviate bloating, and elevate natural energy levels.
But as its a booming and growing market, finding a greens powder that suits you can be tricky. To start, it's best to identifying your primary health goal - whether that is targeted gut health, a metabolic lift, or clearer skin - is an ideal starting point. Then it often comes down to budget, which is why my extensive evaluation covers everything from luxury, all-in-one investments like AG1 to highly effective high-street budget buys. Over the course of my testing period of many months, I tried the UK's leading greens powders to evaluate their flavour profile and nutritional density, plus how well they mix, to help you make an informed choice. Keep reading for my verdict.
