This week's poll
According to some of those who work/worked at AI company Anthropic, AI has the capacity to "kill all humans" within a decade. Alignment science lead Evan Hubinger posted on X this week: "We really do earnestly believe AI could kill all humans! I personally think it is >10% within the next decade. I believe Anthropic is trying its best, but we do not yet have a plan to solve alignment for superintelligence and are not clearly on track to." So, how worried are you? Do you think it's all nonsense and scaremongering - or are you really very worried about the rise of the machines? Let us know in our new poll...
