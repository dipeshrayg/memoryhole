Rescue helicopters were called to a UK beauty spot after a light aircraft crashed into a loch on a training flight.
Emergency services responded to reports of a glider crashing into Loch Leven near Kinross just after 11am on Friday.
A spokesperson for the gliding centre confirmed that the glider was on a local training flight after taking off from Scottish Gliding Centre.
Two coastguard helicopters joined the emergency response, a spokesperson confirmed. It is not known if anyone was injured in the crash but police have said their inquiries into the incident are ongoing.
A Police Scotland spokesperson said: "Around 11.05am on Friday November 20 2026, we received a report of a microlight glider having crashed into water in Kinross.
"Emergency services are in attendance and inquiries are ongoing."
A spokesperson for Scottish Gliding Centre said the incident is currently under investigation by Air Accident Investigation Branch of the Department for Transport and the police in conjunction with the British Gliding Association.
They added: "Our club is co-operating fully with these organisations. At the present time there is no indication as to the cause of the accident."
The Maritime and Coastguard Agency said: "Two HM Coastguard helicopters joined emergency services responding to an incident at Loch Leven, reported at about 11.20am on November 20."
