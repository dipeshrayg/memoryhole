Bellingham hailed as a 'role model' after defending female reporter
- Real Madrid midfielder Jude Bellingham has been praised after stepping in to ensure BBC Sport reporter Elizabeth Conway could finish her post-match interview.
- Conway was interrupted by a male reporter in the mixed zone following Real Madrid's 5-1 Champions League win over PSV Eindhoven.
- Bellingham intervened by telling the male journalist, "Sorry, she's got one more", allowing Conway to ask her follow-up question.
- Conway described the mixed zone as intimidating and chaos, praising the England international as polite and respectful, with social media reaction afterwards calling Bellingham a role model to all young men.
- Bellingham came on at half-time to score a 25-yard volley and provide an assist, maintaining Real Madrid's 100 per cent winning record in the Champions League phase.
