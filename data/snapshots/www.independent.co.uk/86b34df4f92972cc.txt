Moment Pete Hegseth is reprimanded by soldier at Arlington cemetery
- An on-duty guard publicly reprimanded members of Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's entourage for laughing and taking selfies at the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier during a Veterans Day visit on Wednesday.
- Pentagon Spokesman Sean Parnell acknowledged the incident, stating that a soldier correctly reminded the group to maintain proper decorum.
- The Arlington incident occurred shortly after Hegseth narrowly escaped an impeachment vote in the House of Representatives regarding unauthorized military operations in Iran.
- The impeachment resolution was defeated 211-204 after 11 House Democrats failed to cast their votes due to severe Capitol traffic jams, sparking criticism from colleagues.
- Hegseth continues to face mounting political pressure and bipartisan criticism following significant Republican midterm election losses attributed to the administration's military policies.
