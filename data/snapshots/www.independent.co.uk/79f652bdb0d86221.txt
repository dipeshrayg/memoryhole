Katie Price's ex-husband Kieran Hayler has been found not guilty of raping and sexually assaulting a 13-year-old girl.
Hayler, 39, was charged with three counts of rape and one count of sexual assault following alleged incidents in West Sussex in 2016.
He was acquitted on Thursday following a trial at Lewes Crown Court.
He was also cleared of four alternative counts of sexual activity with a child.
Hayler's turbulent relationship with Ms Price, to whom he was married from 2012 to 2021, was laid bare during the court proceedings.
He accused the media personality of setting him up with "fabricated" allegations, saying that it was a "smear campaign against me".
On the stand, Ms Price denied being "angry and vengeful" towards her ex-husband.
The jury, made up of four men and six women, returned the verdicts on Friday after nearly 15 hours of deliberation.
Hayler appeared emotional in court, and bent over as he was told by the judge that he could leave the dock.
The complainant first contacted the NSPCC in 2021 over the allegations, and the charity referred her to the police. She is not part of Hayler or Ms Price's extended families.
The complainant's police interview was played in court during the trial, in which she alleged that Hayler told her that sex was a "special thing" and cried before raping her.
Speaking after the alleged third incident, the complainant told police: "I think by that point I knew it wasn't quite right, he kept telling me to 'shh', I had to be secretive and not tell anyone."
She said he told her: "You can't talk about it, you can't tell anyone, I might get in trouble for this."
She told police: "I tried to forget it for so many years."
Hayler was first interviewed by police in 2022, and consistently denied the allegations.
The court heard that Ms Price sent the complainant a text message asking if Hayler had kissed her and saying: "I will make you a little star."
Hayler said he believed that Ms Price was "coaching" the complainant.
