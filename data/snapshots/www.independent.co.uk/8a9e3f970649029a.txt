What the polls are saying ahead of seismic election in Israel
- Israeli prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu's far-right coalition is unlikely to retain power following next week's election, according to a poll of 4,000 voters by Agam Labs.
- The right-wing Haredi bloc, which includes Netanyahu's Likud party, is projected to win 52 to 54 seats, falling short of the 61 required for a Knesset majority.
- The opposition party Yashar, led by Gadi Eisenkot, is projected to win the largest individual share of seats with 20, though liberal parties overall remain short of a majority.
- With neither main bloc likely to secure a controlling majority, potential outcomes include a unity government, an alternative coalition supported by Arab parties, or a repeat election.
- Under Israeli election law, the publication of political polls is prohibited after Friday leading up to the vote on 27 October.
