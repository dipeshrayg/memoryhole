Gypsy Rose Blanchard has spoken out about the death of her fiancé and the father of her child, Ken Urker. He died on his 34th birthday.
Blanchard, 35, confirmed Urker's death in a statement to TMZ Thursday night, saying: "Ken was an amazing father and a man who was deeply loved."
She added that he "will forever be my soulmate and my red string, a connection that time, distance, and even death can never erase."
Blanchard and Urker's relationship began as penpals when she was serving time in prison for her role in her mother's death in 2015.
