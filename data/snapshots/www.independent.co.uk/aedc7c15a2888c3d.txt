Arsenal outgoings - could Zubimendi be on the move?
With Arsenal splashing £75m on Bruno Guimaraes, Mikel Arteta may be required to sanction some sales.
That has led Mundo Deportivo to report that Martin Zubimendi, who only joined Arsenal last year, could leave the club, but only if a potential suitor matches their £77m asking price.
Meanwhile, West Ham are exploring a move for Reiss Nelson who has entered the final year of his contract, as per Sky Sports.
Another Chelsea-Strasbourg loan on the horizon
Filip Jorgensen is set to move to Strasbourg on loan, according to Sky Sports, becoming the latest Chelsea player to make that jump to the Alsace sister club.
Aston Villa in talks for Palhinha - but face competition from Newcastle?
Aston Villa transfer chief Damian Viadagany has confirmed the club are in talks to sign Joao Palhinha from Bayern Munich.
He told Bild: "We are in talks, but there is currently no agreement.
"If something should happen, it is always on the basis of the great respect we feel for FC Bayern.
"We are friendly clubs; our relationship with Max Eberl (Bayern's director of sport), Christoph Freund (Bayern sporting director) and all other responsible persons is excellent.
"Therefore, I cannot and do not want to comment on any further details."
This comes amid reports that Newcastle are also keen on the Portuguese midfielder, who was on loan at Tottenham last term, joining Pierre-Emile Hojbjerg on the list of potential Bruno Guimaraes replacements.
Palace getting busy
As well as potentially signing Dwight McNeil in a swap deal, Crystal Palace are interested in Monaco midfielder Lamine Camara and Torino winger Alieu Njie, according to Sky Sports.
Youssef Chermiti stretchered off amid Galatasaray interest
News emerged before Rangers' clash with Hibernian on Sunday that Galatasaray had seen a bid rejected for striker Youssef Chermiti, with the Gers unwilling to sell.
However, the Turkish side's pursuit of Chermiti may now be over after he was stretchered off in that game.
Man Utd working over deal for EFL Apprentice of the Year
Manchester United are working on a deal to sign Leicester City starlet Louis Page, according to BBC Sport.
Page, an 18-year-old midfielder, was named the EFL Apprentice of the Year last term and is an England U20 international.
Hull announce seventh signing of summer
Sweden wing-back Elliot Stroud has joined Hull from Mjallby for an undisclosed fee on a four-year contract.
Shea Charles set for Fulham medical
Southampton midfielder Shea Charles is set to undergo a medical at Fulham after the clubs agreed a deal worth up to £30m, according to the Press Association.
The 22-year-old is expected to sign a five-year contract at Craven Cottage and become the most expensive Northern Irish footballer.
Fulham will pay an initial fee of £26m, while the transfer includes a further £4m in performance-related add-ons.
Ipswich bid for Exequiel Palacios
Ipswich Town have sent in a bid for Exequiel Palacios, according to Fabrizio Romano.
The Argentinian, who is among their key targets, is also being pursued by four other clubs in the Premier League and Bundesliga.
Another big name set for Turkey move
Mohamed Salah's switch to Trabzonspor may have knocked over the first domino of big names making moves to Turkey this summer.
That's because now, Fenerbahce are set to finalise a deal to sign Belgium striker Romelu Lukaku from Napoli, as per Gazzetta dello Sport.
Chelsea will receive a sell-on fee.
