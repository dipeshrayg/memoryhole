Who is Sharon Rooney? Scottish actor taking on The Celebrity Traitors
- A new series of The Celebrity Traitors launches this evening, hosted once again by Claudia Winkleman.
- Participants will compete as Faithfuls or Traitors to win up to £100,000 for their chosen charities.
- Sharon Rooney is among a star-studded cast including as Michael Sheen, Richard E Grant, Michael Sheen, Maya Jama and Ross Kemp.
- Scottish actress Rooney, 37, made her name after starring in Channel 4's My Mad Fat Diary where she played Rae Earl alongside Jodie Comer.
- She most recently starred in the 2023 blockbuster Barbie alongside Margot Robbie.
