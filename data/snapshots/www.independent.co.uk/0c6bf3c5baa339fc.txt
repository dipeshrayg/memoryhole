Stunning aerial views of New York City marathon captured by police helicopter
A police helicopter captured stunning shots of the New York City marathon as thousands of runners took to the streets of the Big Apple on Sunday (November 2).
The New York Police Department chopper posted footage showing marathon competitors making their way from the start in Staten Island across the Verrazzano-Narrows Bridge into Brooklyn.
A record of almost 60,000 runners competed in the event with Andreas Almgren of Sweden and Sheila Chepkirui of Kenya finishing as the first man and woman respectively.
