Who: Spain vs England
What: UEFA Nations League, Group A3
Where: Metropolitano Stadium, Madrid, Spain
When: Sunday, November 15, at 7:45pm (19:45 GMT).
How to follow: Al Jazeera Sport will bring you all the build-up from 16:45 GMT before our live text commentary stream.
England have the chance to avenge their defeat and end Spain's 43-match unbeaten record when they face the reigning world champions in Sunday's final Nations League group encounter.
Spain staged a 3-2 comeback victory at London's Wembley Stadium in late September, as Lamine Yamal scored an early opener and Mikel Oyarzabal finished the job.
Spain and England have both sealed their berth in the competition's quaterfinals, with La Roja assured of the top spot should they avoid defeat on Sunday. England, meanwhile, will finish second if they fail to beat the two-time World Cup winners.
Teenage sensation Yamal, with four goals, and Mikel Merino, with three, have headlined Spain's attacking charge, while newly-crowned Ballon d'Or recipient Harry Kane leads England.
Before Spain vs England, here's everything you need to know:
How have Spain played in the Nations League?
Spain have a perfect record and lead Group A3 with 15 points.
They beat England 3-2 in the first game, an away trip, and followed it with a 4-1 rout of Croatia at home.
Then, they beat Czechia 3-1 at home, followed by a 2-1 away win over Croatia and a 2-0 away win over Czechia.
How have England played in the Nations League?
England are second in Group A3 with 12 points from five games, having won four times and lost once.
Their Nations League campaign started with a 3-2 home defeat to Spain before Thomas Tuchel's side beat Czechia 2-0 and thrashed Croatia 7-0 in back-to-back away fixtures.
Later, they beat Czechia 3-0 at home and Croatia 2-1.
Tuchel eager to end Spain's unbeaten run
Tuchel believes England are ready to end Spain's unbeaten record when they face the world and European champions in Madrid.
While victory offers little more than the prospect of a more favourable Nations League quarterfinal in March, it would be a landmark moment for England if they could end Spain's run of 43 matches without defeat.
"It (Spain) is a team that has forgotten how to lose, so of course it requires a top performance first of all, and if we can deliver this performance, it will give us a big boost," said Tuchel.
"I think we're there, and I think we're ready to win on Sunday.
"I know what it takes, and no one can ever guarantee it, but I think it's the moment to check how far we are and to push our limit and proof of concept," the German added.
"It's important that we play with the same mindset that we played at home, and attack them and try to make it a physical game, and try to impose our strength."
'We want to get to 44': De la Fuente
De la Fuente said their unbeaten record provided his players with extra motivation.
"We want to keep setting new milestones, and this is another one," he explained.
"None of us would probably ever have thought we'd even reach 35, because we already felt those were astronomical numbers, but having reached 43, you think, my goodness, we want to get to 44, of course we do."
What happened the last time Spain and England met?
Spain came from behind to beat England 3-2 in an entertaining Nations League encounter on September 26, denying the hosts a cathartic win after their painful World Cup exit.
Yamal put Spain ahead in the second minute, but Anthony Gordon equalised, and Kane added another as England ended the first half with a 2-1 lead.
The Three Lions captain missed a penalty in the second half, though, allowing Spain to bounce back, as Alex Baena found the equaliser before Oyarzabal sealed victory in the 74th minute.
