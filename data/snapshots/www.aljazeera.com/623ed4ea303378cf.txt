Rescue efforts are under way in the Gulf of Finland after a Russian cargo ship was hit by drones close to Russia's maritime border.
The ship, the Sparta II, is on fire and has now drifted into Finnish territorial waters, authorities say.
Finland's Ministry of Defence said its forces had detected a drone attack on a Russian vessel in international waters, with the vessel sustaining damage, and dispatched Finnish Border Guard units to the scene.
A photograph taken by a Border Guard patrol plane shows flames and thick smoke coming from the rear of the ship.
The attack happened west of Gogland Island on Saturday morning, with Finland temporarily instating air and sea traffic restrictions in the eastern part of the gulf.
Finland's Border Guard said the vessel is known to have links to the Russian armed forces.
According to the Border Guard, the crew of the Sparta II have not been forced to abandon ship, and there has been no environmental damage so far from the drone attack, while the vessel is in the open sea off the southern coastal town of Loviisa.
"The Border Guard is preparing to evacuate people and assist in firefighting operations," Finnish authorities said.
It is not clear who was behind the attack, but Finnish Prime Minister Petteri Orpo wrote on X that "war is spilling over into our immediate vicinity. The Finnish authorities are handling the situation in the Gulf of Finland, where a drone strike has hit a Russian merchant vessel. Ukraine has the right to fight back against the aggressor."
Alexander Drozdenko, governor of Russia's Leningrad region, said the damage had yet to be fully assessed.
"Three nautical miles from the Russian Federation's border, a cargo vessel bound for Kaliningrad was attacked by two drones," he wrote on the messaging app Max.
Kaliningrad's Governor Alexey Besprozvannykh said supplies to the Russian exclave were not at risk.
The Gulf of Finland is a narrow arm of the Baltic Sea between Finland and Estonia, both NATO members. It connects Russia's former capital of Saint Petersburg with its Kaliningrad enclave by sea.
Finland's Ministry of Defence said air and sea traffic restrictions had been put in place as a precaution in case further drones needed to be intercepted, adding that Finland had notified its allies of the incident.
Ukraine and Russia have attacked each other's shipping throughout the war, mostly in the Black Sea.
Earlier this month, Finland's military said it had detected a strike on a Russian warship in the Gulf of Finland, which Ukraine took credit for. Helsinki said it had raised concerns with Kyiv about striking in the area afterwards.
