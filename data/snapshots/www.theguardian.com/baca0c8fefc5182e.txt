Jobe Bellingham and Josh King called up by England
Jobe Bellingham and Josh King are the headline names after Thomas Tuchel handed both uncapped midfielders call-ups to his England squad for the Nations League games against Croatia and Spain. Borussia Dortmund's Bellingham, 21, joins his brother Jude in the senior set-up after impressing as captain of the Under-21s.
King's rise has been just as rapid. The 19-year-old has become a fixture in Fulham's midfield this season, and his two Premier League goals make him their top league scorer. He travelled with England's preparation squad before the summer's World Cup and has kept catching the eye for the Under-21s.

We need more of it, for sure."

Portugal pledged to honour Cristiano Ronaldo as manager Jorge Jesus named his first squad since the 41-year-old announced his international retirement.
Jesus faced a barrage of questions about the rift that preceded the departure of Ronaldo after the forward announced his retirement decision on social media on Thursday, ending a Portugal career spanning 234 appearances and 146 goals.
Portuguese FA president Pedro Proenca promised to celebrate Ronaldo's contribution, although there was no word on whether the forward would agree to a farewell appearance. "No tribute will ever be enough to thank him for everything he has done for Portuguese football, for Portugal," Proenca said before the squad announcement.
"We will do everything we can to honour the legacy he leaves us. Everything he has done deserves to be honoured and celebrated."
Jesus selected seven new faces, including five players from the Under-21s, for November's Nations League matches against Wales and Denmark. Reuters
Arsenal midfielder Max Dowman has been called up to England's Under-21 squad for the first time.
The 16-year-old, previously capped at Under-17 and Under-19 level, is already on the radar of senior boss Thomas Tuchel owing to his achievements at Arsenal. Dowman has now been named in Lee Carsley's group for games against Portugal and France this month.
With captain Jobe Bellingham promoted to the senior set-up, midfielders Trey Nyoni, Harrison Armstrong and Divine Mukasa remain in the group following their step up from the Under-20s. Brighton's Jack Hinshelwood is named in the squad after missing the last camp due to injury
Tottenham striker Mikey Moore, who is on loan at FC Koln, also receives his first call-up. Will Lankshear, the striker who has scored 15 goals in 16 games since joining Middlesbrough from Spurs in the summer, has not been included after missing Boro's midweek clash with Wrexham through injury.
Carsley's men play friendlies against Portugal on Friday 13 November and France three days later, both at Ashton Gate as they prepare for next summer's Under-21s European Championship.
Luis de la Fuente has called up three uncapped players for his Spain squad to take on England and Czechia in the Nations League.
Jacobo Ramon, who played in Como's record-equalling 9-1 Champions League win over Lens, plus Real Betis pair Alvaro Valles and Marc Roca are in line for debuts after being named in a squad missing the injured trio of Rodri, Eric Garcia and Dani Olmo. AFP
Nottingham Forest boss Oliver Glasner has warned his players they are not at a "spa and wellness hotel" and has demanded they improve on a difficult start to the campaign.
Forest welcome Manchester City to the City Ground on Saturday, with the visitors looking to bounce back themselves, following successive Premier League defeats.
Glasner says he is still trying to create a culture at his new club:
We are not a spa and wellness hotel where you like to relax and enjoy. But of course, as I mentioned, we are also not the military. We need to find in-between. What I want is to get every player having this intrinsic motivation, to want to improve his game.
'Come on, coach, tell me what do I have to do to get better?' And this is what I hope, and I'm pretty sure we will create. That we will be more challenged by the players in the future.
Dedic set to escape Newcastle sanction over carrying bow and arrow
Newcastle's Amar Dedic is unlikely to face a club sanction after being spotted holding a bow and arrow in the Northumberland countryside and sparking a major police pursuit involving an armed response unit.
Dedic is expected to start Newcastle's match at Fulham on Saturday following an incident that at one point saw six patrol cars pursue the Bosnia right-back back to his home on the upmarket Darras Hall estate, just outside Ponteland.
A bit more Maresca on Nico O'Reilly, who has been left out of the latest England squad. The Manchester City manager is not concerned about his form and believes the 21-year-old could benefit from a rest during the international break:
It's what he needs. With Nico, it's quite easy. Since we started the season, between his back (injury) before the Coventry game and some more problems unfortunately, he's not 100%.
In the last game I really liked him, I thought he was playing a very good game and this is what we need from Nico.
Let's just keep him fit because we know how good he is and no doubt in the future that he will be back with the international team. Now use that break to do the right things in terms of therapy and these kind of things and be 100% after the international break.
Manchester City boss Enzo Maresca is confident goalkeeper Gianluigi Donnarumma will get back to his best with little guidance in his native Italian.
Donnarumma's form has been under scrutiny after a number of mistakes in recent games. Maresca has continued to back his first choice throughout his troubles and has no plans for in-depth conversations with his fellow Italian. He said:
It's much easier for me with the language! We are (having conversations) but there are players who when they make mistakes, you need conversations with them and there are players that when they make a mistake you just need eye contact to understand that he recognises and he knows.
With Gigio it's quite easy because every time he has made some mistake or something that was not correct, he recognises immediately. I spoke with Gigio but sometimes you don't need to be so deep with some players because they know they have made a mistake and recognise that. Just try to avoid that and move on.
Overall he's good. He's quite relaxed in terms of he recognises that he has done some mistakes but at the same time he has also made some good saves. It's just a matter of balance - try to avoid mistakes and continue to do the right things.

Bayern Munich coach Vincent Kompany is on track for a "full recovery" after a lengthy absence following his collapse at a training session, his assistant Aaron Danks has confirmed.
Kompany has been out of action for two weeks since collapsing at a reserve team training session on 23 October. He was hospitalised and treated for meningeal irritation, which is an inflammation of the tissue around the brain or spinal cord.
"I've had a few really good phone calls with Vince," Danks said. "He seems very positive and appears to be well on the way to a full recovery. The international break provides a good opportunity to reflect and see when he can return."

First of all Lee [Carsley] is speaking very highly of him for the Under-21s for a while now. He is the standout player. He found his way and his rhythm with Dortmund, where he has had an excellent start to the season. He's a regular starter there and very influential and there's an opportunity.
There is an opportunity coming through the pathway, coming on his own merit, Jobe deserves to make an impressive for us. We're happy to have him in camp and see what he can do for us.
On the first question of Palmer's absence, Tuchel said:
It's enough No 10s and we stick to the guys we had in camp. Camp was excellent and we were happy with the last two and half weeks we had togther. The impact of the players was immense throughout the whole camp and that's why we stick mainly with the group.
We have Josh King in there, but I want to see him on both wings. He's kind of an extra player that we use, we have training numbers and he can cover all eventualities because Josh can play all four positions, so that's basically the reason.
