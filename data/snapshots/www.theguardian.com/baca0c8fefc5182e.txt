As well as missing United's home match against Villa on Sunday, Baleba is now likely to also sit out the trip to Liverpool on November 22 as well as matches against Sporting Lisbon and Brentford before the end of November.
"Carlos, we don't know exactly [the severity], but it'll be a number of weeks yet," Carrick said. "Definitely not this week and not over the international break, it will be a little bit longer yet."
