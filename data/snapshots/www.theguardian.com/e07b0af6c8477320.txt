An Inglis century and a flurry of wickets late in the day has Australia in control as South Africa close the third day on 140-4 in their second innings
Here is Geoff Lemon's day three report from the third Test at Newlands ...
If Temba Bavuma was decisive for South Africa as he batted through day after day in Gqeberha, his fall in the shadow of stumps on the third day might prove as decisive at Cape Town. Bowled by a Nathan Lyon classic with eight balls to go, he saw his team reach stumps at 140 for 4, with a lead of just 40 to build on.
Stumps: South Africa 140-4, lead by 40 in their second innings
Another compelling, gripping, dramatic day of Test cricket comes to an end.
Australia well on top thanks to Inglis' ton and some pretty ordinary batting from the South Africans.
Staring at a 100-run defecit, Markram and then Bedingham threw their wickets away before Ackerman was bounced out by Starc for 62.
Bavuma dug in before he was bowled by Lyon playing a rare, and loose, attacking shot late on.
Rickelton, who survived this last over from Starc, the 47th of the innings, will start tomorrow on 8 having picked up two fours alongside Breetzke who was watchful for his 19 off 59 balls.
Batting isn't easy, certainly not on this pitch which is offering great turn, but it's a heck of a lot harder when you gift wickets.
Australia's game to lose, but in this see-sawing series, there is no telling where we'll be this time tomorrow.
46th over: South Africa 131-4 (Breetzke 18, Rickelton 0) Lyon finds Rickelton's outside edge with the first ball to the lefty, pushing off the back foot, but somehow it splits the two slippers and scampers away for four. Oh, what an over from Lyon who compelled the mistake from Bavuma to castle the SA skipper. He almost bagged two in two. South Africa clinging on with mere minutes remaining. They lead by a paltry 35.
Bavuma loses his head and loses his wicket! He's played within himself all innings but this time he swishes at a full ball from Lyon and misses it as it rags out of the rough and knocks him over. Credit to the bowler, who has toiled and tested and challenged for almost 14 overs, but that must go down as another South African who has tamely given his wicket away. I'm not entirely sure where Bavuma was trying to hit that. Lost his shape and he's left the ground with his team in a deep, deep hole.
45th over: South Africa 129-3 (Bavuma 33, Breetzke 18) Starc on for his fourth spell of the day. One more push from the big lefty who has produced some magic moments this Test. He's over the wicket to Breetzke for just a ball before switching to around as he'll look to angle it in and perhaps get it stand up, or shoot through, off the pitch. Breetzke drives away from his body, though he meets it with the full face as he eases it for one down to the sweeper before Bavuma does likewise.
Remarkable speeds from Starc, touching 150km/h even at this late stage. What a warrior he is!
44th over: South Africa 127-3 (Bavuma 32, Breetzke 17) Just the one over for Renshaw as Lyon returns, which makes sense to me. The shadows are lengthening. Such a crucial period in this Test. Bavuma drives twice, which Lyon won't mind, especially as he finds the mid-off fielder on both occasions. A maiden from Gaz.
43rd over: South Africa 127-3 (Bavuma 32, Breetzke 17) Cummins has moved himself a metre off the pitch, standing at a close-in short mid-on. I like it. That means Hazlewood can attack the stumps, which he does after forcing Breetzke to duck under a bouncer. Hazlewood misses his line and offers width and Breetzke eyes it up quickly and eases a lovely square drive to the boundary. Survival is key, but so are runs and the South Africans won't want to miss out on loose stuff. An on drive is hit straight to Cummins to close the over.
42nd over: South Africa 123-3 (Bavuma 32, Breetzke 13) Renshaw replaces Lyon. Again, I don't quite get that. My guess is that the change of pace - he's slower through the air than Lyon - and the fact that Renshaw is a part-timer might cloud the minds of the South Africans. He's also taller, so perhaps a little extra bounce might be found. Bavuma steers into the covers for one but Breetzke is trapped on his crease for three balls. An outside edge squirts past Marnus, who hasn't stopped yapping for a second, and they get another single.
41st over: South Africa 121-3 (Bavuma 31, Breetzke 12) Hazlewood targets the stumps. He almost gets one to burst through Breetzke's defence. A slightly shorter one is met with a firm forward press and they take a cheeky single past the bowler. For Bavuma they move the midwicket to a very wide leg gully, but the short ball doesn't get up and Bavuma tucks it into the newly created gap for one. The bumper to finish the set is down the leg side.
40th over: South Africa 119-3 (Bavuma 30, Breetzke 11) I like this from Lyon, who brings up his cover and tells the Saffas, 'hit me through the field if you want something'. That might compel them to try and hit it harder. It certainly cuts off the easy single. There's an appeal for lbw as Lyon drops on onto the rough and finds Breetzke's pad from his big forward press. Five dot balls come before a stiff push into a gap that allows them to through for a single.
39th over: South Africa 118-3 (Bavuma 30, Breetzke 10) Hazlewood now. He's around that nagging length, looking to bring it back it back into Breetzke off the deck. He then attempts a yorker, or had one slip out his hand. Either way, it gets to Breetzke on the full and os bunted down the ground but there's no run. Dangerous balls, those. Breetzke flicks a single down to fine leg.
Right then. We're a long way away, but what would South Africa want to defend? And what would constitute a 'tricky score', beyond 'something to bowl at'?
38th over: South Africa 117-3 (Bavuma 30, Breetzke 8) Both batters show a bit more intent as they force Lyon off his length by making big strides to meet the ball and negate the spin. Rather than play from the crease, they're able to steer four singles across the over, two down the ground to long on and two through the vacant cover. Better, but hard not to feel like one Lyon delivery will have their name on it.
37th over: South Africa 113-3 (Bavuma 29, Breetzke 7) Even on this pitch, Cummins is able to deliver searing bouncers. For me, he has the best bumper in the game. One cannons off an uncomfortable length and catches Bavuma's glove as the batter looks to protect his head. It could have gone anywhere. Instead ot went over the close leg gully and away for a single.
36th over: South Africa 112-3 (Bavu,a 27, Breetzke 7) Lyon is getting so much turn out of the rough outside off for the right-handers. Bavuma shoulders arms and watches it spit back onto his pads. Lyon appeals. And though it looks totally speculative, it underlines how much it's ragging. Bavuma gets a single away, making room and plinking a cut through cover. South Africa lead by 12 but the crucial thing for them is to not lose another wicket.
35th over: South Africa 111-3 (Bavuma 26, Breetzke 7) Cummins bowls a rare loose ball in this innings, dragging it down to Bavuma who pulls well in front of square for four. Cummins doubles down and then lands a ripper of a bouncer that takes Bavuma by surprise, causing him to hop and duck and pull out of the pull shot. He then works a single past midwicket.
34th over: South Africa 106-3 (Bavuma 21, Breetzke 7) Lyon has Breetzke pinned in his crease, looking pretty ungainly as he does his best to keep gary the Goat out. It's gripping, it's spitting, it's turning. Marnus won't shut up behind the stumps. The fielder at forward short leg is hovering. But he survives the maiden.
33rd over: South Africa 106-3 (Bavuma 21, Breetzke 7) Cummins back on. He's pounding away back of a length before he over pitches slightly and Breetzke sends it back where it came from with a lovely drive for four past the bowler. Cummins brings his length back and finds a little seam. It kept low, and if Breetzke missed that he'd have been plumb. Instead a thick inside edge sees it squirt down to fine leg for one. Cummins to Bavuma and it's a gem, he finds the edge but it doesn't carry! Renshaw, stooping forward, gathers it on the half volley.
32nd over: South Africa 101-3 (Bavuma 21, Breetzke 2) Lyon back. This Australian attack just doesn't let up. Bavuma punches off the back foot to bring up his team's 100 and level the scores. But crucially South Africa have lost three wickets which means they're basically 0-3. That becomes 1-3 when Breetke bat-pads a single past the forward short leg. Bavuma is almost caught in the same area as Lyon is extracting sharp, really sharp, turn. Harmer will like seeing that. The key thing, though, is how many runs will he have to defend?
31st over: South Africa 99-3 (Bavuma 20, Breetzke 1) Starc is peppering Breetzke with some short stuff. When he goes fuller he finds the outside edge, but soft hands means it lands well short of Green at second slip. Bavuma takes a single at the start of the over to close the gap to just one.
30th over: South Africa 98-3 (Bavuma 19, Breetzke 1) Breetzke is off the mark with a clipped single off Connolly. Bavuma takes two of his own. Marnus Labuschagne is whooping and hollering after every ball. He got some stick when he was batting and he's giving it right back. Which is fair enough with his team well on top. At drinks, South Africa still trail by two with their top three back in the hut.
29th over:South Africa 95-3 (Bavuma 17, Breetzke 0) Starc breaks the game open once again after a 64-run stand threatened to swing momentum back towards South Africa. But now the Aussies are firmly back on top. A hayuuuuuuge innings is needed from both these Saffas. Carey and Inglis put on 185 together. Something similar is desperately needed here.
WICKET! Ackerman c Green b Starc 62 (South Africa 95-3)
Starc bounces him out! What a delivery from the left-armer who returns after a change of ends and sends the set batter packing with a ripper! From over the wicket, it keeps going on with the angle, cramping him up as he tries to sway out of the way. A slight edge then a ricochet off the helmet before it looped back towards the cordon where Green make a simple catch. They considered reviewing but missed the deadline, which is just as well as the decision would have stood. South Africa effectively -5 for 3.
28th over: South Africa 95-2 (Ackerman 62, Bavuma 17) Renshaw into the attack. Finger spin from both ends as they look to exploit a perceived weakness from Bavuma, who was dismissed by Renshaw in Durban. The Saffas start by milking him for five comfortable singles to bring the deficit down to just five.
27th over: South Africa 90-2 (Ackerman 59, Bavuma 15) Connolly's left-arm spin joins the part, replacing Hazlewood. Again, I'm surprised by that, but I'm sure Pat Cummins knows a lot more than I do. It's a maiden, though a drag down should have been put away by Bavuma instead of cut straight to backward point.
26th over: South Africa 90-2 (Ackerman 59, Bavuma 15) Starc replaces Lyon, which surprises me. I'd have thought Lyon would bowl out the day as he looked so threatening. Anyway, here comes the big lefty. A full ball invites a drive from Ackerman. He nails it, but Inglis is sharp at cover. Then Starc bumps him and he takes on the pull. It's a thin edge, or a glove, but it's just beyond Carey who can only watch it skip away for four. But that really could have gone anywhere. Ackerman gets another opportunity to drive. He doesn't quite time this one like the last, but finds the gap in front of cover and takes two.
25th over: South Africa 84-2 (Ackerman 53, Bavuma 15) Hazlewood is relentlessly at Bavuma. A bumper is ducked. A back-of-a-length nipper is shoots up and hits the splice of the bat. A tempter outside the off stump is left alone. It's a maiden, with no attacking shots, yet all of cricket was contained in that microverse.
24th over: South Africa 84-2 (Ackerman 53, Bavuma 15) These batters are looking nervy against Lyon. Bavuma rocks back and pulls, but it doesn't quite get up and he just about manages to smear it away for one. Then Ackerman charges - recklessly if you ask me - and smokes it right at short extra. I thought for a second it was a catch but it was hit straight in the ground. Two singles closed the over.
23rd over: South Africa 81-2 (Ackerman 52, Bavuma 13) Really good from Hazlewood who is doing what he does best. Off a great length, a little bit of seam back into Bavuma, he cuts him in half twice, thwacking his body and pad with some delicious movement off the deck. A bit of width allows Bavuma to steer a cut to the sweeper at deep point and that brings up the 50 stand between the two. Their job together has only just begun.
22nd over: South Africa 80-2 (Ackerman 52, Bavuma 12) Ackerman skips down the track, thwacks Lyon through the covers. It's in the air, and as it left the bat every South African in the crowd would have held their breath. Lyon then tempts him with a floater wide of the off stump but Ackerman resists. A single down the ground brings up his 50. Well played Marques. A single from Bavuma brings Ackerman back on strike who goes deep in his crease to cut for two in front of the sweeper at deep point. Phew, another eventful over.
21st over: South Africa 72-2 (Ackerman 45, Bavuma 11) Hazlewood into the attack, replacing Cummins. Two singles to start before he bangs in a bumper to Ackerman from round the wicket. There's a short leg and men in the deep, but he takes on the pull and finds the rope at backward square. Then he dinks a single to deep third. He's playing well, and playing with confidence. Bavuma is brought forward by Hazlewood who finds the edge but it lands short of Marnus at second slip. Oh my goodness, it's so tense!
