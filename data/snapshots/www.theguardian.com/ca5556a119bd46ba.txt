The only result from this round of championship matches came 1min 45sec into the day when Matthew Potts cracked the four required to give Durham a 10-wicket win against Gloucestershire at Chester‐le‐Street. Gloucestershire, who had refused to bowl spin on Saturday night amid bad light, therefore extending the game into yesterday, chose to open with Ollie Price's fruity loops, with the winning runs coming off his sixth ball.
Durham's head of cricket, Ryan Campbell, was phlegmatic. "I guess it's a bit ironic that they opened the bowling with a spinner this morning, but these things happen. We don't worry about the opposition. We do things our way, and it was delayed, but same result."
Durham cement their place at the top of Division Two, 40 points clear with four rounds left. Things have more of a wooden-spoonish tinge for Gloucestershire, bottom with nine defeats in 10 games.
