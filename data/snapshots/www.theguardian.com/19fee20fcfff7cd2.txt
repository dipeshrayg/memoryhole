'No, no': Carrick denies Manchester United are unmanageable
When Michael Carrick was asked if he can trust the players to deliver against Bournemouth at Old Trafford given the last week, under-pressure Carrick said: "I definitely trust them, yeah, 100 per cent I trust them. It wasn't a very good night at all for so many reasons. We all know that. I think that's pretty obvious. I definitely trust them, yeah. I trust them. We'll be a different team on Sunday. We have to be a different team on Sunday."
Asked if he felt that the team and club are unmanageable, as some have suggested, Carrick retorted: "No, no".
He then spoke of his relationship with Sir Alex Ferguson and Ole Gunnar Solskjær, for whom he was key player and assistant coach. "I speak to both of them quite regularly, to be honest. There's nothing in particular from that. I'm close to both of them and we do have regular conversations, so it's nothing different this week."
On Bruno Fernandes: "I think you've got to be careful with just saying 'not running'," he said. "As I said, we've won a lot of games over a period of time and this pressing thing, that suddenly everyone thinks about pressing.
"Pressing is one small part of football. There's all sorts of different ways of what people are classed as a press. What actually is it? What you're doing it for? Is it to your strength? Is it covering up other spaces? It's just thrown about as a bit of a word at the minute of pressing.
"You don't necessarily need to press to win games of football. Sometimes you do, sometimes you don't."
