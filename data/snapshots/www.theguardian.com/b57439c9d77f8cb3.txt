GREY DAWNING WIN LAUNCHES HUGE CELEBRATIONS AT HAYDOCK
Grey Dawning, the 11-10 favourite, joined the illustrious list of dual winners of Betfair Chase at Haydock on Saturday, prompting a big Saturday crowd to sign the praises of Dan and Harry Skelton after a convincing defeat of Jagwar in the first Grade One event of the British season.
Harry Skelton, Grey Dawning's rider, was always perfectly positioned and travelling well against the rail as first Affordale Fury and then Jagwar led the field through the first two-and-a-half miles.
Skelton then made smooth progress to challenge Jagwar between the third- and second-last fences. Jagwar raised a second effort to jump alongside Grey Dawning at the last, but Skelton had more than enough in hand to go four-and-a-quarter lengths clear at the line.
A crowd packed half-a-dozeen deep around the paddock serenaded the winner with a chorus of "Walking in a Skelton Wonderland" as Skelton returned to unsaddle.
"This is very special," the rider said. "One day when I am not riding, I will always remember this place. Those are the memories you do it for. He was fantastic out there today and he never put a foot wrong."
Dan Skelton too was swept up by the atmosphere and reception for his grey chaser.
"It is great to be involved in such iconic horses and it is just an amazing achievement from the horse and a fabulous ride from Harry," Skelton said. "Harry is riding at the top of his game and he does more for me than you will ever appreciate, and it is a fantastic team effort."
Grey Dawning finished fourth in last season's Cheltenham Gold Cup, beaten around 16 lengths behind Gaelic Warrior, and is expected to head instead to the Ryanair Chase, over two-and-a-half miles, at next year's festival meeting.
"The truth is we tried for a Gold Cup and we weren't quite good enough," Dan Skelton said. "The Ryanair is a Grade One, it is the marquee race on the Thursday and this horse is definitely better in a Ryanair than a Gold Cup and that's where he will go."
Grey Dawning can be backed at around 10-1 for the Ryanair Chase, in a market headed by Willie Mullins's Fact To File at around 5-1.
