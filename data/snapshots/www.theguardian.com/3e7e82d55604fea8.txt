Russell had another good run in Friday's FP3, but just missed out on a hat-trick:
Max Verstappen denied Mercedes rival George Russell a clean sweep of practice for the Azerbaijan Grand Prix by going fastest for Red Bull in Friday's final session ahead of qualifying.
The Dutchman clocked a 1min 43.922sec lap in the closing minutes of the hour-long session to pip Russell by 0.099. Lewis Hamilton also pulled out a late flier to go third for Ferrari 0.111 seconds off Verstappen's pace.
Kimi Antonelli, the runaway championship leader, was fourth. He lost valuable track time to a hydraulics problem on Thursday. Reuters
Russell did go off the track at one point, into a run-off area at turn seven. It's another tricky street circuit.

Right, half an hour till qualifying. What has been happening so far in Baku? Thursday was a decent start for George Russell, as Giles Richards reported:
Russell opened well in Azerbaijan, quickest in first practice in a session where Antonelli lost half his run time with a hydraulic problem. In the afternoon Mercedes were once more on top, looking ominously quick on the street circuit which is energy-starved and plays to their car's strengths in energy harvesting and deployment. Russell was quickest again, a full half a second up on Antonelli with their nearest competitor, Red Bull's Max Verstappen, eight-tenths back in third.
Russell has not given up hope on the title front:
"I'm going to keep fighting at every single race until it's mathematically impossible," he said in Baku. "I want to try and win every single race and put it on pole at every single race. That's my goal and that's what I'm working towards every single weekend."
Welcome to Friday F1 qualifying. No, this is not Las Vegas, but the result of Sunday being Azerbaijan's Remembrance Day. So from 1pm BST the 11 teams and 22 drivers will be chasing grid positions for a race that starts at noon BST on Saturday.
