New Zealand will be disappointed to lose but their performance level was sufficient to win that match nine times out of ten. They probably would have won tonight if Hughes had not overcooked his kick on the last with five minutes to go.
If this is the final pairing in a month's time the Kiwis will head to Brisbane with plenty of confidence, especially after the way James Fisher-Harris, Joseph Tapine, Moses Leota and co dominated up front.
Their greater concern coming out of the night will be the fitness of Kalyn Ponga. He is their focal point and game breaker. If he is out for an extended period he cannot be replaced like-for-like, it will require a reshuffle that could unsettle the balance of the side.
The Kangaroos were far from vintage. The forward pack was soundly beaten, Harry Grant was off key, Latrell Mitchell was barely involved, and it required the calf injury to Mitchell Moses and the introduction of the dynamic Tom Dearden to bring some energy to the halves.
The defensive effort was heroic though, keeping New Zealand at bay for long spells. And then they struck clinically when opportunities presented themselves. Two intercept breakaway tries and the Reece Walsh solo stunner cannot be legislated for.
Kalyn Ponga is walking around in a sling. He clearly damaged something when he collided with Cam Munster in the second half. He played on, scored a mind blowing try, but eventually succumbed to the injury shortly before time. Hopefully for him and NEw Zealand he isn't ruled out of the tournament.
"This should be the final, what a game," emails Ashley Baxter. "I've not watched rugby league for a long time after being brought up on it but moving to Wales my focus became Union. But this game is sensational."
It still may well be the final, Ashley. What tonight's result means (other than bragging rights) is the Kiwis will have the more difficult of the semi-finals.
The two exhausted groups of players embrace. They all know they've played their part in a classic. They also know it's only the opening round of a tournament that could well see them meet again in a month's time to determine the world cup winners.
