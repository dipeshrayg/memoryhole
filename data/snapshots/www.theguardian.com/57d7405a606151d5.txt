It would be unfair to say that Australia let a winning position slip on the fourth day of the third Test at Cape Town, only because that would undersell how Ryan Rickelton and Wiaan Mulder prised it away.
Australia's vaunted four bowlers, owners of 1,674 wickets at day's beginning, could only raise that to 1678 by day's end. In the process, a lead of 40 became 287. South Africa had been 40 for 4 in the first innings at Gqeberha and bounced back to win; they were effectively 40 for 4 in the third innings here, and now have a great chance to do the same.
South Africa were in danger of losing the series today. Instead, Ryan Rickelton and Wiaan Mulder made superb, methodical centuries to take them to 387 all out. I still have no idea who are the favourites, but we should get a positive result one way or the other.
Thanks for your company and emails, and sorry again for my addled state. Geoff's report will be here shortly, and he'll be back for the start of day five. It could be a classic.
