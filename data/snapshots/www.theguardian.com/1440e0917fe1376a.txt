Mikel Arteta is hopeful the shoulder injury David Raya sustained at Newcastle is not too serious, but confirmed the goalkeeper would miss Tuesday's Champions League game against Borussia Dortmund.
Raya was forced off in the second half of Saturday's 2-2 draw with a suspected dislocated shoulder, raising fears he could be sidelined for an extended period. He had an MRI scan on Arsenal's return to London and was absent from training on Monday. Kepa Arrizabalaga is expected to deputise at home to Dortmund.
"He's not fit for tomorrow, unfortunately, but it doesn't look too serious," the manager said. "With the pain he was showing immediately after the action we were all quite worried, but he seems to be better than what we expected. There's no need of surgery, which was the main fear."
Asked whether Raya could play at home to Manchester City on Sunday, Arteta said: "Tomorrow we'll have more tests and we will know. I think we've seen what David brings to the team. That's why we are very protective with Kepa and [third-choice] Illan [Meslier]. Everybody needs to be ready because these things happen."
Dortmund are a point behind Arsenal in the Champions League after three wins from their first four matches and Niko Kovac's side lead Bayern Munich by two points at the top of the Bundesliga. Arteta said a youthful side expected to feature Jobe Bellingham - who was called up by England for the first time for the most recent international break alongside his brother, Jude - would provide a real test.
"You have to congratulate mum and dad for what they've done," Arteta said of the Bellingham brothers. "Two amazing footballers, what they're doing is remarkable. So let's give credit as well to the parents."
