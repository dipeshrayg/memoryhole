I will say, in a partial defence, I think that was pragmatism at work today. Green can play spin, he was really good in Sri Lanka a couple of years back, and did ok in India in 2023. His century there was on Route 1 at Ahmedabad, but still.
I think today they recognised that, for whatever reason, Harmer was a big threat against Green specifically, with the rough patch and men around the bat with the way Green lunges, and that Cummins is historically very vulnerable against Rabada, so they reasoned that the safest play was to have Green take the fast stuff and Cummins the slow stuff, to best apportion the batting. It means each player could concentrate on one job. As much as it's disruptive for bowlers to change who they're bowling to, it can be for batters to change who they're facing. It worked, too, Cummins faced Harmer better than anyone else in the innings.
Green's dismissal is another matter, though. That was the work of somebody who was done in his own head before he was done on the pitch.
As for Australia, what a dire performance with the bat two matches in a row. All out 99 in the previous Test, all out 111 here, both times with so much to play for. They've had batting problems for a long time, and have had their heads in the sand when it comes to selection. I'll run through all of this in more detail in a postmortem column to go up in the next 24 hours, so I won't go into chapter and verse here, but you know what I'm talking about.
Openers who aren't openers and keep changing roles, a number three who can't make a run, a number four who behaved erratically through the series and missed the last match, a keeper at six who offered far more at seven, and bit-part players at seven who've added nothing with either discipline.
You don't deserve to win matches when you put together teams like that, and they haven't done. Pressure to make some significant changes will be high.

What a performance from this home side. Underclubbed, under-resourced, underprepared, no Test matches played for most of this year, none at home for two years. They looked it in the first Test, beaten badly, and they were in all sorts of trouble in the second. Then they got fired up and got their act together, coming back to win in Gqeberha, and laying a series of body blows on Australia here.
They are at the top of the world rankings, and they are a big step closer to the next World Test Championship final to defend their title. They looked a rabble last time they visited Australia a couple of years back, they had a sixth-string side go to New Zealand after that, and they were at risk of becoming a joke. Now this team is saying, to its own board and to the ICC, let us show what we can do. Let us play this game, this format, that suits us so well.
