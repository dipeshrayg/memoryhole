"There's enough going on without it being the other end of crazy"
Some thoughtful chat about pitches in the Reporters' Network quotes last night:
Glamorgan's Zain ul Hassan, who took four for 26 in Surrey's 115 all out at The Oval:
"There have been a lot of result pitches around in these final weeks of the season and that's I think because there are quite a lot of teams either going for the Championship, trying to avoid relegation or get promoted.
"More pitches have been greener and it has been really hard for batting around the country."
And Hampshire's Scott Currie at Grace Road:
"The pitch is doing a bit but when you're at the bottom of the league, I think teams earn the right at home to do what they like with the pitches. As bowlers, we're not going to complain - I had a season on loan here and I wish the pitches had done as much then.
"We never had a wicket with shaved ends while I was here but credit to Alfonso Thomas, Claude Henderson and the ground staff here for trying to produce wickets that are doing a bit. Some balls have gone up, some have gone down, but no one's been hit. There's enough going on without it being the other end of crazy."
