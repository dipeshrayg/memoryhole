It's a bit early in the season to start talking about 'must-win' games, but this could be one of them for both sides.
Neither Spurs nor Villa have secured a victory so far in this nascent campaign, and, well, they must start stacking up some ticks in the W column before things really turn sour.
Tottenham are 17th with two points, two places and one point ahead of Villa, but they've not scored a league goal across four games. That is six hours of Premier League football without finding the net, a run that has left Roberto De Zerbi's expensively rebuilt side looking short of confidence, cohesion and, most worryingly, attacking threat.
Villa have only scored once, although they arrive with a little more encouragement after beating Coventry in the Carabao Cup in midweek. Unai Emery's side have also won four of their last five meetings with Spurs, including their most recent visit to this stadium.
Both clubs began the season with European ambitions. A win today would not resolve every problem, but it would at least provide some breathing space. Another afternoon without three points, however, would make the pressure around both clubs considerably harder to ignore.
