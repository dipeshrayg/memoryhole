Starc has had a middling two Tests matches. He also has 450 wickets, and has literally got better every year of his career so far. Cricket is a highly variable sport. Performances jump all over the place. Someone not having an impact in one game doesn't mean, when you're at the selection table, that their next one will be poor. It's about how much you rate their ability to possibly have a good game. The best players have a higher possibility rating, a greater likelihood that they will play well.
Australia have five excellent bowling options, and can pick four. That's all it is. Whoever was left out, there would be a strong case that they should play. You can say 'How could they not pick x' about everyone. It's as simple as that.
