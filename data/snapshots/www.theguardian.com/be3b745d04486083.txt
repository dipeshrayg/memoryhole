Some quotes from the Manchester City manager, Andree Jeglertz, following that shock defeat: "I think we struggled with their press in the first half and then in the second half they punished us on a couple of transitions. They ran through us too easily.
"We didn't create that many chances. We got a little bit stuck and they knew what we were doing. We didn't give the urgency I was hoping for. We've played a lot of games and I felt that in the second half we lacked a bit of energy.
"We played a very good team today and the title is far in Spring so we don't talk about that now. We talk about winning games, performing how we want to and that's what we didn't do today.
"We've a lot of games to play - great games and tough games - and we want to do better than this. I'm not concerned at all."
The Manchester City players looked stunned, dejected and a little bewildered, as they offer polite applause to their supporters who have stayed behind after the shock defeat. The home fans here had known nothing but home victories since Andrée Jeglertz took charge, but his 100% home league record has come to a crushing end here. They were second best and they will know it. Tottenham, on the other hand, as overjoyed as they celebrate in front of their fans on the far side. Are they in the Champions League race? Surely they are now.
