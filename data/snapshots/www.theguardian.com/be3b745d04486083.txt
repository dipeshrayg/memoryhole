Here's Tom' Garry's roundup of the day's WSL action.
Arsenal remain winless at home in the WSL this season after being held to another frustrating draw at the Emirates, this time by West Ham. Arsenal had 22 shots at goal but were unable to break the deadlock, as their under-pressure head coach Renée Slegers oversaw a sixth home league draw out of six, leaving Arsenal eight points behind the top three.
Manchester United left it late to come from behind and beat Birmingham 2-1 but Simi Awujo's last-gasp header from a corner gave them a valuable win and extended Eva Olid's side's club-record unbeaten run to 14 matches in all competitions. Birmingham had led inside four minutes through Lee Geum-Min's expert volley but Elisabeth Terland's close-range finish with 10 minutes to go and Awujo's 94th-minute winner snatched it, moving Olid's fourth-placed team just six points off the top.
Elsewhere, the bottom side Charlton collected their first point of the season as they drew 1-1 with Aston Villa, while Brighton moved into the top half with a 1-0 victory at Everton, courtesy of Maz Pacheco's early own goal.
Kym Little spoke to the BBC: "We all take responsibility [for not winning]. They've all been tight games and performances where we've not hit our rhythm and found the net. It's something we work on relentlessly but it's just not coming off in matches. We need to go away, look at ourselves, keep working hard and together to make sure it comes in the next game.
"There are lots of elements across the board that we can work on and we review. We need to find our way to score, whatever they may be. We'll put everything into it to be better."
West Ham's Viviane Asseyi, who had a fine game, spoke to the BBC: "Really happy. It was really a team performance. We stayed collected, we stayed together. It was not easy but we stayed compact and played together. I'm very happy."
