Five Levski Sofia supporters were taken to hospital after violence before last night's Europa League game at Marseille. Both the French and Bulgarian presidents are blaming the home fans.
The lead-up to the match at the Stade Vélodrome was marred by confrontations in the stands between the two sets of fans, including the throwing of projectiles and flares.
French president Emmanuel Macron condemned "in the strongest possible terms... acts of violence targeting Bulgarian fans", the Élysée Palace said. The Bulgarian president, Iliana Iotova, said it was "unacceptable" and "shocking".
"The attacks against the Bulgarian fans desecrate one of the great games that brings millions of people together around the world," she said in a statement on Facebook. "Everyone should be able to support their team without being afraid for their safety."
Macron spoke to his Bulgarian counterpart on the sidelines of a European summit in Brussels, assuring her that "these acts would not go unpunished", the Élysée said.
Several hundred Marseille fans ran from the stand where they were situated about 10 minutes before kick-off, appearing to pursue a group of Bulgarian supporters. They breached the stand designated for away fans, where around 500 Levski fans were gathered.
"Five people were taken to hospital with eye injuries, head trauma and a dislocated shoulder," the Marseille police prefecture told AFP. "Six others were treated on site for various wounds." AFP
