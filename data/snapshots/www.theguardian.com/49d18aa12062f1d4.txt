The unique selling point of I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here! is that it is currently the only show on television where you get to watch animal abuse and reputation-destroying arguments at the same time. The former is an ongoing affair, with petitions and boycotts rumbling on as always. But the latter has just exploded, thanks to Mariella Frostrup and all her microaggressions.
If you haven't been watching, and in this day and age why would you, here's a brief precis. Last Thursday, latecomer AJ Odudu offered to help Frostrup cook a meal, which Frostrup took as unwelcome criticism of her skills. But when they cooked together the following night, things went badly, Frostrup snapped and Odudu cried. But last night, during an attempt to clear the air, Frostrup said she had actually been glad to have had help. Odudu's's reply was essentially that she had a funny way of showing it, to which Frostrup replied: "You see, that's just aggressive and that's what keeps happening." Odudu's response was to sigh: "Please don't use those words towards me." Nevertheless, the altercation was enough for Frostrup to be called to the Bush Telegraph so producers could "discuss why what was said might make someone uncomfortable, how to address it, and how to move forward."
The spat has caused something of a backlash against Frostrup. Fellow campmate Leomie Anderson has said that, "Black women are always painted to be aggressive when we're just expressing ourselves," while Judi Love posted on social media: "For most of us black women who have been through this in all scenarios, workplace, the shop, schools, wherever, we know what AJ's feeling in that moment." Good Morning Britain host Narinder Kaur wrote on X that, "Reality TV has never been a safe space for any person of colour," while Alesha Dixon wrote: "Mariella in the bin." On the other hand, Nigel Farage has defended Frostrup, so there's that.
But if you've watched I'm a Celebrity for any length of time, you'll know that flare-ups - when they happen - tend to be slightly more pathetic than this. Here are some of the more notable instances.
Insectgate, 2015
Quite honestly, you could make a whole list from this series. The biggest, and easily the most entertaining, blow-up came when Lady Colin Campbell picked a live beetle and threw it on to a campfire. Tony Hadley from Spandau Ballet reacted badly to this, informing Campbell of her cruelty. It is not an exaggeration to say that Campbell took this particularly badly. In the ensuing melee, she called Hadley a "bully", a "liar", a "gutless creep", a "total zilch", a "greedyguts", a "dumbo", a "hypocrite", a "pretentious arsehole", "the lowest of the low" and a "chippy oik", while adding that he had "the brains of a pea and the mouth of diarrhoea".
Soupgate, 2015
Oh fine, let's have another. This time, despite her best efforts, Lady Colin Campbell ended up being outheckled. The context is that her team had just won a banquet, while the opposing side were left to go hungry. Campbell had previously purchased a can of soup from a jungle vending machine, and proposed that the best way to end the suffering of the other team would be to give them a quarter of her can of soup between them. Duncan Bannatyne objected, saying it might be fairer if they had the whole can. Campbell took this particularly badly, calling Bannatyne a "mouse" and a "little pussy". But Bannatyne fought back, calling Campbell a "nasty woman" and "the most selfish person I have ever met".
Strawberrygate, 2017
After winning the camp a reward of strawberries and cream, Iain Lee and Amir Khan decided to eat the whole thing themselves then tell the other campmates there had been no reward. A long and tense stalemate followed before Lee eventually relented. This enraged Dennis Wise, who informed them both that they were banned from eating any other future treats. It is also worth pointing out that Amir Khan was dressed as a cat when he apologised.
Sausagegate, 2003
The wildest blow-up to ever happen during I'm a Celebrity happened relatively early on in its run. John Fashanu had won a reward for the camp. However, due to either a mix-up or a genuinely dark social experiment, this reward came in the form of one sausage each. At this point, Antony Worrall Thompson became so outraged that he effectively unionised the entire camp, urging them to tear off their microphones in protest. The campmates bundled into the Bush Telegraph and watched in awe as Worrall Thompson exclaimed: "What if we all walk out tomorrow? You're fucking screwed", citing the show's own title as a precedent for their simultaneous departure. According to contemporaneous reports, producers backed down and gave him what he wanted, which was presumably two sausages.
