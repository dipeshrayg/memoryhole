Escaping Idi Amin
BBC iPlayer; available now
Summed up in a sentence Four people share their shocking testimonies of what happened when the dictator gave Asians 90 days to leave Uganda.
What our reviewer said "There is a happy ending, of sorts. Bharti, Paresh, Harminder and Maz never stopped missing the country they were born in, but they made a new home in Britain, and they made Britain better: it would be hard, for example, to calculate the size of the debt we owe Bharti for her 40 years as a social worker safeguarding children in care. Every minute of Escaping Idi Amin contains an urgent lesson to learn." Jack Seale

Netflix; available now
