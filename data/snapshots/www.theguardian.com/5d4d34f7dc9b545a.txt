Japan head coach Hajime Moriyasu will step down next week, the country's football association revealed today, cutting short his tenure in the wake of the 2027 Asian Cup's postponement.
Moriyasu, Japan's longest-serving coach, was originally set to lead the team at the Asian Cup in Saudi Arabia in January before handing the reins to current Under-23 boss Go Oiwa.
But the Asian Football Confederation announced last week that the tournament would be pushed back to December 2027 due to "exceptional circumstances" in the region, following Houthi attacks on the host country.
That left Moriyasu in limbo, with Japan Football Association (JFA) chiefs last week declining to clarify when the coaching handover would take place.
They said that Japan's friendlies in Singapore against Brazil on Saturday and Paraguay on Tuesday would be Moriyasu's last games in charge. AFP

Pochettino's confirmation as Manchester United's new head coach ends a run of near-misses that date back a decade.
He was linked with the post as far back as 2016, also coming close to being appointed in 2018, 2021 and 2022. He has long had an admirer in Sir Alex Ferguson, who praised Pochettino in a 2016 interview when the Argentinian's Spurs team were approaching their apex.
"This lad has composure," said Ferguson.
"You never see him ruffled and the way his team play is a testimony to the work he is doing.
"The most important thing for English football is how many English and young players are in his team. He understands the advantage of playing young people. They won't let you down and they have been fantastic."
United's own statement describes Pochettino as the club's new 'head coach' and reveals he will begin work on 18 November, with "Mauricio and his staff to be released after the conclusion of their responsibilities with the U.S. Men's National Team".
Pochettino's long-time assistants are Jesus Perez and Miguel D'Agostino, as well as goalkeeping coach Antonio Jimenez.

Fernandes rejects second offer of new United contract
Will Unwin
The Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes has rejected a second offer of a new contract at Old Trafford but the club are confident of getting the midfielder to commit his future to Old Trafford. The 32-year-old's current deal runs until the end of the season but United have the option to trigger a further year.
If United elect not to utilise that clause, Fernandes would be allowed to speak to clubs abroad in January. The Portuguese has previously had interest from Saudi Arabia and Galatasaray but chose to stay.
Fernandes won the PFA player of the year award last season after helping United to third in the league with a record-breaking 21 assists, but his form has dipped. He also took aim at Carrick's tactics after United's defeat by Como in the Champions League, in the weeks before the manager was sacked.
