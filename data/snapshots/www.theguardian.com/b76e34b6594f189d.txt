MEETMEBYTHESEA ENDS MCMANUS'S WAIT FOR PADDY POWER GOLD CUP WINNER
JP McManus, one of jump racing's leading owners for four decades, finally got his hands on the trophy for the Paddy Power Gold Cup on Saturday as Meetmebythesea, the 2-1 favourite, held the challenge of BluekingD'Oroux (100-30) by two-and-a-half lengths in the feature event of Cheltenham's November meeting.
The race was one of several on the card that suffered a series of non-runners due to drying ground, which was officially good, good-to-firm by the start of the first race, but Harry Cobden's mount delivered the result that many in the crowd of more than 32k racegoers wanted to see and gave the jockey his first big-race success since signing a retainer to ride as McManus's No.1.
"This is the race that really made me tick growing up," Ben Pauling, Meetmebytheseas's trainer, said, "and I've been coming to this meeting since I was 14. This has always been a big race for me and to have the favourite was a dream, so to win it is even better.
"I had so much faith in this horse and I talked him up plenty in the lead up. It's all well sitting on the fence, but I knew he was good, just not how good. You just have to believe he is going to keep on improving and hopefully that puts him bang there for some of the better races."
Meetmebythesea could now step up into Grade One company as soon as the King George VI Chase at Kempton on Boxing Day, for which he is quoted at around 12-1 by bookmakers.
Pauling won the race last year with The Jukebox Man and sees Meetmebythesea as having similar quality.
"I suppose we have to think about Kempton at Christmas and I think he's going to stay every yard of three miles," Pauling said. "To keep a horse like this improving is cool and we need these types of horses to keep us going the right way. It's not easy when you're in the top 10 and you need to find horses of this calibre. He looks to be one of them."
