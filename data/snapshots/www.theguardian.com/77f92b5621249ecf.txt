Arsenal head coach Renée Slegers tells Sky Sports: "Very good for us, of course. Liverpool away is a difficult game. It's an honour to play at Anfield, it's a historic ground. I'm just happy with the three points. We didn't get them last weekend at home and we really needed them.
"You could see the way the team came out in the first half, good with a lot of energy. Scored two really good goals. Liverpool are a good team, they spent a lot of time in possession so there was sometimes a lot more time than we usually spend out of possession and we had to defend well. They asked us questions towards the end of the game, there was so much fight, passion and pride for the club to defend our goal with everything we could. There were bodies on the line, I'm just really proud that we showed that character and did everything we could to get the win over the line."
