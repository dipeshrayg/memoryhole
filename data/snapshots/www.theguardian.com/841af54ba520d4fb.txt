The first song I fell in love with
My mum gave me an old Dansette record player and a pile of albums. I must have been eight or 10, with my younger sister Corinne, getting butterflies to These Boots Are Made for Walkin' by Nancy Sinatra. Years later, I read that she was told to sing it with a much more provocative attitude. You can hear the sass and the strength. Maybe that's where my whole "don't fuck with me" thing came from.
The song I do at karaoke
Give me any Marvin Gaye song, like You're All I Need to Get By, and I'm all over it, but I'd normally pull someone up to duet with me.
The song I inexplicably know every lyric to
Tired of Being Alone by Al Green is ingrained in my DNA. I know every lyric, every nuance. Years later, Texas did a cover of it, and we still occasionally play it live.
The best song to play at a party
I love everything Daft Punk do. Get Lucky is one of those songs everybody knows and it just makes everybody smile.
The song I can no longer listen to
My grandparents used to play Ave Maria when I was growing up. My mum absolutely loved it and had asked for it to be played at her funeral, just before lockdown. She also loved Queen. She used to say: "They're doctors of music." Last year I was awarded a doctorate of music from the University of Glasgow, so I phoned my sister and she just started laughing: "Oh my God. If Mum was here, she'd have a freakout. You're now a doctor of music - just like Queen." I'm just waiting for somebody to shout: "Is there a doctor in the house?" I can say: "Yes!" I can't save your life, but I can sing you a song.
The song I secretly like but tell everybody I hate
I don't have guilty pleasures, but I can rattle out a really good version of Highway to Hell by AC/DC, which normally throws people.
The first single I bought
Oliver's Army by Elvis Costello & the Attractions, from a little record shop inside an old cinema. We'd moved from Glasgow to Balloch, and used to go "down the Vale". There was an arcade with Centipede, Defender, Pac-Man and a pool table, then this record shop tucked away in the corner. I was intrigued because he was called Elvis. I thought: "Who is this fucker calling himself Elvis?"
The song that changed my life
I Don't Want a Lover by Texas. I went from being a hairdresser in Glasgow to this. It was the first song Johnny [McElhone] and I ever wrote, one week before my 18th birthday. He asked: "Do you write?" and I said: "Yeah," because I was a cheeky little bitch. I thought: "How hard can it be?"
The best song to have sex to
If you ever want to put anybody off a record, tell them which song you have sex to. I don't want to put people through that shit.
The song that makes me cry
Thin Line Between Love and Hate by the Persuaders. The whole idea of there being this thin line between loving someone and hating them is so true. If you truly love somebody, you're incredibly close to that love being turned if something goes wrong. The line about the sweetest woman in the world becoming the meanest - it's one of the greatest lines ever.
The song I want played at my funeral
Blackbird by the Beatles.
