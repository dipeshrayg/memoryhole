Burnham says using North Sea reserves can 'accelerate' move to a 'clean energy future'
Danny Kruger (Reform UK) asks Burnham if he confirms the budget numbers assume that the Rosebank oilfield will be allowed. (See 10.25am.)
Burnham says the Rosebank decision is a quasi-judicial decision that will be taken by the energy secretary.
But he says Kruger is right to say the use of the UK's North Sea reserves can "accelerate" the move to a clean energy future.
[Kruger is] right in pointing to the fact that use of our reserves can support and accelerate a clean energy future. And that very much was the logic behind the decision that the government announced on Jackdaw last week.

Burnham ducks invitation to condemn Steve Witkoff for calling Putin a humanitarian
Johanna Baxter (Lab) asks if Burnham agrees that Steve Witkoff, Donald Trump's envoy, was wrong to call President Putin a humanitarian.
She says:
The people of my constituency looked on in horror that our closest ally could use such words for a man wanted by the international criminal court for the systematic abduction of 20,651 Ukrainian children ...
So will the prime minister remind our American partners that appeasement, flattery and sanction dodging deals will only embolden Putin and risk further aggression to our Nato allies?
Burnham says Baxter is "absolutely right" in what she says about Putin. But he does not comment on Witkoff calling him a humanitarian.
