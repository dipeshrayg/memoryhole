Geoff Lemon was on hand as South Africa levelled the series against Australia with an emphatic victory in Gqeberha. Here is his report ...
It took time, it gently frayed nerves, but South Africa sealed the second Test in Gqeberha deep into the final day, clear by an innings and 31 runs, levelling the series with one to play. Although Australia made 295 and got within 28 overs of safety, they did not deserve to save this game, even if Scott Boland and Travis Head did.
Letting South Africa escape from 40 for four to pass 400 was the first offence, then came Australia's capitulation for 99 to be forced to follow on. The salvage operation was in waters too deep.
That's it from us today, what a brilliant Test match. Get those loins girded for a series deciding showdown in Cape Town next week. From all of us on the OBO - thanks for your company and correspondence. Goodbye!
Ithought there was some good fight today. Boland and Trav (Head) played their roles but it was a fair way to come back from there and it wasn't enough.
We were a long way away from our best. We didn't start off too strong. We just need more from all departments. You saw a blueprint today, how Scotty and Trav went about. They got stuck in. You need to weather the storm but when you do, there are some runs to have. After a loss there a few things to look at. The pitch was good and we needed to score a few runs. We will look at it. It's a reverse of last week, isn't it? They played well."
The heart rate's quite good! We were a little bit scrambling in the changing room and not too sure with the weather but we were very excited as soon as we got on the field. They made us work hard for those six wickets.
I think we saw throughout the days that batting was quite difficult, especially against the new ball. So I think to be able to perform like that, to be able to get the team in a very competitive position, I take a lot of pride in that.
I think our team, the way we go about it, we play good old Test cricket, you know, try and wear the opposition down and then take them down when the opportunity presents itself."
South Africa were 20 for 2 on day one then 97 for 5 and 110 for 6 on day two. Temba Bavuma's ensuing 179 off 334 balls against a gun bowling line up and with the series on the line will surely go down as one of the great modern innings for the Proteas, heck, potentially for anyone.
Handshakes all round and beaming smiles from the South African's who managed to get the final few wickets with the gloom circling, the clouds certainly added a further layer of anxiety to proceedings but Rabada and Jansen went bang bang after Wiaan Mulder managed to prise out Scott Boland and then nip out Pat Cummins with a couple of doozies. A great effort from the Proteas bowlers in the second innings with Anrich Nortje out of action through a groin injury.
