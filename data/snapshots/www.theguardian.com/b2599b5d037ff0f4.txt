In a word, yes. My theory from my experience working a lot in both countries, which others may dispute, is that broadly speaking the English are good losers and bad winners, while Australians are good winners and bad losers. Caveats! Generalisations! Do not deluge me with contrary case studies, I'm talking broad social averages here.
The theory being that because Australians expect to win, they move on from it quickly and don't wallow in smug gloating to the same extent, but when their teams lose, they are shocked to the core that such an outrage could happen, and can't cope with it.
All out for under a hundred is UNACCEPTABLE to an Australian mind, it is the province of weak teams from weak countries that do not have our indomitable pioneering spirit born of the hardscrabble clay, or something. There's a lot of hypernationalist myth mixed in there, and a lack of ability to be philosophical about failure.
