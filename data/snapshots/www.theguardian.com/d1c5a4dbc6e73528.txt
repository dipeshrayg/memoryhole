The losing captain Angela Stanford, bitterly disappointed but still wearing a sporting smile, speaks to Sky Sports. "You hear people say how difficult it is ... and it really is ... we fought hard ... the Europeans played great today and that's how it goes ... good for them ... I try not to have regrets ... I believe in all 12 ... there are a lot of captains in line ... I think we have a lot lined up ready to go."
That was yet another magnificent Solheim Cup. Nip and tuck all the way to the end. Carlota Ciganda's channelling the walkabout spirit of her compatriot Severiano Ballesteros on Friday evening; Jennifer Kupcho making those putts heard around the world in the gloaming on Saturday; Charley Hull and Lottie Woad finally sprinkling their stardust on Sunday, before Ciganda saw off a Kupcho comeback to seal the deal. Marvellous entertainment. Europe the thoroughly deserved winners, but Team USA will be pleased with their performances too, once the pain of defeat eases off a bit. Every last player a hero.
