Bryson DeChambeau will play on the DP World Tour for the 2027 season following the highly publicised demise of LIV Golf.
DeChambeau is ineligible to join the PGA Tour, where he has not played for more than four years, due to a blanket ban on LIV players that runs for 12 months from the date of their last tournament appearance on the Saudi Arabia-funded circuit.
The Tour stated on Tuesday that DeChambeau is "joining as a member for the first time in the category one" status. He had the option to join because he won a major - the 2024 US Open - in the last five years.
The American player was the most prominent face of LIV, which announced two weeks ago in bankruptcy court it plans to wind down operations instead of launching a new version after losing the financial support of Saudi Arabia.
On Monday, the PGA Tour CEO Brian Rolapp said his board had "thoughtful discussions" about the future of players at LIV Golf, adding: "I hope to have more to say in the beginning of the new year."
The addition of DeChambeau, who has his own YouTube channel with more than 3m subscribers, is a huge boon to the DP World Tour. It comes after Jon Rahm and Tyrrell Hatton - the winners of the last two events of the 2026 season - announced they will be returning to the tour after their departure from LIV, along with Sergio García.
In a statement announcing its membership categories for 2027, the DP World Tour said nine players coming off LIV - including Dustin Johnson and Cameron Smith - had signed agreements to receive invites to a specific number of tournaments next season.
The other players were Abraham Ancer, Lucas Herbert, Michael La Sasso, Joaquin Niemann, Carlos Ortiz and Thomas Pieters.
