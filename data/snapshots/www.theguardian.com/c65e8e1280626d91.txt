Shadab Khan smashed a century to help Pakistan book their place in the final after England somehow lost the one-day international from an unbeatable position in Lahore
It's done! Haris Rauf pings Henry Crocombe down the ground for four and Pakistan complete the most incredible of comebacks!
The Gadaffi Stadium is absolute bedlam and has been for the last hour as Shadab Khan and Haris Rauf pull off an astonishing victory. Pakistan were 154 for 9 and needed 88 more.
It looked like it was all over, England certainly thought so. Shadab chipped away, getting a boundary off nearly every over and farming the strike with aplomb. Haris Rauf played his part too, blocking out when he needed to, ducking and weaving as England threw everything at him. He hit the winning runs and hared off with his arms raised.
I think I can't say anything! Shadab and Haris, the way they finished the game... I'm really happy as a captain as well, starting with the bowling, the team effort was spot on. We thought we had a lot of variation in the team so that's why we brought Shadab in. We need top-order runs, we're struggling. I hope the next few games we'll bring that energy. I think tonight we'll be celebrating, tomorrow back to normal."
Here's Harry Brook at the post match presentation:
I don't think it went wrong with the ball to be honest. I thought we did an exceptional job to restrict them to us nearly winning the game. We probably didn't quite get enough runs on the board but the effort the boys put in with the ball and in the field was phenomenal. I couldn't ask for any more.
Ben Duckett played an exceptional innings - his fifth ODI hundred which came at a good time. He played really well and that partnership that he and Sam Curran had put us in a decent position to kick on. Unfortunately we lost a few wickets in quick succession but the way that Gus Atkinson finished the innings off was awesome.
We have to hope Pakistan beat Sri Lanka but we have got to just focus on what we can do against Sri Lanka really and try to beat them by a big margin."
A tough loss for England. Two hard to stomach defeats in a row in fact. The bowlers and Brook will get plenty of heat (is this The Traitors?) for the way the game went in the final hour but England's batters left too many out there today too.
"You can't beat a Tri team tournament" laughs Nasser Hussain on the tv coverage. I'm inclined to agree after witnessing that. There's steam coming out of my ears and smoke out of my laptop keyboard.
England aren't actually done for, they need Pakistan to beat Sri Lanka on Tuesday and then have to beat Sri Lanka (heavily) themselves on Thursday to have a chance of squeaking into Saturday's final.
Shadab Khan and Haris Rauf's partnership of 89 is the highest for the tenth wicket in a successful chase in ODIs. The previous record of 64 was held by Deryck Murray and Andy Roberts against Pakistan in the 1975 World Cup.
England went to pieces under pressure after bowling brilliantly until Shadab arrived in the middle but you have to give all the credit to Shadab and Haris Rauf. The shot and decision making under pressure was remarkable.
