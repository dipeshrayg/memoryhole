Yorkshire sign up with Urban Chanin renewable energy company
While there is still life in this match, just wanted to congratulate Yorkshire who have signed up with renewable energy company Urban Chain to provide their power from the new year. Urban Chain connects renewable energy generators to buyers, so Headingley's power will come from a range of renewable sources, with the hope that, in time, more and more of them will come from within Yorkshire's boundaries. It is part of a wide strategy to improve sustainability, which the club kicked off by doing a transport survey with members to work out how people travel to the ground.

Roundup: Bess frustrates old teammates to jolt Somerset's title ambitions
It was a tense day at Headingley, cold enough in the shade for the stewards to wear gloves, but hot on the pitch where Somerset wobbled but just about held on to their Championship dream. It took more than a session to break Yorkshire's overnight partnership of George Hill and Dom Bess, who played with swaggering patience in a seventh-wicket partnership of 166.
Hill finally fell to a top-edged pull for a season's best 89 to Craig Overton, who chased him to the rope to say well done. The former Somerset player Bess followed for 77, all material for Tom Abell's best man's speech at Bess's wedding next month. Set 287, Somerset had a catastrophic start when Josh Thomas was caught behind in the first over, and a couple more wickets followed, the fielders providing regular reminders that the title has never gone to Taunton, before Abell and Tom Lammonby saw things through to the close.
