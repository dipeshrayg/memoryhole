If Kiwi fans thought last week's loss to Wales was painful this was arguably as bad in terms of the reputational damage. The home side were 19-0 up after just 36 minutes and had the game all but wrapped up within a couple of minutes of the restart when Ollie Chessum smashed his way over to claim his side's fourth try. From a margin of victory perspective this was England's biggest victory over New Zealand in the 121-year-old history of the fixture.
There were even occasions when the fabled silver fern was in some danger of being totally trashed, with almost three-quarters of the game taking place in New Zealand half. If anything it was England who more closely resembled the slick All Black sides of yesteryear, particularly in the first-half when tries from Henry Slade, Jack van Poortvliet and Ben Earl rewarded the hosts' purposeful approach play and seemingly greater desire.

After all the flak Steve Borthwick took pre-tournament with suggestions he had lost the dressing room, what a November. The players rejected those claims verbally and have now demonstrated it in fine style.
79 mins. The ball is soon back in England hands, with Fisilau prominent in the carries moving them up to the 5 metre line. On an advantage Smith chips it in-goal but an All Black hand grounds it. However, we come back for the penalty.
78 mins. The scrum on halfway from the Barrett knock-on takes a few moments to complete, and when it does Marcus Smith boots the ball aimlessly back to NZ.
76 mins. England's forwards drive into the line with a few carries, but Scott Barrett gets amongst the breakdown to win a holding on penalty. The ball is cleared upfield and on the next possession Chessum rattles Barrett's torso to send the ball flying from his hands.
68 mins. As you can imagine the All Blacks are forcing it now, with the latest possession being shovelled to the right and into touch in the face of a white wall that remains unhurried and unmoved.
The most worrying thing if you are a New Zealander is how utterly weak they have looked. The energy is low which is likely a confidence thing, but their weak physicality is a real concern.
63 mins. Ellis Genge is off the bench and having a wonderful time running with the ball. His latest punches a hole into the 22 and a calm reset sends the ball to Atkinson to dive over in the right 13 channel.
60 mins. Aumua is on at hooker but the outcome remains the ball being chucked to an England player in the linout. The All Blacks have now lost 40% of their lineouts in this game; there really are no words.
58 mins. NZ are working phases near halfway where their initial pass is always 10 metres from the ruck to first receiver, and the England defenders are absolutely gobbling this maladroit nonsense up. In the end McKenzie has had enough and chips it forward for Barrett to chase, but the ball is gathered by England and that's that.
