Trump threatens to bomb Oman if it 'gets in the way' of Hormuz shipping deal
US President Donald Trump on Monday threatened to bomb Oman if it "gets in the way" of a deal over shipping in the Strait of Hormuz. His threat came after the prime minister's office in Iraqi Kurdistan was targeted by two Iranian drones overnight, authorities said Monday. Follow our liveblog to see how the day's events unfolded.
