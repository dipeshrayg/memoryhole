'We're kind of at a stalemate': French government and unions 'dancing around each other'
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Amid continuing protests in France and tense negotiations between the government and trade unions, Delano D'Souza is pleased to welcome Paul Smith, Associate Professor in French and Francophone Studies at the University of Nottingham. "What's happening is that there are two parties kind of dancing around each other." Smith explains that the government wants to delay negotiations until after trade union elections on 10 December, while the unions want an agreement as quickly as possible. With talks "more or less broken down" over a two-point difference in public sector pay proposals, he describes the situation as "kind of at a stalemate."
