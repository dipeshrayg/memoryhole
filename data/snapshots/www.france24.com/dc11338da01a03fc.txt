Search continues after deadly mine collapse on Cameroon-Central African Republic border

In tonight's edition: A deadly landslide at a gold mine in the Central African Republic leaves dozens dead, with victims from Cameroon and Chad. Meanwhile, Ethiopia is turning to Bordeaux expertise and grapevines in a bid to become East Africa's next wine destination. Finally, Belgium continues to confront its colonial past, more than a century after Congolese people were put on display at the 1894 Antwerp World's Fair.
