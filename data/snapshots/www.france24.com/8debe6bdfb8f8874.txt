Russell Brand sentenced to 10 years, 6 months for rape and sexual assault
Comedian Russell Brand, once one of Britain's most high-profile broadcasters, was sentenced to 10-and-a-half years in prison on Monday after he was convicted of raping one woman and sexually assaulting three more around two decades ago.
British comedian and actor Russell Brand was sentenced Monday to 10 years and six months in prison for raping one woman and sexually assaulting three others.
Judge Joel Bennathan told Brand that he showed a "casual and arrogant disregard" for any woman to say no to him during sentencing remarks that put the crimes in the context of his rise to stardom.

"About 20 years ago, Russell Brand, you were becoming famous," Bennathan said. "You were clever - and in many people's eyes, funny and charismatic. You had also become a sexual predator."
The 51-year-old actor-comedian - known for outrageous stand-up routines and behaviour on television and radio broadcasts, along with drug and alcohol battles - had asserted he was innocent and all his encounters were consensual.
But a jury of eight men and four women found him guilty of attacking four of six accusers on allegations dating back to 2004. He was cleared of two counts of rape and one count of attempted indecent assault.
