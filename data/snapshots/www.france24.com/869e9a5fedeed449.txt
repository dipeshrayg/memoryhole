US Supreme court rejects Trump request to set aside 20,000 California ballots
Issued on: Modified:
Share
The US Supreme Court rejected the Trump administration's push to force California to set aside 20,000 ballots from people the federal government claims are noncitizens, a decision that came hours before the final day of voting in the pivotal midterm elections. FRANCE 24's correspondent Wassim Cornet reports.
