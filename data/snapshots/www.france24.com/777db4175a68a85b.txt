Trump heads to Nebraska as Republicans fear strongholds could flip
Issued on:
Share
US President Donald Trump will visit Nebraska on Monday to rally Republicans and promote his economic record, taking his message of new investment and manufacturing to a state where farmers and truckers face sharply higher diesel prices and voters worry about inflation. His stop in the city of Grand Island is part of Trump's 32-day sprint to the November 3 midterm elections, a campaign in which he is spending considerable time in Republican strongholds. Trump has urged Republicans to "pretend" he is on the ballot, arguing that voters should view the midterms as a referendum on his administration and economic record. France 24's Fraser Jackson has more.
