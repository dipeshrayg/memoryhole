Across Africa: The Scars of South Africa's Soweto Uprising
Issued on:
50 years ago, thousands of young South Africans from Soweto protested against apartheid's stranglehold on their education and the imposition of Afrikaans as a language of instruction. What began as a peaceful protest ended in bloodshed. And men in Northern Benin confront uncomfortable realities about the lives of the women who live and love them. [Archive edition, June 2026]
