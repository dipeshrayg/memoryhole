Champions League: Everything still to play for between Lyon and Fenerbahce
Issued on:
Olympique Lyonnais and Fenerbahce drew 1-1 in the first leg of their Champions League play-off. The second leg will take place on Wednesday 26 August in Lyon.
Patrick Vieira is Senegal's new manager. Rodri is officially a Barcelona player. In tennis, Aryna Sabalenka and Mirra Andreeva have qualified for the last 16 in Cincinnati, as has Brandon Nakashima. At the World Badminton Championships, Christo Popov has already been knocked out.
