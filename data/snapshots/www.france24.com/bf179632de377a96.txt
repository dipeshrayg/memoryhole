Champions League: Everything still to play for between Lyon and Fenerbahçe
Issued on:
Olympique Lyonnais and Fenerbahçe drew 1-1 in the first leg of their Champions League play-off. The second leg will take place on Wednesday August 26 in Lyon.
Also in this edition:
Patrick Vieira is Senegal's new manager. Rodri is officially a Barcelona player. In tennis, Aryna Sabalenka and Mirra Andreeva have qualified for the last 16 in Cincinnati, as has Brandon Nakashima. Finally, at the World Badminton Championships, France's Christo Popov has already been knocked out.
