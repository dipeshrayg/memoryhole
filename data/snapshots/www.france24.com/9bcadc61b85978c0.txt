The twice-yearly ASEAN summit of Southeast Asian nations is underway, with major global powers also descending upon the Philippines. It is a crucial moment for the region, as ASEAN leaders look to strengthen their influence and collaboration in an increasingly divided global landscape. The arrival of US President Donald Trump on Tuesday is also being welcomed as well as protested, as Charli James reports.
