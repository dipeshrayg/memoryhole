'We are ready to facilitate removal of Netanyahu': Head of Israel's Arab-majority Joint List
Issued on:
In an interview with FRANCE 24, the head of the Arab-majority Joint List said his political alliance is prepared to play a decisive role in bringing down Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's government following the recent election. "We are ready to facilitate the removal of Netanyahu" from power, Yousef Jabareen said, signalling a willingness to explore political alliances beyond traditional divisions in Israeli politics.
Jabareen described the current political climate as an emergency for Israel's Arab minority, pointing to years of growing political tensions and marginalisation under Netanyahu's leadership and the influence of far-right ministers Itamar Ben-Gvir and Bezalel Smotrich.
"I feel that we are in an emergency," Jabareen said. "We, as the Arab minority in Israel, have witnessed very awful and tough years under Netanyahu, Ben-Gvir and Smotrich."
