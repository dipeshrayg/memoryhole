Airports, public transport shut down amid national strike in Belgium
Issued on:
Share
Belgium's prime minister on Monday set a new year-end deadline for his coalition government to agree a cost-cutting budget or face collapse. The announcement came as a national strike against the planned cuts grounded all flights in and out of Belgium. Public transport, schools and other public services also faced disruption. France 24's Dave Keating has the latest from Brussels.
