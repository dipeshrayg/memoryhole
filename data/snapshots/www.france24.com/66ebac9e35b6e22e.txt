Boston Ballet returns to Paris and builds lasting legacy
Issued on:
In this edition of Entre Nous we speak to Mikko Nissinen, the artistic director of the Boston Ballet, which is performing this week at the historic Théâtre des Champs-Elysées in Paris. He tells us what it means to come back to Paris and what the French audience can expect from works by Alexander Ekman and Akram Khan.
A programme produced by Amanda Alexander, Marina Pajovic and Georgina Robertson
