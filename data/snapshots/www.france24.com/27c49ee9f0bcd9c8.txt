The chocolate countdown: The art of the advent calendar
Issued on:
The holiday season is almost upon us and with it come advent calendars. The annual physical countdown originated in 19th-century Germany as a Christian tradition to count down the days until Christmas. Eugénie Pont from the magazine Fou de Pâtisserie tells us more about what we can expect from the biggest chocolate makers in France.
A programme produced by Amanda Alexander, Georgina Robertson and Marina Pajovic.
