'Free' land and thriving businesses: French village defies rural decline
Issued on: Modified:
Share
As France's mayors gather in Paris for their annual convention, the decline in living standards and public services in both urban and rural areas is high on the agenda. Rural mayors, in particular, are concerned about the shrinking populations of their communities. But one small French town bucking the trend is Parlan, in the Cantal region, where the population has doubled and local services have flourished thanks to an offer of virtually free plots of land.
