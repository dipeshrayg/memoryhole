COP31 wrapping up in Turkey: Climate deal elusive as fossil fuels go unmentioned
Issued on: Modified:
Share
The UN's COP31 climate talks were set to spill into overtime Friday as countries remained deadlocked over a draft deal that makes no mention of fossil fuels, with a host of other disputes also snarling negotiations. The talks come with a series of stark reminders of the urgency of climate action, from heatwaves in Europe and devastating floods in Nepal, while a record-breaking El Nino supercharges temperatures. FRANCE 24's Environment Editor Valerie Dekimpe tells us more.
