Lens defeat Paris Saint-Germain to claim second title this year
Issued on:
RC Lens won the Champions' Trophy (French Super Cup) against Paris Saint-Germain (1-0). With Zinédine Zidane watching on, the Lens side held out for an hour despite being a man down.
Also in this edition:
Arsenal comfortably won the Community Shield against Manchester City (3-0). Cameroon won the Women's Africa Cup of Nations. Léon Marchand once again impressed at the European Swimming Championships. It was a successful final day for French at the European Athletics Championships. Finally, France's Félix Lebrun made table tennis history.
