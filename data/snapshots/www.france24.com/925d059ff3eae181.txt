Ligue 1: Paris Saint-Germain aim to build on recent progress at Strasbourg
Issued on:
Fresh from their impressive 4-2 Champions League win on Manchester City's home turf on Wednesday, PSG travel to Strasbourg on Saturday in Ligue 1. They aim to secure a fourth consecutive league win and maintain their growing momentum ahead of Tuesday's Champions League showdown with Barcelona.
Elsewhere in Ligue 1, Toulouse impressed with a convincing win at Le Mans (3-0).
In England, Manchester City aim to bounce back against Ipswich, amid off-field controversy, while Chelsea must cope without injured captain Reece James, who faces a lengthy spell on the sidelines.
In Spain, Barcelona are chasing a ninth straight La Liga win, ahead of a huge week featuring clashes with PSG and Real Madrid.
Rugby is in mourning following the sudden death of South African second-row Hyron Andrews, found dead on the Île de Ré at the age of 31.
And finally, at the Shanghai Masters, Spanish rising star Rafael Jodar has caused a major upset by reaching the semi-finals, where he will face Daniil Medvedev.
