Is Israel opposition figure Gadi Eisenkot going to replace Benjamin Netanyahu?
Issued on:
Share
In Israel, Gadi Eisenkot emerged on Thursday, November 5, as the frontrunner to be tasked with forming a government. This comes after one of the two main parties representing ultra-Orthodox Jewish voters - a longtime ally of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's coalition - declined to recommend any candidate for the task. FRANCE 24's correspondent in Jerusalem Noga Tarnopolsky reports.
