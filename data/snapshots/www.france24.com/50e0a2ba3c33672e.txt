Moldova launches national preparedness campaign after drone incursions
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Moldova is urging its citizens to prepare for emergencies as the war in neighbouring Ukraine brings new risks across its border. The "Moldova Ready" campaign, launched in Chisinau, includes a display of the remains of a drone carrying 52kg of explosives after it crashed in the country last month. FRANCE 24's Daniel Whittington reports.
