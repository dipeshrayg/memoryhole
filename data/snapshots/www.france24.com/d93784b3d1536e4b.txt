US unveils proposed tariffs on major partners, including EU and China
Issued on:
Share
The United States on Wednesday unveiled proposed tariffs on 14 economies including the European Union and China, after a months-long probe into alleged excess production - the latest move by US President Donald Trump to remake Washington's trade relationships. The new tariffs will need to undergo a period of public comment before taking effect. The new levies target some of the United States' largest trading partners. FRANCE 24's International Affairs Editor Kethevane Gorjestani tells us more.
