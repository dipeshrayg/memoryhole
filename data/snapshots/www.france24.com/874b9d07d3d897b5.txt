Brazil's countdown to election day: Crime and security high on voters' agenda
Issued on:
Share
Security is a central issue in Brazil's presidential campaign, in a country where gangs and militias have tightened their grip in recent years. Lula is accused of losing ground amid rising insecurity and violence - an issue seized on by Flavio Bolsonaro and the far right. Rio de Janeiro is one of the country's most violent cities and a showcase for public security policy. How are Rio residents experiencing this insecurity, and will it influence their vote? FRANCE 24's Fanny Lothaire and Jan Onoszko report.
