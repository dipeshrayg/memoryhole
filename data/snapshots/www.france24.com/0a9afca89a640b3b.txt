Live: France will ban 'illegitimate' protests in coming days, says Macron
French President Emmanuel Macron said on Thursday that "illegitimate" protests would be banned in the coming days, while those from organised groups such as unions would be offered alternative paths for the marches to protect businesses. The government has said it will put French soldiers in the street after rioting and looting broke out on the fringes of mass peaceful demonstrations over the weekend. Follow our liveblog for the latest updates.
- France is bracing for yet another weekend of protests by high school students and the "Yellow Vest" movement.
- Paris police have banned a student protest scheduled for Thursday, citing the organiser's "inability to prevent violence and vandalism", a police source told AFP.
- French Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu will on Thursday meet with the mayors of some of the cities that were worst hit by the riots over the weekend, including Paris, Marseille and Lyon.
