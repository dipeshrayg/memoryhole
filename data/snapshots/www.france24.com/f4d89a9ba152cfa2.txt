Top Spanish court closes case against Catalan separatist leader
Issued on:
Share
Spain's Supreme Court has applied a controversial amnesty law to former Catalan separatist leader Carles Puigdemont, closing criminal proceedings against him over his role in the region's failed 2017 independence bid. The ruling, which comes ahead of a snap general election on Sunday, marks the final step in the implementation of the amnesty law, passed by parliament in 2024.
