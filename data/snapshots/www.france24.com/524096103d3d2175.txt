Fires in Europe live: France gives green light for 84,000 evacuees to return home
French authorities on Thursday gave the go-ahead for 84,000 evacuees forced out by a massive wildfire in the country's southwest to return to their homes. More than 220,000 people were forced to flee their homes or holiday accommodation since the blaze broke out. Follow our liveblog for all the latest updates.
