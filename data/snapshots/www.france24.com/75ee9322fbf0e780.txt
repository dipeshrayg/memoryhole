France's far right gathers as Le Pen prepares her presidential campaign

Meanwhile, France's far-right National Rally is holding the second day of its party congress in Orléans, south of Paris. Marine Le Pen is expected to officially launch her presidential campaign in a speech later this afternoon, while Jordan Bardella is being re-elected as party leader. On Saturday, a large counter-demonstration against the far right was held in the city. For more on what's happening there, let's cross to our reporter, Antonia Kerrigan.
