Egypt mulls joining Saudi Arabia in war against Houthis
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Egypt may be about to join the war against Houthis. Saudi Arabia has come under sustained attack from the Iran-backed group in recent weeks after a collapse of a truce in Yemen in September. Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman visited Egypt mid-September to request potential military support from the country - a demand put before parliament by the Egyptian president on Saturday. Story by Camille Corcoran.
