The Israeli government believes an incident on a flight from the United Arab Emirates to Israel, which diverted to Saudi Arabia after transmitting emergency signals on Wednesday, may have been an attempted "terror attack" by one of the pilots. France 24's Shirli Sitbon takes a look at what we know about the incident so far, with many of the details still emerging.
