Xi embraces Trump in China: 'Diplomacy playing out in real time'
Issued on:
Share
President Xi Jinping personally greeted US counterpart Donald Trump on the tarmac at a Chinese airport on Wednesday, with the two leaders briefly hugging ahead of their highly anticipated meeting with Russia's Vladimir Putin. Reporting from Shenzhen, France 24's Jan Camenzind Broomby says the warm greeting was a "big sign of respect" from Xi but also a calculated component of diplomatic efforts.
