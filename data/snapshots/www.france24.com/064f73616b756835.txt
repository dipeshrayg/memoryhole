Inside Ethiopia's war: Rare access to a hospital treating victims of renewed fighting
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Ethiopia's renewed conflict is taking an increasing toll on civilians, with humanitarian organisations reporting a rise in casualties. In the Amhara region, FRANCE 24's correspondent Tom Canetti was granted rare access to a civilian hospital where surgeons are struggling to treat the influx of wounded patients. But for many caught in the fighting, reaching medical care is a challenge in itself. Warning: The report contains images of surgery that may be confronting for some viewers.
