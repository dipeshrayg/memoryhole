'Clashing narratives': How are young Americans today taught about 9/11?
Issued on:

An estimated 100 million Americans, almost a third of the population, were either not yet born or were too young to remember the September 11 attacks. Now, as the US marks the 25th anniversary of 9/11, how are young people in America being taught about what happened that day and its consequences? France 24's Douglas Herbert takes a look.
