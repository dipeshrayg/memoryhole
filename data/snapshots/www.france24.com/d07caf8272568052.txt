AI leaders warn UN Security Council of risk to 'humanity as a whole' without regulation
The current moment in artificial intelligence development "calls for extreme care", OpenAI CEO Sam Altman told an emergency session of the UN Security Council on Wednesday following weeks of global headlines about the risk of uncontrollable AI. Read our live blog to see how the day's events unfolded.
- UN Secretary-General Antonio Guterres warned that humans risked increasingly handing decision-making power over to machines as artificial intelligence advances at break-neck speed.
- US President Donald Trump threatened to "annihilate" Iran if Tehran and Washington don't reach a peace deal, before claiming a deal would be struck following the US midterm elections.
- France's President Emmanuel Macron condemned the ongoing humanitarian crisis in the devastated Gaza Strip as "a spectacle that shames us all".
- US special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met with Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly.
