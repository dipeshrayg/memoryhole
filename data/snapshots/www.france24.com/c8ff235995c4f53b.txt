Finland summons Russian ambassador after suspected airspace violation
Finland on Monday said it had summoned Russia's ambassador over a suspected violation of Finnish airspace by a Russian helicopter and after a Ukrainian drone strike on a Russian cargo ship located in the Gulf of Finland in recent days. Helsinki also condemned Moscow's escalating strikes on civilian targets in Ukraine.
Finland said Monday it had summoned the Russian ambassador, days after a Russian helicopter was suspected of violating Finnish airspace, and condemned Moscow's strikes on civilian targets in Ukraine.
"We strongly condemn Russia's illegal war of aggression and its escalating strikes against civilian targets in Ukraine. We stand in solidarity with Denmark, Poland and our other European partners," the foreign ministry said on X.
It added that Russia's "dangerous and reckless actions in Europe" were "unacceptable".
The move comes after a Russian cargo ship, identified as the Sparta II, was hit by a Ukrainian drone attack in international waters in the Gulf of Finland on Saturday, causing a fire and forcing the evacuation of the crew, one of whom suffered minor wounds.

Just hours after the Sparta II was hit, a Russian helicopter was suspected of violating the Nordic country's airspace.
Finnish authorities, with help from Estonian and Russian rescue vessels, put out the fire as the vessel had drifted into Finnish waters and was anchored about 90 kilometres east of the capital Helsinki.
It was towed from Finnish waters to Russia on Sunday evening.
"Ukraine has the right to defend itself against Russia's illegal aggression," the foreign ministry added in its statement Monday.
