Middle East live: Riyadh's financial district targeted following deadly Saudi strikes in Yemen
Riyadh's Aramco Tower was damaged by falling debris on Sunday after the Saudi-led coalition fighting Yemen's Houthi rebels intercepted two ballistic missiles headed for the capital's financial district. The attack comes a day after Saudi air strikes killed at least 33 people near Sanaa. Follow our liveblog for the latest developments from the Middle East.
Yesterday's key developments:
- Yemen's health ministry said at least 33 people were killed in Saudi air strikes near the capital Sanaa.
- Israel's President Isaac Herzog chose former military chief Gadi Eisenkot to try to form a government after parliamentary elections left neither Eisenkot nor Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu with a clear path to building a majority in the Knesset.
