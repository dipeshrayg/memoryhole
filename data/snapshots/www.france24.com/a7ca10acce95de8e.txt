Nepal-Tibet floods live: Death toll passes 580, nearly 2,500 people missing
Rescuers in Tibet reached the main area wiped out by a devastating mudslide this week, Chinese state media reported, as the search for missing people battled treacherous conditions. The devastating flooding in Nepal and Tibet has claimed more than 580 lives and left some 2,500 missing. Follow our liveblog for the latest updates.
• The search for 1,300 people continued after landslides caused by a glacial collapse unleashed a torrent of mud and water across Nepal and China's autonomous region of Tibet.
• Nepal and China both warned of fresh risks of flooding in the Himalayas as two lakes that have developed on either side of their border threaten to burst.
• Devastating floods on the Nepal-Tibet border have killed at least 359 people, Nepali police said in an update.
(FRANCE 24 with Reuters, AP and AFP)
