Behind the UK's use of AI in asylum applications
Issued on:
Share
The UK is increasingly turning to artificial intelligence to manage its immigration system, from analysing migrants' phones to processing asylum applications. But what happens when AI gets involved in deciding how an asylum seeker's story is understood? Stella Elgersma, journalist at InfoMigrants, explains how the UK Home Office is testing AI tools to process asylum applications, including a system that generates summaries of asylum interviews. With errors, limited human oversight and concerns over transparency, could AI put asylum seekers' lives at risk?
