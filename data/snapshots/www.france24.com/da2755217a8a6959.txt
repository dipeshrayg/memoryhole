Nepal: Rescue efforts continue in the aftermath of a flash flooding disaster
Issued on: Modified:
Two weeks after the devastating flash floods, the number of dead and missing continues to rise. At Kathmandu's public hospital, as well as outside the military base in Bidur, hundreds of photographs of missing people have been put up. Hundreds of families come forward, hoping for news - good or bad - of one or more loved ones. A race against time is underway: helping survivors, finding those still alive and identifying the victims. Rescue teams have been carrying out repeated helicopter flights, as air access is now the only way to reach remote areas cut off from the rest of the world. Civil society has also mobilised, particularly Nepal's Generation Z. From Kathmandu to Betrawati, FRANCE 24's reporters Alban Alvarez and Fantine Dantzer accompanied a humanitarian convoy along one of the country's most treacherous roads.
