Paris: Cézanne takes centre stage at Paris' Grand Palais
Issued on:
Share
The Grand Palais in Paris is showcasing more than 170 works, including 69 by Paul Cézanne and around 100 by 78 other artists who were influenced by his work. The exhibition, titled Cézanne et Nous - Cézanne and Us - runs until January 17, with artists such as Pablo Picasso and Auguste Renoir among those inspired by Cézanne. Morgan Ayre reports.
