Live: Massive wildfire in southwest France 'remains under control'
A massive wildfire that has burned some 42,000 hectares in southwest France over the past week "remains under control" after a second quiet night, regional authorities said Wednesday. But the regional prefecture warned that hot and dry weather conditions remained "unfavourable". Follow our liveblog for all the latest updates.
- Some 2,750 firefighters battled the massive wildfire in France's southwest Gironde region.
- French authorities ordered the evacuation of 4,000 people from tourist sites on the Atlantic coast.
- An 'orange' heatwave alert was issued for 16 departments.
(FRANCE 24 with AFP, AP and Reuters)
