Russian strikes kill three near Kyiv, Zelensky makes landmark visit to Serbia
Issued on:

Russian missile strikes killed three people, including a child, in the Kyiv region on Saturday, said local authorities. The attack came hours after Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky arrived in Belgrade on a landmark visit to Serbia, a country with close ties to Moscow. FRANCE 24's Emmanuelle Chaze reports from Kharkiv.
