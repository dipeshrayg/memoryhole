Tech giants lose $1 trillion in one day after Democrats' victory in midterms
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Vast sums have been wiped off the stock value of major tech firms in the US, Europe and Asia. Investors appear worried about the possibility of an AI slowdown and tougher regulation after Democrats won the midterms in the US. Some of the Democratic candidates had expressed their opposition to the data centres that are needed to power AI technology.
