Israel calls Erdogan an 'anti-Semitic dictator' after Turkey issues Netanyahu arrest warrant
Turkey issued an international arrest warrant for Israel's Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu as part of an investigation into Israel's interception of an aid flotilla for Gaza and its detention of hundreds of activists on board, Justice Minister Akin Gurlek announced Friday. Netanyahu's office in turn said Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdogan was an "anti-Semitic dictator". Read our liveblog to see how the day's events unfolded.
