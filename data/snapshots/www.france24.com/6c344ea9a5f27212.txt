Trump, Xi, Putin summit: What was it all about?
Issued on:
Share
Donald Trump has left China after a historic first meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin on the sidelines of the APEC summit. Ukraine was discussed without Kyiv or European leaders in the room, which raised eyebrows, though Trump defended dialogue with rivals and Putin said the talks created conditions for future agreements. Xi warned major powers against conflict, and topics included reducing nuclear risks, reforming the UN and increasing energy flows.
