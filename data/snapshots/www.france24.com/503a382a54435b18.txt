Two trapped workers rescued from mud-clogged tunnel 9 days after Nepal floods
Issued on:
Share
Nine days after catastrophic floods hit Nepal, hope emerges from the dark as two workers have been rescued from a collapsed tunnel. They are receiving treatment as emergency crews race to find more survivors. The floods killed more than 1,200 people and left over 5,000 missing. An estimated 115 people are still believed trapped in the tunnel.
