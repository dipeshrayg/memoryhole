Speech at Association of Colleges conference
In her speech, the Education Secretary calls on colleges to work with government to get today's 16-18s not currently in education back in and re-engaged.
Good morning, everyone.
Thank you, Sameena, for that lovey warm welcome. And thanks for having me here today.

maybe a few more...

Almost one million young people not in employment, education or training.

I will take a whole child response...
a whole childhood response...

and leaves no one behind.
Excellence and inclusion side by side.
