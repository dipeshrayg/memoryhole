Charity regulator appoints Interim Managers to CG Community Council
The Charity Commission, the regulator of charities in England and Wales, has appointed Interim Managers to CG Community Council (registered charity number 502955).
The charity was placed under statutory inquiry in April 2025 to investigate concerns about its governance and financial management.
Charles Turner and Emma Mifsud of Opus Business Advisory Group LLP have been appointed as Interim Managers of the charity to the exclusion of the charity's trustee. The Interim Managers' tasks include:
Assuming responsibility for the day-to-day management and administration of the charity, including ensuring any rents due to the charity are collected and banked appropriately.
Disposing of the charity's properties in a way they consider is in the best interests of the charity and its beneficiaries.
Assessing the charity's financial viability.
The Interim Managers were appointed in July 2026. The Commission's inquiry into the charity remains ongoing.

Notes to editors:
The Charity Commission is the independent, non-ministerial government department that registers and regulates charities in England and Wales. Its ambition is to be an expert regulator that is fair, balanced, and independent so that charity can thrive. This ambition will help to create and sustain an environment where charities further build public trust and ultimately fulfil their essential role in enhancing lives and strengthening society.
On 27 July 2026, the inquiry exercised the Commission's power to appoint Interim Managers by Order under section 76(3)(g) of the Charities Act 2011 to the exclusion of the charity's trustee.
