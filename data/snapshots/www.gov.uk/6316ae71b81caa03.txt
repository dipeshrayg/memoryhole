To mark International Day of the Girl on 11 October, the British and Danish Embassies in Tunisia are inviting girls to enter the Ambassador for a Day competition.
Ambassador for a Day is a competition run by the British Embassy and the Danish Embassy in Tunisia.
The competition gives winners the opportunity to spend one day shadowing the British or Danish Ambassador and learn more about diplomacy, leadership and public service.
It aims to encourage girls to learn about diplomacy and share their views on issues that affect their communities and the wider world.
How to enter
To enter, submit a video or short essay in English answering one of the following questions:
How can we use technology and AI to support the rights of women and girls? What are the challenges?
As a future leader, what changes would you make to laws and social attitudes to create safer online spaces?
Who can apply
Girls aged 15 to 18 who live in Tunisia can apply.
Applications close at 11:59pm on Friday 25 September 2026.
