Adviser Finder and Adviser Register temporarily unavailable
We apologise for any inconvenience and are working to restore access as quickly as possible.
The Immigration Advice Authority's Adviser Finder and Adviser Register are currently offline, while we carry out essential checks and assurance activities.
We expect them to be available again early next week.
If you are looking for an immigration adviser, please wait until the Adviser Finder is back online before choosing one if possible. If you need to check whether an adviser is registered, submit an enquiry to us or wait until the Adviser Register is back online.
Please be careful about using an adviser whose registration you have not been able to check.
We are working with our technical supplier to complete the system checks and bring both tools back as soon as possible. We will publish an update on Monday 9 November 2026.
