Change of His Majesty's Ambassador to Mali
Ms Catherine Mary Inglehearn OBE has been appointed His Majesty's Ambassador to the Republic of Mali in succession to Mr Angus Philip McKee OBE, who will be transferring to another Diplomatic Service appointment.
Ms Inglehearn will take up her appointment during February, 2027.
