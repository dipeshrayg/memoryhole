Change of His Majesty's Ambassador to Chad
Ms Fiona Blyth MBE has been appointed His Majesty's Ambassador to the Republic of Chad in succession to Ms Helena Owen, who will be leaving the Diplomatic Service. Ms Blyth will take up her appointment during December 2026.
