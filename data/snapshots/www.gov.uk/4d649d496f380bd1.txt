Local government reorganisation: committee system letter to North Warwickshire leader
Letter to leader of North Warwickshire Borough Council about changing to a cabinet system model as part of the local government reorganisation process.
Applies to England
Documents
Details
On 9 November 2026, the Minister for Local Government, Devolution and Regional Growth issued a letter to North Warwickshire Borough Council council regarding the transition of that council from the existing Committee system to Cabinet model as part of the local government reorganisation process.
