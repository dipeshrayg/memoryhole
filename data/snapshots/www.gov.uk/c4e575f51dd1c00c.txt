Confirmed 2026/27 EPR for packaging fees now available
PackUK has published confirmed EPR for packaging waste disposal fees for 2026/27
PackUK has today, 25 November 2026, published confirmed Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) for packaging waste disposal fees for 2026/27.
For the first time, producers will pay modulated (Green, Amber, or Red) fees, based on the recyclability of the packaging they place on the market, in line with the Recyclability Assessment Methodology (RAM).
The modulation approach aims to incentivise sustainable packaging design by linking fees to recyclability ratings:
- Green-rated packaging (widely recyclable) will attract lower fees
- Red-rated packaging (difficult to recycle) will incur higher fees
- Amber fees represent the baseline for materials without modulation
Producers can clearly see how much they can save by using more recyclable packaging, with modulation rewarding circular design choices, packaging innovation and improving environmental outcomes.
This transparency aims to support informed investment decisions and encourage producers to move away from difficult to recycle materials over time.
The EPR for packaging scheme forms the cornerstone of the UK's packaging reforms. Over £2 billion has already been provided to local authorities in the UK through the scheme to improve household waste collection and recycling services, directly benefiting local areas.
As the scheme matures, PackUK expects increasingly high-quality, well-evidenced data from producers. To further support producers with planning, a defined resubmission deadline has been introduced to provide a clear point at which data is used to set fees. This will provide greater fee stability and certainty, helping businesses to plan with confidence.
Producers who are liable for EPR for packaging fees will be notified by PackUK Customer Service team when their Notice of Liability is available.
