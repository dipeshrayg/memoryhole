Role
The Pharmacovigilance Inspector ensures that Marketing Authorisation Holders (MAHs) comply with their legal obligations for pharmacovigilance in accordance with Veterinary Medicines Regulations 2013 (as amended) (VMR) and EU 2019/6.
This role is critical to safeguarding animal and public health, and the environment through rigorous inspection, compliance oversight, and stakeholder engagement.
The role leads a discrete, high-impact operational area and operates within a complex scientific and regulatory framework. This requires expert judgement, leadership of inspection activities, and collaboration across multidisciplinary teams made up of internal VMD teams, external stakeholders and other regulators.
