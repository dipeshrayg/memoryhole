Daniel E. Pinto appointed to the Bank of England's Financial Policy Committee
Daniel E. Pinto has been appointed as an external member of the Financial Policy Committee.
The Chancellor of the Exchequer has appointed Daniel E. Pinto as an external member of the Financial Policy Committee (FPC).
Mr Pinto has over 40 years of experience in financial services, including in senior leadership roles across global financial markets, most recently as Vice Chairman at JPMorgan Chase. He will serve a 3-year term, which will begin on 4 January 2027. His appointment fills the position held by Jonathan Hall, who will step down from the Committee on 31 December 2026.
Economic Secretary to the Treasury, Lucy Rigby, said:
The Financial Policy Committee plays a vital role in protecting and enhancing the stability of the UK's financial system. Daniel Pinto's significant experience across global financial markets will be valuable for the Committee's work. I would also like to thank Jon Hall for his contribution to the Committee over the past six years.
Andrew Bailey, Governor of the Bank of England, said:
I am delighted that Daniel will be joining the Financial Policy Committee. He brings an exceptional breadth of international experience and a perspective that will add greatly to the Committee's discussions. My thanks also go to Jon for his dedicated service over the years.
Daniel Pinto said:
I am honoured to join the Financial Policy Committee and look forward to working with the Governor and my fellow members. The resilience and stability of the financial system are critical to a strong economy, and I look forward to contributing to the Committee's important work.
About Daniel E. Pinto
Mr Pinto has more than 40 years' experience in financial services, most recently as Vice Chairman of JPMorgan Chase. He previously served as the firm's President and Chief Operating Officer and as CEO of its Corporate & Investment Bank, having held a range of senior leadership roles across Europe, the Middle East, Africa and emerging markets. He has also served on a number of international financial services and corporate boards.
About the Appointments Process
The appointment follows a fair and open competition in line with the Governance Code on Public Appointments, and is subject to pre-commencement scrutiny by the Treasury Select Committee on 15 December 2026.
Mr Pinto has confirmed that he has not engaged in any significant party political activity in the last five years. A panel interviewed a number of candidates and made recommendations to the Chancellor, which informed his decision.
About the Financial Policy Committee
- The FPC is the UK's macroprudential regulator: its objective is to protect and enhance the stability of the UK's financial system by identifying, monitoring and addressing systemic risks.
- The FPC has thirteen members. Six of them are Bank of England staff: the Governor, four Deputy Governors and the Executive Director for Financial Stability Strategy and Risk. *There are also five external members who are selected from outside the Bank for their experience and expertise in financial services.
- The Committee also includes the Chief Executive of the Financial Conduct Authority and a non-voting member from HM Treasury.
