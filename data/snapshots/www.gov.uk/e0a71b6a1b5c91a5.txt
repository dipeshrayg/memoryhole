Improvement notice issued to London Borough of Bexley: May 2024
An improvement notice issued to the London Borough of Bexley following an Ofsted and CQC inspection in February 2024.
Applies to England
Documents
Details
The improvement notice for the London Borough of Bexley was lifted following a report by Ofsted published on 11 May 2026.
This improvement notice requires the London Borough of Bexley to take steps to improve its special educational needs and disabilities (SEND) services.
It follows an Ofsted and Care Quality Commission (CQC) area SEND inspection that judged there were 3 areas for priority action and 2 areas for improvement.
