Appointment of the Public Sector Fraud Authority Independent Reviewer
The Government has announced Richard Las as the inaugural Public Sector Fraud Authority Independent Reviewer.
The Government has announced Richard Las as the Public Sector Fraud Authority Independent Reviewer.
The Independent Reviewer of Public Sector Authorities Fraud Investigation and Enforcement Services (PAFIES) is a new Ministerial appointment established under the Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025 (PAFER).
As the Independent Reviewer, Mr Las' responsibilities will include reviewing enforcement, investigative, and recovery functions carried out by PAFIES to ensure legal compliance, effectiveness, and proportional exercise of PAFER powers.
PAFER gives powers to investigate, penalise, and recover money from fraudsters who take money from our public services, including those who exploited the Covid pandemic. The government created the Independent Reviewer to make sure these powers are being used safely.
Mark Cheeseman, Chief Executive of the Public Sector Fraud Authority, said:
I am delighted to welcome Richard Las CBE as the inaugural Independent Reviewer overseeing the enforcement, investigation, and recovery functions of the Public Sector Fraud Authority. His extensive experience leading complex fraud investigations at HMRC will be invaluable as we embed the new enforcement and recovery powers under the Public Authorities (Fraud, Error and Recovery) Act 2025. Independent oversight of these powers is essential to maintaining public confidence, ensuring they are used proportionately, effectively, and to the highest professional standards.
Richard Las CBE, Independent Reviewer of the Public Sector Fraud Authority, said:
I am privileged to be appointed as the first Independent Reviewer overseeing the enforcement and investigation functions of the Public Sector Fraud Authority. Rigorous, independent scrutiny is vital to ensuring that the new investigative and recovery powers are exercised lawfully, fairly, and proportionately as the Public Authorities Fraud Investigation and Enforcement Service becomes established. I look forward to working constructively with the Authority, wider government, and Parliament to uphold public trust and protect taxpayers' money.
Richard Las' biography
Richard Las CBE is a public servant with over three decades of experience leading counter-fraud strategy, investigations, and enforcement across government. Until 2026, he served as Director and Chief Investigation Officer for HMRC's Fraud Investigation Service, having previously held senior operational roles, including Head of Operations as well as leading the Parliamentary Committees Team on Select Committee briefings and negotiations.
