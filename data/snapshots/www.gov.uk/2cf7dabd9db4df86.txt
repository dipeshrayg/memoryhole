Publication of the DVSA business plan 2026 to 2027
Announcing the publication of the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) 2026 to 2027 business plan.
I am pleased to announce the publication of the 2026 to 2027 business plan for the Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA).
DVSA's business plan sets out the key:
- business priorities the agency will deliver, including any significant changes it plans to make to its services
- performance indicators by which its performance will be assessed
This plan allows service users and members of the public to understand DVSA's plans for delivering its core services and managing its finances.
The business plan will be available electronically on GOV.UK and a copy will be placed in the libraries of both Houses.
