Sededorm 1 mg/ml Solution for Injection for Dogs and Cats - product defect recall alert
Recall alert for Sededorm 1 mg/ml Solution for Injection for Dogs and Cats by Vetpharma Animal Health, S.L .
We wish to inform wholesalers and veterinarians that Vetpharma Animal Health S.L. has initiated a Class III recall with immediate effect for:
The reason for the recall is due to mislabelling issues in the product packaging.
Please note that this recall only affects products in this batch which are mislabelled in the Spanish language.
Vetpharma Animal Health S.L. is contacting wholesalers to examine inventory immediately and quarantine products subject to this recall.
