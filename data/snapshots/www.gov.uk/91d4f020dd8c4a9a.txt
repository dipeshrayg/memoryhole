Louise Cantillon joins the British High Commission following a distinguished career across UK Government. Most recently, she served as British Consul General Sydney and Deputy Trade Commissioner Asia Pacific (Australia & New Zealand).
The High Commissioner will focus on supporting greater trade and investment between the UK and New Zealand, building on the opportunities created by the Free Trade Agreement. She will also work with partners across government, business and civil society to promote economic growth, strengthen collective security, and address global challenges affecting the Indo-Pacific region and beyond.
She said:
I am delighted to be taking up my role as UK High Commissioner to Aotearoa New Zealand. The UK and New Zealand share a close and enduring partnership, and I look forward to meeting people across the country and further strengthening the ties between our nations.
The UK and New Zealand are natural partners with shared values, strong economic links and deep connections between our peoples. I look forward to working closely with communities, government, and businesses across New Zealand to unlock new opportunities for growth and innovation, while strengthening our cultural connections and shared prosperity.
At a time of increasing global uncertainty, our commitment to a strong rules-based international system has never been more important. Together, the UK and New Zealand can continue to champion our free trade agreement, support regional and global security, and work with partners to address the challenges facing our world.
The appointed High Commissioner succeeds Ms Iona Thomas OBE who completed her tenure earlier this year.
- the UK-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement (FTA) was signed on 28 February 2022 and entered into force on 31 May 2023. It is a landmark agreement that supports economic growth, strengthens trade and investment links, and delivers tariff-free access
