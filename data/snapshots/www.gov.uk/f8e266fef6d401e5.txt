Slough Borough Council: Explanatory Memorandum (updated 17 August 2026)
The Explanatory Memorandum to the Directions made under section 15(5) and (6) of the Local Government Act 1999 in respect to Slough Borough Council. It summarises the circumstances in which the Secretary of State has appointed Dr Dave Smith as Managing Director Commissioner.
This Memorandum is intended as a companion document to the Directions issued on 20 November 2024.
