Supporting people in West Cumbria through horticultural activity: case study
The NDA has contributed £195,000 of a £505,000 total to transform a derelict nursery into a vibrant working site at Growing Well in Egremont, West Cumbria.
The project
Growing Well, in Egremont, Cumbria, is a mental health charity and horticulture enterprise with a proven track record of supporting people with moderate to severe mental health issues to function and recover within the community.
Our investment
The NDA has contributed £195,000 of a £505,000 total to transform a derelict nursery into a vibrant working site.
The outcomes
Growing Well is now able to offer support for people in West Cumbria through therapeutic interventions, championing mental and physical health through horticultural activity.
In figures
- Creation of 6 new full-time jobs in Egremont.
- Supporting between 50-100 beneficiaries at the site each week.
- Attracting 20-40 volunteers each week.
- Offering a minimum of 100 boxes of fresh produce per week to the local community.
One user said:
I have had a really difficult time but coming to Growing Well helps me to cope. I'm learning about punctuality and social skills, routine and increased confidence. It means I have something to look forward to each week. Growing Well has made an enormous difference.
