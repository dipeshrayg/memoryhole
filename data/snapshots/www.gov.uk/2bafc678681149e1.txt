- 60,000 rural homes and businesses across Lincolnshire and Yorkshire can now access faster, more reliable broadband through the UK Government's Project Gigabit.
- Villages surrounding Barnsley, Huddersfield, Hull, Bradford, York, Boston and Wisbech among the latest to benefit.
- Project Gigabit is delivering digital opportunities to Yorkshire and Lincolnshire, helping rural communities grow businesses, learn new skills and get on through better connectivity.
Thousands of people across Yorkshire and Lincolnshire can now stream, work, study and stay connected more easily, as over 60,000 homes and businesses benefit from faster broadband backed by the UK Government's Project Gigabit.
It means more families in rural communities can make video calls without dropouts, more students can learn online, and more local firms can take payments, reach customers, and run their businesses without being held back by poor connectivity.
Winterton in North Lincolnshire, Fosdyke in Lincolnshire and Sessay and Linton on Ouse in Yorkshire are among the latest communities to benefit, with more villages, hamlets and rural homes being brought onto the network every week.
The rollout is also creating opportunities beyond faster broadband. Quickline has supported more than 1,700 young people to learn more about skills and careers in Science, Technology, Engineering and Maths (STEM). The provider ran a series of STEM workshops in East Yorkshire for 80 Year 5 and year 6 pupils from Skirlaugh Primary School. The students were immersed in a range of activities designed to bring engineering and telecommunications to life.
More than 130 people from Yorkshire and Lincolnshire have also gained hands-on experience through Quickline's apprenticeship programme - all as a direct result of the £127 million the firm has received through Project Gigabit.
Minister of State at the Department for Digital, Media, Culture and Sport, Ian Murray said:
Good broadband is about giving families, workers and local businesses the tools they need to get on, wherever they live.
This milestone is proof that Project Gigabit has put better digital opportunities into local communities across Yorkshire and Lincolnshire that have had to work around patchy connections for too long - whether that's running a business from a rural village or taking a job that would once have felt out of reach.

Project Gigabit is bringing lightning-fast broadband to hard-to-reach communities across the UK. The programme has already helped upgrade more than 1.3 million premises across the UK through government support, largely in rural areas where gigabit coverage is lower.

Project Gigabit
Project Gigabit is the UK government's rollout of lightning-fast, reliable broadband across the UK. The programme targets homes and businesses that are not included in broadband companies' commercial plans, reaching parts of the country that would otherwise miss out.
Project Gigabit is crucial to the government's mission to kickstart economic growth. It will create and support thousands of high-paid, high-skilled jobs. It is fueling our innovators and wealth creators, and enabling anyone to start up and run a business of any size from anywhere in the UK.
It will also accelerate the government's work to break down barriers to opportunity across the UK. Our rollout will enable people to shop, bank and access public services without endless loading screens and enjoy cutting-edge tech from virtual reality to ultra-HD streaming - regardless of where they live or work.
