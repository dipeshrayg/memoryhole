DCMS letters to Sky and ITV plc regarding decision to refer to Phase 1
Correspondence relating to the anticipated acquisition of ITV M&E Holdings Limited by Sky UK Limited.
Documents
Details
DCMS letters to the solicitors acting on behalf ofSky UK Limited Sky and ITV plc, informing them that the Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport has decided to refer for Phase 1 investigation the anticipated acquisition of ITV M&E Holdings Limited by Sky UK Limited.
