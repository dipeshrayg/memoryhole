UKHSA cold-health alerts winter 2026/27
Updates on latest cold health alerts.
Latest update
UKHSA has issued a yellow cold-health alert (CHA) for the North West of England, North East of England and Yorkshire and the Humber regions, running from 9pm tomorrow, Sunday 15 November until 9am on Thursday 19 November. Other regions of England are currently unaffected.
Under the Weather Health Alerting system introduced by UKHSA and the Met Office, yellow cold-health alerts may be issued during periods of heat or cold which would be unlikely to impact most people but could impact those who are particularly vulnerable.
Low temperatures can result in increased use of health care services by vulnerable people and an increased risk to health for individuals aged 65 years and over, those with pre-existing health conditions (including respiratory and cardiovascular diseases), and some other vulnerable groups, such as those sleeping rough. UKHSA has published guidance for professionals protecting vulnerable people and for the public on staying safe during periods of cold weather.
Paul Coleman, Consultant in Public Health at UKHSA, said:
This is the latest cold-health alert of the season, and we can expect more as we move further into winter. Cold weather can have a serious impact on the health of some people, including those aged 65 and over and those with pre-existing health conditions, so it is vital to check in on friends, family and neighbours who are most vulnerable.
We're also reminding people to take simple steps to stay warm and look after their health. If you can, try to heat the rooms you spend most of your time in, such as your living room or bedroom, and dress warmly to help you stay safe and well during colder weather.
