New Chairman appointed to the Disclosure and Barring Service
Julian Blazeby will take up the role on 1 December 2026
Julian Blazeby has been appointed as the new Chairman of the Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS).
He joined the DBS Board in April 2023 and has been Vice Chairman since April.
Julian served as a police officer for 30 years in 3 different forces. He retired as an Assistant Chief Constable in Staffordshire in 2014 before going on to hold the role of Deputy Chief of Police in Jersey between 2017 to 2018.
He has also held senior civil service roles in the Ministry of Defence and the Independent Police Complaints Commission and was a Director General in the Government of Jersey where he led the Justice and Home Affairs Department. Julian is also the Independent Chair of the Criminal Injuries Compensation Authority.
Julian succeeds Dr Gillian Fairfield, whose 8-year tenure as Chairman ends on 30 November 2026. His appointment follows a robust competition in accordance with the requirements of the Governance Code of Public Appointments. He will begin his tenure on 1 December 2026.
Julian Blazeby said:
I am honoured to be appointed as Chairman of the Disclosure and Barring Service. DBS plays a vital role in helping employers make safer recruitment decisions, and I look forward to continuing to work with the Board, the executive team and colleagues across DBS to build on the strong foundations Dr Gillian Fairfield has laid.
