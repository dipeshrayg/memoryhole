Change of His Majesty's Ambassador to Seoul
Dr Rachel Emma-Jane Fentem CMG has been appointed His Majesty's Ambassador to the Republic of Korea in succession to Mr Colin James Crooks LVO, who will be leaving the Diplomatic Service.
Dr Rachel Emma-Jane Fentem CMG will take up her appointment during March, 2027.
