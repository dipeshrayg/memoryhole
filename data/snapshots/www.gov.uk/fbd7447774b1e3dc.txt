Scott Smith appointed to the UK Sport Board
The Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport has appointed Scott Smith as Board Member to UK Sport for a term of four years, commencing on 1 November 2026 to 31 October 2030.
Scott Smith
Scott Smith is Chief Financial Officer of Six Nations Rugby, having previously held CFO roles at the England and Wales Cricket Board and the America's Cup. He has experience in financial leadership, commercial strategy, media rights, major events and corporate governance.
He also holds non-executive and advisory positions with Racecourse Media Group, Archery GB and the EURO 2028 Oversight Group. Scott is a Fellow Chartered Accountant in both the UK and Australia.
Remuneration and Governance Code
UK Sport Board Members are remunerated £218 per day, with a time commitment of 24 days per year.
This appointment has been made in accordance with the Governance Code on Public Appointments. The appointments process is regulated by the Commissioner for Public Appointments.
Under the Code, any significant political activity undertaken by an appointee in the last five years must be declared. This is defined as including holding office, public speaking, making a recordable donation, or candidature for election. Scott Smith has not declared any such political activity.
