England have called up uncapped Durham opener Ben McKinney for their Test tour of South Africa as cover for injured batter Jacob Bethell.
The 22-year-old, a tall and attacking left-hander, has long been highly-rated by England and has now been rewarded with his first international call-up.
It leaves McKinney's Durham team-mate Emilio Gay, Ben Duckett and Jordan Cox set to continue as England's top three after relative success through the summer.
Bethell had been expected to rival Gay or Cox for a place in the top three before it was revealed he requires further surgery on a knee issue.
Who is Ben McKinney?
McKinney first caught the eye when scoring his maiden Durham century aged 19 in 2024 and that winter further displayed his talents by hitting 110 from 110 balls for England Lions against a strong Australia A side.
He has been name-checked by England's director of men's cricket Rob Key since and has been in a regular in England Lions squads.
Last summer, he scored 1,044 runs at an average of 52.20 in Division Two of the County Championship, where England's selector Marcus North also had first-hand experience of the batter in his previous role as Durham's director of cricket.
McKinney scored 145 from just 65 balls in a 50-over match for England Lions against West Indies A in St Kitts earlier this month and was already set to tour South Africa as part of the Lions squad.
Though sometimes loose outside off stump, his domestic centuries this season included 244 against Gloucestershire, 140 against Derbyshire and 184 against Middlesex.
At Durham, McKinney opens the batting with Gay at number three - an option for England if injury or loss of form means a change is required.
Their series against South Africa begins with the first Test in Johannesburg from 17 December.
