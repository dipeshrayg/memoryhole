The past four seasons - a pattern of inconsistency
For a club to have spent as much as Chelsea, it is no surprise fans might be frustrated with the returns - or lack of them.
The 12th-placed Premier League finish in 2022-23 - the first season since the takeover by the BlueCo consortium - was clearly a big disappointment.
Improvements followed, with sixth and fourth in the next two campaigns signalling strides in the right direction, but finishing 10th last season was another big blow.
Since 2022, Chelsea have won two trophies: the Conference League and the Club World Cup.
But for owners with such big ambitions, they will surely feel like their expectations haven't yet been met.
The Alonso era - back to ready-to-go signings?
If his previous managerial approach is anything to go by, Xabi Alonso favours a blend of youth and experience.
The Spaniard signed four players over the age of 27 for the 2023-24 season at Bayer Leverkusen. They included Granit Xhaka and Alejandro Grimaldo - two key players in their Bundesliga title-winning side.
Another four players over the age of 27 came the following season.
It could be a similar story now at Chelsea.
