The Duchess of Sussex has said she was informed about "disturbing allegations" about her estranged father in 2018.
In a statement, Meghan said a letter containing allegations against Thomas Markle was sent to the Metropolitan Police that year and subsequently shared with her.
Her comments come after reports that images of child sexual abuse allegedly belonging to her father had been found at his former home in the Philippines.
Thomas Markle, who is 82 and based in the US, denies possessing any such images.
Commenting after the allegations, first reported by The Sun newspaper, external, the duchess said: "I am deeply distressed at the news that has come to light in the last 24 hours, and my thoughts are with any children who may have been harmed."
She said that when she received the letter outlining allegations against her father, she was "given assurance that these were being investigated".
She added she hoped it could be understood why she had kept her distance from her father and did "not allow him to have contact with our children".
This was "to protect them", Meghan said, adding that she had "trusted that the authorities were doing the same for other families".
She described the allegations as "more sickening than one could ever imagine" and said her "sincerest hope is that justice will be fully served and that the priority remains the safety and protection of innocent children".
Meghan's half-brother, Thomas Markle Jr, alleged to The Sun that he had found child abuse images at his father's home in the Philippines and reported it to the police.
The Undersecretary for the Philippines Department of Justice, Nicholas Ty, told the BBC that "the matter is currently being investigated" by an anti-human trafficking and child sexual exploitation task force in Cebu.
Philippines Interior Minister Jonvic Remulla separately told the BBC that national police had secured Thomas Markle Snr's computer to conduct a comprehensive digital forensic examination.
Investigators are currently analysing the data to determine the full scope and extent of his alleged criminal activities, with the minister saying it would take one week to get the forensic results.
It is understood the Met has not and is not currently investigating Meghan's father. The force said it was carrying out an internal inquiry to confirm the Met's royal protection unit passed on the letter to the Duchess.
The FBI said it was "aware of the allegations and will provide support, as appropriate, to the Philippine authorities. We will also investigate any related allegations of federal violations of US law."
The BBC has contacted Thomas Markle Snr and the Metropolitan Police for comment.
The allegations about Meghan's estranged father cast a new light on their complicated family relationships.
The statement from the Duchess of Sussex says the sharing of information about her father happened in 2018 - the year in which she married Prince Harry.
It is understood that Meghan first learnt about the allegations against her father after the wedding.
Sources close to the Sussexes said Meghan could not speak publicly about the allegations when she first learned of them, in order to avoid jeopardising any future investigation.
Speaking out could have resulted in legal action against her, the sources said.
The claims add a fresh dimension to the questions about why Prince Harry and their children did not ever seem to have met Meghan's father - and why relations seemed to be so strained.
With such question marks over her estranged father - and he has denied any wrongdoing - it also raises the issue of whether that played a part in the Sussexes' hurried return to the UK in late summer.
However the Sussexes' spokesman has denied it was a factor in their decision to leave the US, where her father was living.
The pair and their two children returned to the UK in August after six years in the US.
They moved there after stepping back as senior royals in January 2020, meaning they no longer perform official royal duties or receive public funds.
Meghan is the only child of Thomas Markle's marriage to her mother, Doria Ragland, but he has two other children - Samantha and Thomas Markle Jr - from an earlier marriage.
The relationship between Meghan and her father, a former TV lighting director, broke down in the run-up to her wedding in 2018.
At the time, Thomas Markle was found to have colluded with photographers, accepting payment to stage photographs of him preparing for the wedding.
The resultant fall-out saw him pull out of his daughter's wedding, citing health problems. Meghan was instead walked down the aisle by King Charles III - then Prince of Wales.
In 2025, Thomas Markle moved to the Philippines after a series of strokes.
He is reportedly now living in a rehabilitation centre in Florida, where he is learning to walk again, after having a leg amputated during surgery in December 2025.
Meghan sent a letter to her estranged father while he was in hospital in the Philippines after the procedure.
