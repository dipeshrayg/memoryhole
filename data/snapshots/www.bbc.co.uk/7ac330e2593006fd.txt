US rapper Don Toliver has postponed his concert at the O2 arena in London on Monday, a day after his show in Manchester was evacuated following an "unknown substance" being released into the crowd.
"I'm very sad to sit here and say I have to reschedule The O2 London show to December 15th due to (the) unfortunate incident last night Manchester," he wrote on Instagram.
One person was taken to hospital and a total of 89 people "displayed symptoms believed to be linked to the exposure" after the incident during Toliver's show at the Co-op Live arena on Sunday night, police said.
Fans described "distressing scenes" with people "choking", "screaming" and "coughing up blood" because of the effects of an unknown substance, which police initially believed to be tear gas.
Some fans reported eye and respiratory symptoms, according to the North West Ambulance Service.
Greater Manchester Police (GMP) declared a major incident and said 12,000 people were evacuated from the arena.
One woman was taken to Manchester Royal Infirmary for further assessment and treatment after the incident, which happened at about 22:00 GMT, the force said.
A further 88 people "underwent dry decontamination at the scene and were subsequently accommodated within the Etihad Stadium while the incident was managed", according to GMP.
Police launched an investigation and are continuing to carry out tests on items at the venue to try to identify the substance. No arrests have been made.
Assistant Chief Constable Tom Harding said: "Enquiries remain ongoing to establish the nature of the substance, where it came from and any associated risk. Testing will take place throughout the day as the investigation into the cause of this incident progresses.
"We are trying to establish whether any criminality has occurred in relation to this incident."
Some fans complained about security and a lack of searches going in to the Manchester concert.
Co-op Live said on its website there were a range of security measures, and while "some of these are visible, others you won't be able to see".
All fans at the Manchester show will get a full refund, promoter Live Nation said.
Postponing the O2 arena show, Live Nation blamed "production delays following last night's show at Co-op Live in Manchester".
A statement said: "We are sorry to everyone affected by last night's events in Manchester and full refunds for that show will be automatically processed to ticket holders."
There have been apparently similar incidents earlier on Toliver's tour, when some fans reported that spray or gas were released in the crowd in Zurich and Paris.
In his post on Monday, the rapper said: "I just wanna make sure everyone is safe and the next show goes on properly. My heart and prayers are with everyone that was injured last night."
He added: "The only thing I love more than creating music is performing for my fans," Toliver said, asking people to stop bringing substances into his shows.
"It ain't cool," added the rapper, who was nominated for five Grammy Awards earlier this month. "See you soon London, love you Guys. DT."
Toliver's Nitrous tour, named after his new album, is also due to visit Wembley Arena in London on Tuesday. No update has been given about that show.
It was set to be the last gig of his world tour before he took a break from the road ahead of a Coachella appearance next spring.
Toliver is best known for singles such as No Idea and After Party, from his debut studio album, Heaven or Hell (2020), which was big on TikTok. He also featured on Travis Scott's song Can't Say, from his third studio album Astroworld.
