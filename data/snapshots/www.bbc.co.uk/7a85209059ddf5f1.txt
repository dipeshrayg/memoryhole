Pub company Marston's has said an "excellent World Cup" helped drive stronger trading as annual profits rose by nearly a fifth, despite reporting a dip in sales.
The Wolverhampton-based firm, which operates more than 1,300 pubs across the UK, reported underlying pre-tax profits of Â£85.4m for the year to 26 September, up 18.4% from a year earlier.
The company said trading was improved by new-look pubs, strong trade during this summer's football World Cup, and a partnership with Jeremy Clarkson's Hawkstone drinks brand.
"For the third year running, profit is significantly up, cash flow is strong and margins have improved," said chief executive Justin Platt.
The pub chain also said bookings for Christmas events were 9% ahead of the same point last year.
Marston's has invested heavily in refurbishing pubs into new formats, including its sports-focused "Grandstand" venues.
It plans at least 100 more conversions in the new financial year, in a bid to accelerate sales growth further.

Marston's said it had selected the venue for investment because of its involvement with local sports teams, community groups, and charities.
"We've got two football teams, four darts teams and a dominoes team," explained Sullivan, all of which had also helped bolster sales.
But she recognised not all businesses were thriving.
"It's been a difficult year for a lot of pubs and we have empathised as they've lost a lot of trade," she said.
But investment by the company had brought the pub "back to life", she continued.
"We've managed to keep as much custom as we possibly can and seen new custom."
Marston's also pointed to events and promotions, including darts champion Luke Humphries' darts nights and the partnership with Clarkson.
"Guest satisfaction levels have improved again, thanks to the amazing work of our pub teams, whose passion and commitment keep our pubs at the heart of every local community they serve," added Platt.
"Our new pub formats are performing especially well and are now a proven growth engine for the business, giving us real confidence to accelerate our investment plans in 2027."
