BBC apologises after noise disruption during Strictly
A recording of sex noises appeared to be playing from the audience during the filming of Strictly Come Dancing in Blackpool on Saturday.
Judge Motsi Mabuse was giving feedback to Delta Goodrem when she paused after the sound started playing.
At the end of the programme, presenter Emma Willis apologised to viewers "if you heard any audio disruptions".
A BBC spokesperson also issued an apology and said it was "investigating how this happened".
