Four men have been found guilty of sexually abusing a woman whose husband drugged and secretly advertised her for sex on the internet.
Philip Young, 50, already pleaded guilty to multiple charges of drugging and raping his wife, Joanne Young, over a 13-year period at their home in Swindon, Wiltshire.
Norman Macksoni, 48, Richard Wilkins, 62, Connor Sanderson-Doyle, 32, and Mohammed Hassan, 38, were found guilty of multiple sexual offences by a jury at Winchester Crown Court. Wilkins and Macksoni were both each found guilty of one count of rape.
Joanne Young, who has waived her right to anonymity, beamed at police as she left court wiping her eyes.
Wilkins was also found guilty of one count of assault by penetration.
Connor Sanderson-Doyle was found guilty of one count of sexual assault by touching and one count of sexual assault by penetration.
Hassan was found guilty of one count of sexual assault by touching.
A sixth defendant, Dean Hamilton, 47, of Street, in Somerset, admitted one charge of assault by penetration, two of sexual assault and one of rape at an earlier court hearing in February.
Judge Mr Justice Saini said Joanne Young had shown "amazing bravery through these proceedings".
Police could also be seen sobbing as they left the court.
