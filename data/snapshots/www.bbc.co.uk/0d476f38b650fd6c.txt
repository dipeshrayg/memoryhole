Could the Huntingdon train attack have been prevented?
Anthony Williams - who stabbed passengers on a train between Peterborough and London in 2025 - has been found guilty of all 14 counts of attempted murder.
Williams, 33, stabbed 10 people on an LNER train service on 1 November last year.
He denied all 14 counts of attempted murder, including alleged attacks away from the train, in Peterborough and London.
The BBC's Daniel Sandford looks at whether there were possible missed opportunities to prevent the attacks.
