Jacob Bethell will miss England's Test tour of South Africa because he requires more surgery on the knee injury that has kept him out since July.
Batter Bethell was due to make his return in the ongoing white-ball tour of Australia, but had to leave that trip at the weekend after suffering what England described as a "setback" in training.
The 23-year-old has met with a specialist and, as first reported by the Telegraph, will have a second operation on his right knee.
He has also been released by Indian Premier League side Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB).
With no timeframe placed on Bethell's recovery, RCB director of cricket Mo Bobat said the decision to part ways was influenced by doubts over the England batter's fitness for the 2027 IPL season, beginning in March.
The likely long-term nature of Bethell's recovery means he is a major doubt for England's winter beyond the South Africa tour - two Tests in Bangladesh in February and the one-off 150th anniversary day-night Test against Australia in Melbourne in March.
It could mean that he now does not play again until the summer, in the build-up to a home Ashes series.
England are yet to confirm Bethell's replacement for the three Tests against South Africa. They will choose from the England Lions, who arrive in South Africa before the Test squad.
When the Warwickshire man missed the three Tests against Pakistan during the home summer, his place in the England squad went to Ollie Pope.
Bethell has not played since the third one-day international against India at Lord's in July, when he suffered discomfort in his knee.
In his absence from the Test side during the Pakistan series, Emilio Gay opened and Jordan Cox took Bethell's slot at number three.
It was expected that one of Gay or Cox would have to drop out of the starting XI in South Africa to make way for Bethell's return.
When England announced their touring squad last week, selector Marcus North said that no decisions had been made on the make-up of the first-choice top six.
Now, in Bethell's continued absence, Gay and Cox will have further opportunities to make claims for a long-term place in the Test side.
Surrey's Pope has not played for England since the third Ashes Test against Australia in Adelaide in December of last year.
He was due to be with the Lions in South Africa on a tour that overlaps with the Test series and appears the most likely candidate to replace Bethell.
If England opt against Pope, Somerset's James Rew and uncapped Durham opener Ben McKinney have both been in strong form on the Lions' recent white-ball tour of the West Indies.
This latest setback is a significant blow to Bethell, who looked to have cemented his place in the Test side with a sparkling century in the final Ashes Test in Sydney in January.
But the knee injury cut short his summer, meaning he missed The Hundred, the Pakistan series, white-ball matches against Sri Lanka, then the limited-overs tours of Pakistan and Australia.
England are already without Josh Tongue for the first Test against South Africa in Johannesburg, beginning on 17 December. The pace bowler will be at home for the birth of his second child.
North has previously confirmed that England will call on fast-bowling reserves from the Lions to cover for Tongue.
