Los Angeles Rams wide receiver Puka Nacua has been given a three-game suspension by the NFL for violating the league's personal conduct policy.
In March, the 25-year-old was sued for assault and battery by a woman who says he made an antisemitic statement and bit her on the shoulder on New Year's Eve.
That civil lawsuit is ongoing, with a trial date set for March 2028.
The NFL's personal conduct policy says a player being sued over such an incident is deemed a violation "even if the conduct does not result in a criminal conviction".
Nacua's attorney has denied his client made any antisemitic statement, and says the bite was "horseplay".
That incident came two weeks after Nacua performed an antisemitic gesture during a livestream appearance, for which he later apologised.
The NFL has now concluded its investigation and has suspended Nacua while he is already out with knee and toe injuries.
He was placed on injured reserve (IR) last week, meaning he will miss a minimum of four games. He has been suspended without pay so will forfeit $950,000 (Â£725,000).
According to ESPN, the NFL told Nacua "any further violation of the personal conduct policy... will subject him to additional and more substantial discipline, including banishment from the league".
Shortly before Nacua was sued in March he entered a holistic care facility, external to "improve his overall behavior in every aspect of his life" and completed a rehab programme before attending the start of the Rams' off-season programme in April.
Nacua has been one of the NFL's best wide receivers since being a fifth-round draft pick in 2023. He is in the final year of his rookie contract so is set to receive a lucrative new deal after this season.
