Hard-hitting back row Greg Fisilau will make his first England start in one of five changes to their XV to face New Zealand.
The Plymouth-born 23-year-old, whose father Keni was a Tonga international, has impressed off the bench in each of England's previous three wins this autumn.
He brings an industrious carrying option to a back row that relies heavily on Ben Earl in that aspect of the game. Ben Curry's breakdown skills have been sacrificed to make space, with the Sale man relegated to the bench.
Exeter's Fisilau has carried more than any other player in the Prem this season, as well as sitting fourth in the top-flight's tackle standings.
Elsewhere Freddie Steward's good form is rewarded with a start ahead of George Furbank at full-back, while Tommy Freeman returns on the wing for Saturday's rematch (16:40 GMT).
Freeman missed last weekend's 37-12 victory over the All Blacks with a minor thigh issue which forced his withdrawal in the hours before kick-off.
Ellis Genge comes into the starting front row after a string of barnstorming performances across the latter half of England's Nations Championship campaign.
He is joined in the front row by Luke Cowan-Dickie, who is preferred at hooker to Jamie George.
Northampton's 21-year-old scrum-half Archie McParland, who made his debut off the bench against Japan a fortnight ago, is among the replacements once again.
With less than a year until the Rugby World Cup, England head coach Steve Borthwick's auditions for fringe squad members are taking on more importance.
Victory would ensure an autumn clean sweep, a sixth win in succession and a third-place finish in the overall standings of the inaugural Nations Championship.
A win would also contribute to the northern hemisphere's points tally in a Ryder Cup-style competition which runs throughout the three days of action at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham.
The tournament is being held every other year between Rugby World Cups and British and Irish Lions tours in an attempt to bring more of a narrative to the Test calendar and draw in fans.
Six Nations holders France and world champions South Africa will contest Sunday's grand final (16:40 GMT).
England XV: Steward; Freeman, Slade, Atkinson, Joseph; F Smith, Van Poortvliet; Genge, Cowan-Dickie, Heyes, Itoje (c), Coles, Chessum, G Fisilau, Earl
Replacements: George, Baxter, Stuart, Martin, B Curry, Pollock, McParland, M Smith
