George Russell has said Max Verstappen can "say what he wants" and explained he found the Red Bull driver's criticisms of him after the Las Vegas Grand Prix "quite amusing".
Verstappen said Russell was "so far below my level" after winning the race on Saturday.
His remarks were a response to Mercedes driver Russell saying "somebody has to stand up to this guy" after an on-track confrontation in practice.
Russell said before this weekend's penultimate grand prix of the season in Qatar: "I stand by what I said after practice, but he was the one who went out and won the race and I didn't, and I'm sure he had a bit of a laugh about that and it was reasonably amusing.
"He drove an incredible race and won it and he can afford to say what he wants."
Asked whether he wanted to pursue reconciliation with Dutchman Verstappen, Russell said: "We're all competitors out there. There's no right or wrong way about the mentality everybody has.
"We are all different in many regards and you don't need to agree with one another. I'm not looking to be best mates with anybody on the grid. I am just working and looking and fighting to win. Simple as that."
British driver Russell said he had learned lessons from the incident in Vegas, which is the latest in a series of flashpoints between himself and four-time champion Verstappen dating back several years, and he might change how open he is with the media in future.
"It's all avoidable at the end of the day," Russell said. "It's all a bit unnecessary. It's not something I am looking to continue. I am not looking to have a poor relationship with people. That's not really the kind of person I am.
"There are lessons to be taken from these situations. Maybe I am just too open and honest with the media. I need to remember I'm talking to a camera that is broadcasting to millions of people. That's the biggest takeaway.
"It is not a war of words off track. I maybe have been guilty of being too open and honest when I speak with you guys.
"I feel like I have a good relationship with the media and I sometimes forget I am not talking to one person - I am talking to millions of people on the television.
"That good relationship has probably hindered me a bit and that is life lessons. I respect Max immensely for his driving talent and what he has achieved on track."
