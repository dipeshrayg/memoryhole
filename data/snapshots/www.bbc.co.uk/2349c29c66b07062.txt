Seeing his club win a place in the Football League remains the aim for White, but reaching the Premier League feels a far more distant dream.
"For me the gulf in football is between the Premier League and some Championship clubs, and the rest," White told BBC Sport.
"The Premier League is still the elite place to be, and it's like being part of a financial institution when you are there, but you need to spend your money wisely.
"One big theory I have, if you flip it around the other way, is you would not want to get relegated from the Premier League.
"There are clubs in there, like Fulham or Bournemouth and Brentford, where arguably there are much bigger clubs than them in the Championship.
"That next manager is always so important for clubs of that size because, if they get it wrong and go down, some of them may never come back.
"The Championship is a little bit divided too. We do a lot of business with Charlton Athletic, and their finances, compared to the majority of that division, are nothing. There's no comparison between them and West Ham, for example. They are miles apart.
"Beyond that, there is not such a gap, but you can definitely put a thick border between the Championship and League One.
"I think that's why you see the likes of Bromley and Stockport go on the same journey from non-league that we are trying to make, and go up from League Two too. But then they get to League One and it is like the next bit is the tough bit."
