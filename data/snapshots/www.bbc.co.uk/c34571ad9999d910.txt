Wycombe 3-8 Aston Villa
Round two, 20 September 2005, Adams Park
Aston Villa manager David O'Leary admitted his side were "on the verge" of being part of a cup shock when they faced Wycombe Wanderers in 2005.
But having trailed the League Two side 3-1 at half-time, Villa responded with seven goals in the second half to secure the win.
Some Villa fans reacted angrily when goals from Nathan Tyson, Roger Johnson and Tommy Mooney put Wycombe in a strong position at the halfway stage.
But goals from Milan Baros and James Milner made it 3-3, before a Craig Easton own goal, two from Gareth Barry and further strikes from Milner and Steven Davis completed an emphatic turnaround.
