Watch: Severe storms overturn vehicle and trigger flooding in Ohio
Video from the Ohio Department of Transportation captured the moment a powerful storm swept through the city of Columbus, flipping a vehicle. No injuries were reported.
The storm brought strong winds, heavy rain, flooding and widespread damage across parts of central Ohio, and at least one fatality has been reported.
Governor Mike DeWine has activated the Ohio National Guard and urged residents to prepare for additional severe storms and flooding in the coming days.
