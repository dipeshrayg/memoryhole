Watch: Meghan's first public appearance since father arrested
The Duchess of Sussex has made her first public appearance since her father, Thomas Markle, was charged with the possession of child sex abuse material.
Meghan arrived at the European Parliament in Brussels with her husband, Prince Harry, who delivered a speech about online child safety.
During his address, Harry said he and his wife stood alongside parents who had lost children to online harm
