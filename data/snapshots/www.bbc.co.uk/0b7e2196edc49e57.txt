'A lonely road': Catherine joins cancer support group in new documentary
The Princess of Wales is seen joining a cancer support group in newly-released footage from a forthcoming documentary called Heal.
"I haven't really explored this outside my family walls," Catherine tells a group of people from Shine Cancer Support.
The documentary features her personal thoughts on her illness and recovery.
Catherine has never disclosed what type of cancer she faced, but in March 2024 she revealed that she had a diagnosis of cancer and was receiving chemotherapy.
In January 2025 she said that she was in remission and in the following months began a gradual return to royal duties.
Heal will be shown on BBC iPlayer from 12:00 GMT and BBC One at 18:40 GMT on 29 November. It will also air internationally, including on NBCUniversal in the US on 30 November, Channel 9 in Australia on 1 December and CTV and Noovo in Canada on 7 December
